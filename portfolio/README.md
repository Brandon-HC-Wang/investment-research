# Portfolio Research Layer

This layer connects company and macro research to portfolio-level thinking. It does not alter company theses and does not provide trading instructions.

## Responsibilities

- [`watchlist.md`](watchlist.md) maps research subjects to portfolio-relevant monitoring roles.
- [`allocation.md`](allocation.md) defines evidence and constraint gates for allocation decisions.
- [`risk_management.md`](risk_management.md) defines exposure, correlation, liquidity, and thesis-risk controls.

The existing [`../watchlist/`](../watchlist/README.md) remains the canonical research-priority queue. Portfolio files may reference it but must not duplicate its workflow state.

## Operating principle

Portfolio conclusions begin with company-level sustainable earnings, then consider valuation, scenario asymmetry, correlation, liquidity, and aggregate exposure. A strong company thesis is necessary but not sufficient for allocation.
