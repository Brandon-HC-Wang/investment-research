# Allocation Framework

## Allocation prerequisites

No allocation view should be formed until the company has a causal earnings model, explicit downside, sustainable-earnings assessment, falsification tests, valuation range, liquidity review, and current source evidence. Low research confidence is itself an allocation constraint.

## Decision dimensions

Evaluate business quality, earnings visibility, valuation asymmetry, balance-sheet resilience, thesis independence, liquidity, governance, and correlation with existing exposures. Keep research conviction separate from position size.

## Exposure map

Before any allocation, aggregate exposure by industry, customer, geography, currency, interest rate, commodity, technology platform, policy regime, liquidity, and common macro thesis. Different tickers do not guarantee diversified earnings drivers.

## Scenario sizing

Use downside loss, probability of permanent impairment, time to thesis verification, liquidity under stress, and correlation with other downside cases. Do not size from upside potential alone or from a single valuation multiple.
