# Investment Thesis

This file synthesizes the current research view. It is not a buy, sell, or hold recommendation.

## Current thesis

State whether and how the company can compound sustainable per-share earnings over the next two to five years. Include business quality, competitive advantage, current growth driver, replacement driver, and the largest dependency.

## Variant perception

Describe a specific difference between the evidence-based view and a documented market or consensus assumption. If no reliable consensus evidence exists, state that the variant view is unknown.

## Evidence for and against

Present the strongest supporting and contradicting evidence. Link to canonical company records rather than duplicating detailed analysis.

## Scenario thesis

Summarize downside, base, and upside operating paths. The downside must include a causal EPS-reduction mechanism; the upside must identify evidence required for realization.

## Principal risks

Identify the one or two risks most likely to reduce EPS. Include magnitude or range, timing, early warning signals, and potential mitigation.

## Confirmation and falsification

List observable, time-bound conditions that would strengthen, weaken, or invalidate the thesis. Link each unresolved condition to an ID in `07_open_questions.md`.

## Capital allocation judgment

Assess whether management’s reinvestment, balance-sheet, acquisition, distribution, and issuance decisions are likely to increase sustainable per-share value.

## Quality scorecard

Apply `docs/Scoring.md` to business quality, competitive advantage, revenue drivers, EPS drivers, sustainable earnings, future visibility, and capital allocation. Include dated rationales and weighted score.

## Current confidence and next action

State low, medium, or high confidence, explain the binding uncertainty, and repeat the concrete next action from `meta.yaml`.

## Thesis history

Append dated thesis, confidence, and score changes. Preserve superseded views and explain why they changed.
