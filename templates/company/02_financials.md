# Financials

## Current financial profile

Summarize growth, margins, returns, cash conversion, balance-sheet capacity, dilution, and the principal difference between reported and sustainable earnings. State reporting currency and fiscal year-end.

## Historical income statement

Maintain at least five reported fiscal years when available. Include revenue by material segment, gross profit, operating profit, net income attributable to common shareholders, weighted diluted shares, reported diluted EPS, and normalized diluted EPS. State units and source definitions.

## Revenue and margin explanation

For each material period, explain revenue through volume, ASP, mix, share, acquisition, cycle, and FX. Explain gross and operating margin through utilization, mix, pricing, costs, inventory, and operating leverage.

## Earnings-quality bridge

Reconcile reported net income and EPS to normalized results. Classify each adjustment as recurring core, recurring cyclical, project-based, or non-recurring; include tax and per-share effects.

## Cash flow and reinvestment

Track operating cash flow, capital expenditure, free cash flow, working-capital drivers, capitalized costs, and cash conversion. Separate maintenance from growth investment when evidence permits.

## Balance sheet

Track cash, investments, debt, leases, pension or contingent obligations, inventory, receivables, contract assets, and liquidity. Explain off-balance-sheet or project funding where material.

## Capital allocation history

Record reinvestment, acquisitions, divestitures, dividends, repurchases, issuance, and debt decisions. Evaluate realized per-share outcomes against stated policy.

## Financial assumptions and revisions

Append dated revisions to definitions, normalizing adjustments, or historical interpretations. Never rewrite an earlier judgment without a revision record.

## Sources

List filings and other primary sources supporting the financial record.
