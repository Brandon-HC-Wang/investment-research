# Business

## Current understanding

State how the company makes money in five sentences or fewer. Cover what it sells, who pays, why customers buy, how revenue is earned, and which capability may be difficult to replicate. Label the section incomplete until each claim has evidence.

## Products and customer value

For each material offering, document the customer, problem solved, purchase criterion, pricing unit, revenue recurrence, and switching alternative. Use reported segment definitions but explain the underlying economics rather than copying disclosure labels.

## Revenue model

Map each material stream to volume or activity, price, mix, contract duration, recognition method, cyclicality, and currency. Explain dependencies among segments and identify pass-through revenue.

## Customers and channels

Assess customer concentration, route to market, contract structure, renewal or repeat behavior, bargaining power, and customer economics. Distinguish direct customers from end users.

## Cost structure and capital intensity

Identify major variable and fixed costs, required working capital, maintenance and growth capital expenditure, capacity constraints, and incremental margin behavior.

## Competitive landscape

Name relevant competitors and substitutes. Compare customer value, cost, technology, scale, distribution, capacity, and business-model differences using sourced evidence.

## Competitive advantage

For each proposed advantage, record its mechanism, evidence, durability, reinvestment requirement, and erosion signal. Do not infer a moat solely from past market share or margins.

## Management and governance

Assess incentives, ownership, disclosure quality, related-party exposure, execution record, and strategic consistency. Keep capital-allocation outcomes in `02_financials.md`.

## Sources

List primary sources with title, publisher, publication date, page or section, URL, and access date.
