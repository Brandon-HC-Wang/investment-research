# Growth Drivers

## Driver map

Connect each material driver to an observable operating variable, revenue mechanism, margin effect, expected start and duration, confidence, and source. Classify it as structural, cyclical, acquired, project-based, or accounting timing.

## Current revenue drivers

Explain drivers already contributing to reported revenue. Distinguish unit or activity growth, ASP, mix, market share, capacity, and FX.

## Future drivers

For drivers not yet fully contributing, document development or qualification stage, customer evidence, addressable economics, start window, ramp constraints, and probability. Do not treat an announced opportunity as forecast revenue without supporting evidence.

## Backlog and leading indicators

Use the forward indicators appropriate to the business. Assess backlog for cancellation, price, customer credit, execution capacity, cost exposure, and recognition timing. Where formal backlog is unavailable, use installed base, subscriptions, design wins, orders, land bank, utilization, or other evidence.

## Replacement drivers

Identify what sustains earnings after each current driver matures. If no replacement is visible, make the gap explicit and lower visibility rather than assuming continuity.

## Two-to-five-year visibility

For each forecast year, record the primary growth driver, replacement driver, leading indicator, confidence, and largest uncertainty. Extend beyond three years when the thesis depends on longer project or product cycles.

## EPS-relevant risks

Maintain only the one or two most consequential risk mechanisms. Trace each from event to operating metric, revenue or margin, EPS, cash flow, and early warning signal.

## Revision history

Append dated changes to driver timing, magnitude, status, or confidence.
