# Open Questions

Questions are durable hypothesis controls. Never delete a question. Use IDs in the form `Q-YYYY-NNN` and statuses `Open`, `Resolved`, or `Rejected`.

## Question record

Each question must include:

- **ID and status**
- **Opened:** ISO date.
- **Question:** one answerable, decision-relevant question.
- **Why it matters:** expected revenue, EPS, visibility, risk, or thesis effect.
- **Current evidence:** supporting, contradicting, and missing evidence.
- **Resolution method:** source, calculation, interview, disclosure, or observed milestone needed.
- **Owner/next review:** responsible workflow and trigger date or event.
- **Status history:** append dated changes.

For `Resolved`, add the answer, evidence, confidence, and thesis effect. For `Rejected`, explain why the premise was invalid, immaterial, or unanswerable as framed. A resolved question may be reopened through a new dated status event.

## Open-question index

Maintain a compact index of ID, short question, EPS materiality, next trigger, and current status before the detailed records.
