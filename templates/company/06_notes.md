# Research Notes

Notes hold sourced observations that may matter but are not yet sufficiently developed to change the canonical business, financial, model, or thesis sections. This is not a news inbox.

## Observation format

Each note records:

- **Date observed:** ISO date.
- **Type:** fact, management claim, estimate, hypothesis, or opinion.
- **Source:** full source metadata.
- **Observation:** concise original summary.
- **Potential mechanism:** possible revenue, margin, EPS, cash-flow, or competitive effect.
- **Current implication:** why the observation matters or why it is not yet material.
- **Next verification:** specific evidence needed.
- **Promotion target:** canonical file to update if verified.

When a note becomes material, update its canonical target and append the promotion date. Preserve the original note.
