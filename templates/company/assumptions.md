# Research Assumptions

Use this append-only register for every explicit assumption that can materially affect revenue, margin, EPS, future visibility, risk, or valuation. Valid statuses are `Open`, `Verified`, and `Rejected`. Never delete an assumption; append dated changes.

## Required assumption record

Each assumption must contain:

- **ID:** Stable identifier in the form `A-YYYY-NNN`.
- **Description:** One testable claim.
- **Confidence:** Percentage reflecting current evidential support.
- **Status:** `Open`, `Verified`, or `Rejected`.
- **Opened:** ISO date.
- **Evidence for and against:** Sources and observations on both sides.
- **Verification test:** Observable evidence and expected time window.
- **Model effect:** Revenue, margin, EPS, cash-flow, thesis, or valuation consequence.
- **History:** Dated confidence and status changes.

Verification does not erase uncertainty. Rejection does not erase the original assumption.
