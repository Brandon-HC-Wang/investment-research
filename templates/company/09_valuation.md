# Valuation

Valuation follows the operating thesis and estimates scenario implications. It is not a recommendation.

## Valuation context

State the as-of date, currency, share-price source if used, diluted share count, net cash or debt, minority interests, investments, and other adjustments.

## Normalized earnings basis

Link to `04_eps_model.md` and reconcile reported to normalized earnings. Specify the fiscal period and exclude unsupported one-offs or peak-cycle assumptions.

## Method selection

Choose the primary and cross-check methods based on business economics. Explain why each method is appropriate and why common alternatives are less useful.

## Scenario valuation

Apply valuation to downside, base, and upside operating cases. Separate revenue, margin, EPS, and cash-flow assumptions from multiples, discount rates, and terminal assumptions.

## Relative context

When using history or peers, normalize accounting definitions and explain structural differences in growth, returns, leverage, cyclicality, governance, and earnings quality.

## Sensitivity and limitations

Show sensitivity to the most uncertain operating and valuation variables. State terminal-value dependence, data limitations, non-operating assets, dilution, and realization constraints where relevant.

## Valuation history

Append dated changes to methods, normalized earnings, multiples, discount rates, and conclusions. Link operating changes to the EPS-model history.
