# Company Template Guide

The former single-file company template has been replaced by the durable multi-file contract in [`company/`](company/). The split keeps business analysis, financial history, models, questions, thesis, and valuation independently addressable while preserving a stable interface for humans and AI agents.

## Create a company record

Copy the entire `company/` directory to `../companies/<ticker>-<slug>/`, then replace the instructional template content with sourced research. Do not combine the numbered files or change their names.

The template is intentionally methodological rather than populated with a fictional company. Active company records must contain real identifiers, evidence, assumptions, and next actions.
