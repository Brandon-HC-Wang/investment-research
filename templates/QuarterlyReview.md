# Quarterly Review

## Review identity

Record company, fiscal period, release date, review date, reporting currency, and the prior model snapshot used for comparison.

## Operating variance

Compare reported units or activity, ASP, mix, share, backlog, utilization, and other leading indicators with prior assumptions. Explain mechanisms and distinguish demand from timing.

## Financial variance

Bridge revenue, gross margin, operating expenses, below-the-line items, tax, diluted shares, reported EPS, normalized EPS, cash flow, and balance sheet from prior estimates to reported results.

## Earnings quality

Classify new items as recurring core, recurring cyclical, project-based, or non-recurring. Assess cash backing and working-capital effects.

## Forward evidence

Evaluate guidance, orders, backlog quality, qualification, project schedules, and replacement drivers. Attribute management claims and state independent confirming or contradicting evidence.

## Thesis test

List strengthened, weakened, unchanged, or falsified thesis conditions. Update relevant question statuses without deleting history.

## Required repository updates

Identify changes to financials, growth drivers, EPS model, timeline, notes, questions, thesis, valuation, metadata, industry knowledge, and research log. A quarterly review is incomplete until these canonical updates are made.
