# Research Log Entry

## YYYY-MM-DD — Concise finding

- **Scope:** Companies, industry, or model affected.
- **Type:** Fact, estimate, hypothesis, resolution, rejection, or methodology change.
- **Finding:** State the new conclusion precisely.
- **Evidence:** Cite the strongest supporting and contradicting sources.
- **Mechanism:** Explain the revenue, margin, EPS, cash-flow, or competitive pathway.
- **Confidence:** Low, medium, or high, with the binding uncertainty.
- **Repository impact:** Link every canonical record changed.
- **Next verification:** State a concrete source, event, calculation, and time window.

For hypothesis changes, include prior status and new status. Append the entry chronologically; never rewrite an earlier log entry.
