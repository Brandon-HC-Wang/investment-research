# Source Record

## Identity

- **Publisher or company:** Record the legally identifiable issuer.
- **Title:** Use the source’s published title.
- **Document type:** Filing, transcript, presentation, regulatory data, industry data, or secondary analysis.
- **Publication date:** Use ISO format.
- **Reporting period:** Record the covered fiscal or calendar period.
- **URL or repository path:** Prefer a stable primary-source link.
- **Access date:** Use ISO format.
- **Pages or sections:** Identify the relevant location in long documents.

## Reliability

State whether the source is audited, regulated, company-authored, third-party, translated, revised, or potentially conflicted. Record known definition differences.

## Extracted evidence

Summarize only decision-relevant facts and management claims. Preserve units, period, definitions, and attribution. Separate direct evidence from calculations.

## Analytical impact

Describe the affected operating driver, EPS assumption, question, risk, or thesis condition. Link to every canonical record updated from the source.

## Unresolved conflicts

Record conflicting sources or missing evidence and create an open question when material.
