# EPS Model Standard

## Objective

Translate observable operating drivers into reported and normalized diluted EPS while preserving the uncertainty and history behind every material assumption.

## Causal bridge

```text
Units or activity × ASP × mix × FX
= Revenue
× Gross margin
= Gross profit
− Operating expenses
= Operating profit
± Interest, investment income, and other items
− Tax and minority interests
= Net income attributable to common shareholders
÷ Weighted-average diluted shares
= Diluted EPS
```

Use segment-level drivers when segment economics differ materially. Consolidated growth rates are outputs, not primary assumptions.

## Required forecast periods

Include the latest reported year, current-year estimate, and at least the following two fiscal years. Extend to five years when the thesis depends on long-duration projects, capacity, or product roadmaps.

## Assumption register

Each material assumption must state value or range, unit, period, source or calculation, confidence, and last revision date. Append revision history rather than replacing the old rationale.

## Normalization bridge

Reconcile reported EPS with normalized EPS by listing each material one-off, its pre-tax amount, tax treatment, per-share effect, evidence, and recurrence judgment. Do not remove recurring stock compensation, maintenance costs, or ordinary cycle losses merely because management labels them adjusted.

## Scenario architecture

Maintain downside, base, and upside cases. Change operational variables such as units, ASP, mix, utilization, margin, recognition timing, or share count. Only then apply valuation assumptions.

## Integrity checks

- Segment revenue reconciles to consolidated revenue.
- Margin arithmetic and tax calculations are reproducible.
- Balance-sheet and cash-flow consequences are plausible.
- Share count reflects issuance, repurchases, options, and convertibles.
- Backlog is not recognized beyond executable capacity or disclosed timing.
- One-off items do not enter normalized terminal earnings without evidence.
- Thesis statements and valuation inputs match the current base case.
