# Construction Industry Knowledge Package

## Scope

This package covers contractors, homebuilders, property developers, and mixed construction-development businesses. Company analysis must identify which economic model applies because backlog, cash conversion, risk, and valuation differ materially among them.

The legacy overview remains available at [`../Construction.md`](../Construction.md). This package is the canonical location for new construction-industry knowledge.

## Economic model

Contractors convert orders into revenue as work is performed or accepted. Developers invest in land and construction before recognizing sales at completion or handover. Mixed companies require separate models for contract services, wholly owned projects, joint development, urban renewal, and recurring rental or management income.

Monthly revenue is a lagging and timing-sensitive output. Future EPS visibility depends on executable backlog, attributable ownership, completion schedules, margin quality, funding, and the projects that replace current recognition.
