# Construction Open Questions

This registry stores questions that apply across construction companies. Preserve questions and append status changes; do not replace them with a current answer.

## IQ-CON-001 — Backlog comparability

- **Status:** Open.
- **Question:** Which disclosed backlog measures are economically comparable across contractors and developers?
- **Why it matters:** Definitions may include cancellable orders, gross joint-project value, or unpermitted land that overstates visibility.
- **Resolution path:** Build a definition map from filings and reconcile backlog to subsequent revenue and cash collection.

## IQ-CON-002 — Urban-renewal probability

- **Status:** Open.
- **Question:** Which observable milestones provide the best probability weights for urban-renewal projects before construction begins?
- **Why it matters:** Early-stage rights may remain unresolved for years and should not receive full NAV credit.
- **Resolution path:** Compare consent, approval, demolition, financing, and launch milestones with historical completion outcomes.

## IQ-CON-003 — Sustainable margin

- **Status:** Open.
- **Question:** How should normalized margin be estimated when project mix and land cost vintages change materially?
- **Why it matters:** Recent completion margins may not represent replacement-project economics.
- **Resolution path:** Decompose margin by project type, land vintage, pricing, cost inflation, and ownership structure.
