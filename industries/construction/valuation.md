# Construction Valuation

## Method selection

Use valuation methods that match the underlying activity:

- **Contract construction:** normalized P/E or EV/EBITDA, cross-checked against backlog quality, margins, capital intensity, and cash conversion.
- **Property development:** project-level NAV or probability-weighted earnings, adjusted for completion timing, cost, tax, debt, and attributable ownership.
- **Asset ownership:** income or cash-flow methods based on stabilized occupancy and sustainable rent.
- **Mixed groups:** sum-of-the-parts with explicit corporate costs and holding-company adjustments.

## Project valuation discipline

Do not treat gross development value as equity value. Deduct land, construction, marketing, financing, tax, partner shares, and remaining capital. Discount distant or unpermitted projects and probability-weight uncertain urban-renewal rights.

Avoid counting a project in both NAV and forward earnings. If earnings are capitalized, remove the corresponding project value from NAV or explain the bridge.

## Multiple discipline

Peak completion-year EPS rarely represents sustainable earnings. Apply multiples to normalized recurring or replacement-supported earnings. A low P/E may reflect a recognition cliff, funding risk, or one-off property gains rather than mispricing.

## Sensitivities

At minimum, test completion delay, sell-through, selling price, construction cost, gross margin, interest rate, ownership share, and terminal replacement assumptions.
