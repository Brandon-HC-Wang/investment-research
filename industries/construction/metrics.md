# Construction Metrics

## Core operating metrics

| Metric | Why it matters | Quality checks |
|---|---|---|
| Contracted backlog | Starting point for future contractor revenue | Cancellation, price escalation, customer credit, scope, and executable capacity |
| Attributable project value | Measures the company’s economic share | Ownership, joint-development split, minorities, and double counting |
| Land bank | Shows potential future supply | Permits, zoning, acquisition cost, funding, and realistic launch date |
| Presales and sell-through | Tests demand before completion | Cancellation rights, deposit quality, unit mix, and price concessions |
| Construction progress | Supports completion timing | Physical progress, permits, labor, materials, and contractor availability |
| Completion schedule | Drives project recognition | Handover requirements and risk of movement between fiscal years |
| Gross margin by project | Reveals project quality | Land basis, cost inflation, capitalized interest, marketing, and warranty cost |
| Net debt and project funding | Measures balance-sheet resilience | Restricted cash, guarantees, joint ventures, and refinancing maturity |
| Operating cash flow | Tests earnings quality | Customer deposits, land purchases, contract assets, and receivables |

## Required project record

Each material project should identify location, type, ownership, partner, total value, attributable value, units, launch date, presales, construction status, expected completion, recognition method, estimated margin, remaining cost, debt, and the source and date of every assumption.

## Visibility interpretation

Backlog is not equivalent to earnings. Translate it through recognition probability, capacity, timing, cost-to-complete, gross margin, tax, financing, and diluted ownership. Distinguish a timing delay from economic impairment.

## Warning indicators

Monitor falling presale conversion, rising cancellations, stalled permits, cost-to-complete revisions, contract-asset growth without cash collection, large guarantees, project concentration, and a completion cliff without replacement projects.
