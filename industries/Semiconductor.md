# Semiconductor

> Backward-compatible overview. New reusable research belongs in the canonical [`semiconductor/`](semiconductor/README.md) knowledge package. Existing content below is preserved.

## Economic model

Semiconductor earnings combine structural content growth with inventory and capacity cycles. Revenue must be decomposed into shipments, ASP, product mix, market share, end demand, and FX; margin depends on utilization, yield, mix, pricing, and inventory accounting.

## Required metrics

- Inventory at the company, distributor, and customer levels.
- Capacity, utilization, yield, and planned capital expenditure.
- Unit shipments, ASP, node or product mix, and market share.
- Gross margin and sensitivity to utilization or inventory provisions.
- End-market and customer concentration.
- AI exposure measured by actual content, units, qualification, and revenue—not labels.

## Cycle diagnosis

Separate sell-in from sell-through. Rising shipments can refill a depleted channel before end demand improves; falling shipments can reflect digestion rather than lost design position. Cross-check lead times, inventory days, utilization, pricing, and customer commentary.

## EPS sensitivities

Model utilization and gross margin explicitly. Include inventory write-downs or reversals, depreciation from new capacity, FX, investment income, tax incentives, and dilution where material.

## Roadmap discipline

For AI, memory, or new standards, record qualification stage, production timing, content per device, addressable units, share assumption, and margin. A roadmap mention is not a forecastable revenue driver until evidence supports timing and economics.
