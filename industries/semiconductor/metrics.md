# Semiconductor Metrics

## Demand and pricing

| Metric | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| End-market units and content | Separates device demand from semiconductor content growth |
| Company shipments | Measures sell-in, not necessarily end consumption |
| ASP and product mix | Distinguishes pricing from technology or application mix |
| Market share and design wins | Tests competitive position and future ramps |
| Lead times and order changes | Provide cycle evidence but can be distorted by double ordering |

## Inventory and capacity

Track company inventory by raw material, work in process, and finished goods when available; also assess distributors and customers. Use days and absolute value with write-down policy. Capacity analysis should include wafer starts or equivalent capacity, utilization, yield, node mix, outsourcing, capital expenditure, and depreciation.

## Margin bridge

Reconcile gross margin through ASP, mix, utilization, yield, input cost, depreciation, inventory provisions or reversals, and FX. Reconcile EPS further through R&D, selling costs, investment income, tax incentives, minority interests, and dilution.

## AI exposure evidence

Record actual component or service, platform, qualification stage, content per system, addressable units, customer concentration, production timing, and incremental margin. Roadmap participation alone is not recognized demand.

## Warning indicators

Monitor inventory growth ahead of sales, utilization decline, ASP pressure, customer concentration, capacity commitments without demand support, rising receivable days, delayed qualification, and gross-margin divergence from mix claims.
