# Semiconductor Open Questions

## IQ-SEMI-001 — Inventory normalization

- **Status:** Open.
- **Question:** Which combined company, channel, and customer indicators best identify normalized inventory by value-chain position?
- **Why it matters:** Company shipments may recover before end demand or remain weak while customers finish digestion.
- **Resolution path:** Compare inventory days, utilization, lead times, ASP, channel commentary, and end-market sell-through across cycles.

## IQ-SEMI-002 — AI revenue quality

- **Status:** Open.
- **Question:** How should AI-related revenue be classified when expensive components are passed through at low margin?
- **Why it matters:** Revenue exposure may overstate incremental gross profit and EPS.
- **Resolution path:** Reconcile content, bill-of-material treatment, gross margin, working capital, and customer contracts.

## IQ-SEMI-003 — Capacity discipline

- **Status:** Open.
- **Question:** Which leading indicators best predict whether announced capacity will earn its cost of capital?
- **Why it matters:** Capacity can create growth while reducing returns and future margins.
- **Resolution path:** Track committed demand, qualification, ramp yield, utilization, pricing, depreciation, and incremental return on capital.
