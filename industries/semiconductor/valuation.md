# Semiconductor Valuation

## Normalize the cycle

Do not capitalize peak utilization, shortage pricing, inventory reversals, or trough write-downs as permanent. Build mid-cycle revenue, utilization, gross margin, R&D, tax, and capital expenditure from operating evidence.

## Method selection

- **Fabless design:** normalized P/E or DCF informed by product durability, R&D reinvestment, customer concentration, and cash conversion.
- **Foundry or manufacturing:** EV/EBITDA and DCF cross-checked with replacement capital expenditure, utilization, and return on invested capital.
- **Memory:** normalized earnings or asset-aware methods across the price and capacity cycle; spot multiples on peak or trough earnings can mislead.
- **Equipment and materials:** normalized P/E or DCF tied to customer capital intensity, installed base, service recurrence, and technology transitions.

## Growth valuation discipline

Separate semiconductor-content growth from temporary inventory restocking and price recovery. A premium multiple requires durable share, product differentiation, credible reinvestment returns, and a replacement roadmap after the current platform.

## Sensitivities

Test shipments, ASP, utilization, yield, gross margin, R&D, FX, tax, and capital expenditure. For long-duration cases, show terminal-value dependence and the effect of faster technology obsolescence.
