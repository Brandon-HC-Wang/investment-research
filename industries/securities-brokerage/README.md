# Securities Brokerage Industry Package

Created 2026-10-12. A broker earns transaction fees, financing spreads, underwriting/service fees and trading/market-making returns. Asset management may be consolidated or recognized through an associate; ownership and accounting scope determine where its profit appears.

Revenue stability depends on trading value, fee yield, loan balances, interest spreads, average assets under management (AUM), net flows and net market exposure. A recurring fee mechanism can still be cyclical. A crash may initially increase trading activity, followed by reduced activity and financing; assess both phases.

Read [metrics](metrics.md), [valuation](valuation.md) and [questions](open_questions.md). Company evidence and estimates remain in [Capital Securities](../../companies/6005-capital-securities/01_business.md); this package defines reusable analytical rules, not industry forecasts.
