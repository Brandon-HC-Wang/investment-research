# Brokerage Valuation

Use P/B alongside normalized P/E. Interpret P/B through sustainable ROE, required return, growth, capital adequacy and the realizability of book assets. Equity and client funds are not interchangeable. For a regulated intermediary, adding gross cash or trading assets to an earnings multiple risks double counting assets required for business and ignoring funding/client liabilities.

Separate an exceptional strong trading year from through-cycle earnings. Do not automatically remove all trading income: it is part of the operating model. Do not remove asset gains without retaining related hedge losses or ordinary compensation/capital costs. Historical low P/E can reflect a peak denominator.

A yield check must use an explicit cash-dividend forecast and payment timing. Stock dividends at nominal value are excluded from cash yield. Show a capital-retention case even when earnings are high.
