# Brokerage Open Questions

## IQ-SB-001 — Net exposure behind large trading lines

- Status: Open. Opened 2026-10-12.
- Question: How can public disclosures reconcile economic hedges, gross accounting P&L and net tail risk?
- Resolution: Compare trading-line reconciliations, net Greeks/duration, scenario losses, collateral and stress methodology; VaR is not a crash-loss ceiling.

## IQ-SB-002 — Cash payout under capital expansion

- Status: Open. Opened 2026-10-12.
- Question: Which earnings are available as cash distributions after required reserves, subsidiaries' remittance and capital retention?
- Resolution: Trace parent-company distribution decisions and capital budgets rather than applying group EPS × historical total-dividend payout mechanically.
