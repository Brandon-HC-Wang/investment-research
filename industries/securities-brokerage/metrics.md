# Brokerage Metrics and Earnings Quality

- Brokerage: traded value × market share × realized commission yield. Distinguish volume from index level and subtract exchange/variable compensation costs.
- Financing: average margin/securities-loan balances × loan yield, less funding costs and credit losses. Track collateral, concentration, margin calls and liquidity.
- Asset management: average AUM × fee rate, less manager expenses and taxes, then apply ownership. Do not multiply fee revenue by ownership and label it attributable profit. Net flows and price changes both affect AUM; equity/bond/cash products respond differently.
- Trading: reconcile realized securities profits, valuation P&L, short/borrowed-security P&L, derivatives and warrant liabilities together. A hedge asset gain alone is not net profit; operating FVTPL can be ordinary but highly cyclical business income.
- Capital and cash: separate client money and customer liabilities from corporate liquidity; track capital adequacy, funded inventory, commercial paper/repo funding and stress losses. Financial-company operating cash flow includes financing/trading balance-sheet movements.
- Per share: retrospectively align bonus-share denominators, distinguish parent earnings from group net income, and keep forecast employee dilution explicit.
- Dividends: specify earnings year and payment year, cash versus stock, reserves, capital retention and approvals. Stock dividends are not cash yield and future cash payout need not match the historical total payout ratio.
