# Property Agency

> Backward-compatible overview. New reusable research belongs in the canonical [`property-agency/`](property-agency/README.md) knowledge package. Existing content below is preserved.

## Segment separation

Analyze three economic streams independently:

1. **Brokerage:** transaction value, take rate, store or agent productivity, market share, and fixed-cost leverage.
2. **Development:** project ownership, cost, presales, completion, recognition, and margin.
3. **Investment projects:** equity stakes, distributions, fair-value or disposal gains, and timing.

Blended revenue can obscure the distinction between recurring brokerage economics and discrete project earnings.

## Required operating metrics

Track transaction volume, average price, take rate, market share, active locations or agents, revenue per location, personnel expense, advertising, unrecognized projects, invested capital, and attributable project economics.

## EPS modeling

Model brokerage through market volume × share × take rate, then operating leverage. Model development and investments project by project and year. Classify property sales, investment gains, and revaluation effects separately from normalized brokerage earnings.

## Principal risks

The most direct EPS risks are transaction-volume contraction against a fixed cost base and delayed or impaired project recognition. Monitor mortgage conditions, transaction policy, inventory, personnel productivity, and project funding.
