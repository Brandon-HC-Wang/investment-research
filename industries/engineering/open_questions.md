# Engineering Open Questions

## IQ-ENG-001 — Comparable backlog

- **Status:** Open.
- **Question:** How can disclosed backlog be normalized across different cancellation, escalation, and recognition terms?
- **Why it matters:** Nominal backlog can overstate both revenue visibility and margin protection.
- **Resolution path:** Build contract-quality attributes and compare disclosed backlog with subsequent recognition and cash collection.

## IQ-ENG-002 — Early margin warning

- **Status:** Open.
- **Question:** Which balance-sheet and operational indicators reveal project margin deterioration before formal provisions?
- **Why it matters:** Profit estimates can change abruptly late in fixed-price projects.
- **Resolution path:** Test contract assets, receivables, payable timing, progress slippage, procurement cost, and management estimate revisions.

## IQ-ENG-003 — Capacity-constrained growth

- **Status:** Open.
- **Question:** At what point does backlog growth exceed engineering, labor, equipment, or management capacity?
- **Why it matters:** Excess orders can reduce margin and cash conversion rather than create value.
- **Resolution path:** Compare backlog per employee, subcontracting, utilization, turnover, project delays, and incremental margins.
