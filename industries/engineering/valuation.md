# Engineering Valuation

## Earnings basis

Use normalized earnings that reflect a representative mix of projects, bid discipline, and provision experience. Exclude uncollected claims and exceptional reversals unless recurrence and collectability are evidenced.

## Method selection

Normalized P/E is useful when capital needs and below-the-line items are stable. EV/EBITDA can assist comparisons across capital structures but must be cross-checked against working capital, maintenance capital expenditure, and provisions. DCF is useful when backlog conversion and reinvestment can be modeled with credible timing.

## Backlog interpretation

Backlog is not enterprise value. It supports forecasts only after applying recognition timing, cancellation risk, execution capacity, margin, tax, cash conversion, and replacement assumptions. Premium valuation requires profitable backlog replenishment, not merely backlog growth.

## Sensitivities

Test book-to-bill, recognition delay, project margin, cost overrun, change-order realization, customer default, working capital, and guarantees. Downside cases should include correlated problems in large projects rather than uniform percentage cuts.
