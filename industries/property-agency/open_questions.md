# Property Agency Open Questions

## IQ-PA-001 — Recurring earnings disclosure

- **Status:** Open.
- **Question:** What is the most reliable method for separating recurring agency earnings from development and investment income when segment disclosure is limited?
- **Why it matters:** Blended earnings can lead to inappropriate annualization and valuation.
- **Resolution path:** Reconcile segment notes, cash flow, project disclosures, subsidiaries, and major monthly revenue movements.

## IQ-PA-002 — Agency advantage

- **Status:** Open.
- **Question:** Which metrics best distinguish durable agency relationships from exposure to a favorable property cycle?
- **Why it matters:** Repeat mandates and productivity may deserve a higher-quality classification than temporary transaction growth.
- **Resolution path:** Compare multi-cycle market share, repeat clients, take rate, sell-through, and revenue per site or employee.

## IQ-PA-003 — Policy transmission

- **Status:** Open.
- **Question:** How quickly do mortgage and transaction-policy changes affect mandates, sales, fee recognition, and agency margins?
- **Why it matters:** Lag structure determines forecast timing and downside severity.
- **Resolution path:** Map policy dates to transaction data, presales, company activity, and margin with consistent definitions.
