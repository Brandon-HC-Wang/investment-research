# Property Agency Valuation

## Segmented approach

Value recurring brokerage or agency earnings separately from development and investment projects:

- Apply normalized P/E or a cash-flow method to cash-backed recurring agency earnings.
- Use project-level NAV or probability-weighted attributable earnings for development.
- Value investment projects by expected distributions or realizable net asset value.
- Deduct corporate costs, net debt, guarantees, minority interests, and dilution.

## Normalization

Agency earnings are cyclical when transaction activity changes against a fixed branch, site, or personnel base. Normalize across a representative transaction environment and do not give a recurring multiple to one-time project recognition or asset gains.

## Comparative valuation

Peer multiples require consistent separation of fee businesses and principal investment. Compare take rate, productivity, market share, client concentration, capital intensity, cash conversion, and governance before comparing P/E.

## Sensitivities

Test transaction value, market share, take rate, personnel-cost flexibility, project completion, ownership share, gross margin, and funding cost. Keep market-activity assumptions separate from valuation multiples.
