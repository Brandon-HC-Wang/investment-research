# AI Hardware Valuation

## Normalize economic exposure

Value incremental gross profit and free cash flow, not the gross value of expensive components that pass through revenue. Normalize one-time launch costs and peak allocation benefits while retaining recurring warranty, working-capital, and reinvestment needs.

## Method selection

Use normalized P/E or DCF for suppliers with sustainable content, design positions, and cash conversion. EV/EBITDA may aid hardware manufacturing comparisons but must be adjusted for working capital and maintenance capital. Sum-of-the-parts can separate structurally different server, component, and service activities.

## Duration discipline

A premium multiple requires evidence that earnings persist beyond one platform or customer deployment. Assess replacement roadmaps, qualification portability, switching costs, customer diversification, content progression, and returns on incremental capital.

## Sensitivities

Test deployed systems, content per system, market share, ASP, pass-through mix, gross margin, ramp cost, working capital, customer concentration, and platform timing. Keep adoption assumptions separate from the valuation multiple.
