# AI Hardware Metrics

## Platform and demand metrics

| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Accelerator or GPU roadmap | Determines platform timing and architecture transitions |
| Qualification stage | Separates concept, sampling, qualification, order, production, and recognized revenue |
| Systems or racks deployed | Converts infrastructure demand into unit opportunity |
| Content per system | Measures company-specific economic exposure |
| Market share and customer concentration | Determines scale, bargaining power, and risk |
| Backlog and cancellation terms | Tests order visibility and double ordering |

## Margin and cash metrics

Track gross profit dollars as well as revenue and margin percentage. Separate value-added content from pass-through accelerators or memory. Assess ramp inefficiency, warranty, inventory commitments, receivable terms, supplier advances, capital expenditure, and cash conversion.

## Architecture metrics

Follow compute density, memory capacity and bandwidth, network speed, power per rack, cooling architecture, storage, interconnect, and service content. Translate each architecture change into company content, qualification, and margin rather than treating the change as a generic catalyst.

## Replacement metrics

For each ramp, identify the next platform, broader customer adoption, rising content, recurring service, or replacement cycle that can sustain earnings. Record when current growth becomes a difficult comparison.

## Warning indicators

Monitor customer concentration, platform delays, allocation changes, low-margin pass-through growth, inventory commitments, warranty provisions, architecture displacement, and a gap between announced qualifications and production revenue.
