# AI Hardware Open Questions

## IQ-AIH-001 — Pass-through economics

- **Status:** Open.
- **Question:** How can disclosures consistently separate value-added AI revenue from low-margin component pass-through?
- **Why it matters:** Headline revenue may materially overstate incremental profit and cash generation.
- **Resolution path:** Reconcile component content, gross profit dollars, inventory, supplier terms, and customer contracts.

## IQ-AIH-002 — Platform durability

- **Status:** Open.
- **Question:** Which qualification and architecture attributes make a supplier’s position portable across accelerator generations?
- **Why it matters:** A single-platform win may not support two-to-five-year earnings visibility.
- **Resolution path:** Compare socket continuity, redesign requirements, customer validation, content changes, and wins across successive platforms.

## IQ-AIH-003 — Infrastructure digestion

- **Status:** Open.
- **Question:** Which indicators distinguish enduring deployment growth from an order and inventory digestion cycle?
- **Why it matters:** Supply-chain orders can lead actual deployments and create false visibility.
- **Resolution path:** Compare cloud capital expenditure, accelerator shipments, system completions, network and power availability, inventory, and customer utilization.
