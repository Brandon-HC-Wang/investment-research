# Urban Renewal

## Definition

Urban renewal converts fragmented property rights and aging buildings into a redevelopment project through owner consent, public approvals, allocation agreements, demolition, financing, construction, and handover. Early participation is an option on future economics, not equivalent to executable backlog.

## Stage model

1. Target identification and preliminary negotiation.
2. Owner-consent accumulation.
3. Renewal plan and rights-allocation approval.
4. Relocation, demolition, and permit completion.
5. Financing and construction.
6. Sale, handover, and recognition.

Each stage changes probability, timing, capital needs, and valuation. Local law and contract structure determine the exact sequence.

## Required economics

Track consent ratio, site area, attributable rights, allocation to owners, saleable area, expected price, demolition and construction cost, relocation obligations, financing, partner shares, approval milestones, and downside if the project stalls.

## Common analytical errors

- Assigning full project value before consent or approval.
- Ignoring owner allocation and attributable ownership.
- Using a fixed completion date for a path-dependent process.
- Excluding carrying costs and contingent obligations.
- Counting the same economics in land bank, NAV, and EPS.
