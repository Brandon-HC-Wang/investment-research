# Backlog

## Definition

Backlog is contracted or otherwise committed future work not yet recognized as revenue. Its economic meaning varies by contract enforceability, cancellation rights, price protection, customer credit, execution capacity, and accounting definition.

## Causal role

`gross backlog → executable backlog → recognition schedule → project margin → cash collection → EPS and free cash flow`

Backlog supports visibility only after every transition is tested. A growing balance can coexist with weaker economics if pricing, mix, execution, or cash terms deteriorate.

## Required attributes

Track scope, customer, contract date, gross and attributable value, recognized amount, cancellation terms, escalation, start and completion dates, remaining cost, expected margin, cash terms, guarantees, and source date.

## Common analytical errors

- Treating gross backlog as revenue or equity value.
- Ignoring joint-venture ownership and minority interests.
- Assuming timing without checking capacity and customer acceptance.
- Comparing company-defined backlog without reconciling definitions.
- Valuing backlog without remaining cost, tax, funding, or replacement needs.
