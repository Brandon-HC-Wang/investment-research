# Research Strategies

Strategies state why a signal may identify a research opportunity and how that claim will be tested or rejected. They do not contain market-screen results and do not constitute recommendations.

Machine-readable definitions are registered in [`registry.yaml`](registry.yaml). Each strategy directory pairs `strategy.yaml` with English and Traditional Chinese reasoning documents. Status history is append-only.

## Active definitions

- [`forward-dividend-income/`](forward-dividend-income/README.md) — tests whether normalized earnings and distributable cash can identify sustainable forward cash yields.
