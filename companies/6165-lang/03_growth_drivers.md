# Growth drivers and visibility

As of 2026-09-21; [sources](sources/README.md). No quantified backlog exists in the reviewed disclosures. Contract liabilities of TWD 172.550 million at June end are prepaid consumption, not multi-year contracted orders.

| Horizon | Driver and replacement mechanism | Confirmation / failure signal |
|---|---|---|
| 2026 H2 | Live-platform payer retention and spending; maintain improved gross margin; medical disposal changes mix | Quarterly live revenue, gross profit and customer acquisition cost; consolidated monthly revenue alone is insufficient |
| 2027 | Existing creator communities plus voice/gaming monetization; modest growth assumed, not a second margin reset | Live revenue growth around 5% and margin near 30%; falling payers or escalating payouts invalidate the mechanism |
| 2028 | Retention and product depth must replace the 2025 cost-reset benefit | Repeat paid cohorts and incremental profit/cash; growth bought with matching marketing expense adds little value |
| 2029–2030 | Overseas distribution, short-form content and partnerships are optionality | Need segment revenue, customer economics and attributable profit before adding an incremental forecast |

FY25 live revenue growth of 1.71% contrasts with H126's 8.38%; the latter is better evidence for real platform growth, but not a five-year proof. H126 live segment result grew 13.17% (174.462 → 197.434), while other segment result declined 2.436 → -11.782. Segment results are the disclosed measure, not automatically clean operating profit.

IR25 presents voice/game companionship and overseas expansion. Secondary CALL notes attribute a monthly TWD 300 million live-business objective and contract improvements to management; original audio has not been checked, so neither is used as a verified forecast. August consolidated revenue of 286.160 million does not establish attainment of a live-only 300 million target.

WIS reports Wiselink purchased 6.299 million shares, 8.10%, for TWD 310.562 million in July–August. COOP attributes prospective streaming-cost and Southeast Asia cooperation to management. This is another shareholder's secondary-market investment, not cash raised by Lang, and no quantified synergy enters the base case. Medical leasing was disposed of in May; do not project the obsolete 2025 group structure forward.
