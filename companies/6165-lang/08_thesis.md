# Thesis — profitable platform, unproven compounding duration

Initiated 2026-09-21. Research status: qualifying. Confidence: medium in reported operating improvement; low in the multi-year forecast. [Financials](02_financials.md), [model](04_eps_model.md), [questions](07_open_questions.md).

## Current judgment

Lang's live platform has a demonstrated earnings base and H126 operating growth. The investment-research question is whether payer retention and retained margin can compound cash earnings after tax and capital allocation. FY25's dramatic profit improvement mostly reflects economics/cost changes, while most consolidated revenue growth came from other activities. It is not yet evidence of durable high platform growth.

Hypothesis H-2026-001: if paying users remain engaged without higher acquisition/payout costs, roughly 30% consolidated gross margin plus modest live growth can support sustainable earnings near the 2026 base and gradual 2027–2028 improvement. Mechanism: recurring paid consumption produces gross profit faster than fixed engineering/administration grows. Supporting evidence: H126 live revenue +8.38% and operating profit +11.92%. Contradicting evidence: FY25 live growth only 1.71%, absent payer/cohort data, H126 rising expenses and noncore losses. Confirmation: next two quarterly releases sustain live growth, gross profit and cash conversion. Rejection: two quarters of live contraction with margin below 28%, or persistent cash leakage. Window: 2026 H2–2028. Status: Open; confidence 55% (subjective research confidence, not outcome probability).

## Earnings risks that matter most

1. **Retention versus take rate.** Higher payouts or lost high-spending users reduce gross profit before fixed costs fall. A one-point annual gross-margin decline costs roughly TWD 0.36 EPS at the modeled 2026 scale. Track paid retention and creator economics, not downloads.
2. **Capital allocation and shareholder cash.** Property investment, noncore funding, related-party receivables, dividend commitments and employee share transfers can dilute cash returns despite app profits. H126 cash less debt/leases is only TWD 7.959 million before dividend settlement; a repeat 5.00 dividend is not established. Track prepayments, debt, share count and cash-flow coverage.

## Variant perception and falsification

Analytical distinction, not a measured consensus claim: a displayed near-10% dividend yield can obscure payout above earnings, while falling reported EPS can obscure genuine operating growth because tax and bond redemption differ. Both the optimistic dividend narrative and a purely bearish headline-EPS interpretation miss part of the evidence.
