# Lang — business and competitive position

As of 2026-09-21. Sources: [FY25, H126 and IR25](sources/README.md).

## Economic engine

Lang Inc. (浪凡網路科技股份有限公司, TWSE 6165), formerly Jye Tai, owns the Lang LIVE business through 旭瑞文化傳媒. It sells entertainment, interaction and social recognition: users buy virtual credits, then consume them through gifts and platform services. Unconsumed prepaid credits are contract liabilities, not recognized revenue (FY25 p. 23). IR25 slide 10 describes approximately 50% sharing with streamers; this is a management description, not a verified current blended cost ratio.

Revenue drivers are paying users × spending per payer × consumption frequency, modified by promotions, channel fees and business mix. MAU alone cannot forecast revenue. Streamer compensation, payment distribution, moderation and engineering determine retained gross profit; recurring development and stock compensation remain expenses.

## Competitive durability

Opinion: creator relationships, community engagement and local operations can sustain a niche advantage, but a wide moat is unproven. IR25 reports approximately 450,000 MAU and over 8,000 signed streamers; observation dates and subsequent retention cohorts are not disclosed. These are dated management metrics, not 2026 verified KPIs or independent market-share evidence.

A stronger creator network can improve retention and spread engineering cost. Conversely, creators and viewers can use competing entertainment platforms; increasing platform take can cause churn or higher acquisition expense. Obtain paying-user counts, payer concentration, retention, acquisition costs and effective creator payout before assigning a durable premium.

## Capital intensity

The app's operating model can be light in physical assets, but the listed group is not a cash-only platform. FY25 includes an office/parking purchase of TWD 613.700 million including tax; H126 property, plant and equipment is 1,354.846 million, with 1,272.415 million pledged. Capital allocation must be evaluated separately from app unit economics.
