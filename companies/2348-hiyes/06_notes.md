## 2026-08-04 — Monthly revenue is not sufficient visibility evidence

- **Type:** Fact and analytical observation.
- **Source:** Taiwan Stock Exchange company profile produced 2026-07-30.
- **Observation:** June 2026 revenue was TWD 1.498 billion, compared with TWD 140 million in April and TWD 170 million in May.
- **Potential mechanism:** The concentration may reflect project recognition, agency settlement timing, or development sales; the source does not separate these causes.
- **Current implication:** Do not infer a June run rate or a durable recovery from the headline figure.
- **Next verification:** Reconcile monthly changes to 2026 H1 segment notes and material project disclosures.
