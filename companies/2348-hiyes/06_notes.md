## 2026-08-04 — Monthly revenue is not sufficient visibility evidence

- **Type:** Fact and analytical observation.
- **Source:** Taiwan Stock Exchange company profile produced 2026-07-30.
- **Observation:** June 2026 revenue was TWD 1.498 billion, compared with TWD 140 million in April and TWD 170 million in May.
- **Potential mechanism:** The concentration may reflect project recognition, agency settlement timing, or development sales; the source does not separate these causes.
- **Current implication:** Do not infer a June run rate or a durable recovery from the headline figure.
- **Next verification:** Reconcile monthly changes to 2026 H1 segment notes and material project disclosures.

## 2026-08-17 — Q2 earnings recovery is not yet cash-backed sustainable EPS

- **Type:** Fact and analytical observation.
- **Source:** Hiyes International, “Consolidated Financial Statements and Review Report, 2026 Q2,” issued 2026-08-12, pp. 6, 8–9, 17–19, 25, 28–30, 33, 38, and 43–44, [local retained PDF](sources/earnings/2026Q2-hiyes-consolidated-financial-report.pdf), accessed 2026-08-17.
- **Observation:** Q2 standalone EPS recovered to TWD 0.91 as property-sale revenue rose to TWD 1.255 billion, but H1 operating cash flow was negative TWD 2.120 billion, inventory increased TWD 2.599 billion from year-end, and finance costs rose 30.8% year over year.
- **Potential mechanism:** Project handovers can lift construction gross profit abruptly, while inventory accumulation and borrowings finance the next project cycle. If handovers convert inventory into cash and debt declines, EPS may strengthen; if not, finance costs and refinancing exposure can absorb the operating gain.
- **Current implication:** Treat Q2 as evidence of recognition timing, not proof of normalized earnings. Fair-value gains, non-controlling losses, and the preferred dividend must remain explicit in the common-EPS bridge.
- **Next verification:** Reconcile each H2 project to property-sale revenue, gross profit, inventory release, receivables or cash, debt, and finance costs.
- **Promotion target:** Current implications are integrated into `02_financials.md`, `04_eps_model.md`, and `08_thesis.md`; project-level evidence remains open.
