## Business model and competitive advantage

**Fact —** Hiyes provides product planning, integrated marketing and property transaction intermediation for developers and purchasers. Consolidated activities also include development, so it cannot be evaluated as a pure fee business. [S6](#source-S6)

| Activity | Customer value and economic driver | Earnings and funding treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Agency | Developers obtain planning, marketing and sell-through execution; represented sales × fee yield drives revenue | Fees can recur across mandates but depend on transaction activity and site/personnel productivity |
| Development | Land and construction become saleable properties; ownership, completion, handover and project margin drive earnings | Discrete project profit; land, construction, guarantees and interest consume capital before collection |
| Investment projects | Participations in subsidiaries/investees | Model ownership, profit/remittances and commitments separately; do not count the same project twice |

**Hypothesis —** Developer relationships and sales execution may support repeat mandates. Multi-cycle share, repeat-client concentration, fee stability and productivity are not established. September new mandates do not by themselves verify a moat. Agency supply value is not owned development revenue. The baseline service scope is described in [the canonical business record](../01_business.md).

## Thesis, falsification and valuation

**Opinion —** The construction completion-cycle hypothesis is supported; sustainable per-share compounding is not established. Confirmation requires named-project gross profit, inventory release, collection and lower borrowing; agency stabilization; an executable replacement schedule; and a reconciled acquisition/parent cash map.

The thesis weakens if recognition fails to convert to cash, finance cost rises, agency stays depressed or replacement permits/completions slip. A durable-compounding hypothesis is rejected if earnings depend mainly on discrete projects/financial-asset gains without a repeatable fee or replacement base. Revise the cash path if payment timing, debt, reserves or board choices differ.

**Valuation deferred.** After reconciliation, use sum-of-the-parts: normalized recurring agency earnings plus attributable project value/distributions, deducting corporate costs, net debt, guarantees, minorities and dilution. New land cost is not incremental NAV; construction growth and conditional payout do not create a valuation floor. No current fair-value range, price target or buy/sell/hold instruction is adopted. [Current thesis](../08_thesis.md); [valuation method](../09_valuation.md).
