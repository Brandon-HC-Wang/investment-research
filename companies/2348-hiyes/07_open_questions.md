## Q-2026-001 — Normalized earnings by activity

- **Status:** Open.
- **Opened:** 2026-08-04.
- **Question:** How much of 2021–2025 earnings came from recurring brokerage, development projects, investment projects, and non-recurring items?
- **Why it matters:** The answer determines normalized EPS and whether historical earnings deserve capitalization.
- **Current evidence:** Reported EPS is volatile; no segment bridge is yet in the repository.
- **Resolution method:** Reconcile segment notes, project income, investment gains, tax, and diluted shares from annual and interim filings.
- **Owner/next review:** Next filing-review cycle.
- **Status history:** 2026-08-04 — Opened with low confidence.
- **Status history:** 2026-08-17 — Remains Open. The Q2 filing separates H1 service and property-sale revenue and segment gross profit, but it does not disclose represented sales value, fee rate, named-project profit, or a multi-year recurring agency bridge. H1 service revenue fell 44.6% and common EPS was partly supported by non-operating fair-value gains.

## Q-2026-002 — Attributable project visibility

- **Status:** Open.
- **Opened:** 2026-08-04.
- **Question:** Which agency, development, and investment projects can contribute to EPS in 2026, 2027, and 2028, and at what attributable economics?
- **Why it matters:** This is the binding question for replacement earnings and future visibility.
- **Current evidence:** Monthly revenue shows lumpiness but not project attribution.
- **Resolution method:** Build a project table with ownership, total value, presales, completion, recognition, margin, and funding.
- **Owner/next review:** Project-disclosure review after financial reconciliation.
- **Status history:** 2026-08-04 — Opened with low confidence.
- **Status history:** 2026-08-04 — Remains Open. The 2025-11-25 management schedule identifies 2026 and 2027 projects and participation, but sell-through, exact handover, project margins, and accounting attribution remain insufficient to resolve the question. Preliminary ranges are recorded in `04_eps_model.md`.
- **Status history:** 2026-08-04 — The conservative recognition haircut is clarified as primarily a timing assumption. An explicit project roll-forward is still required to separate delayed earnings carried into later years from permanent economic loss.
- **Status history:** 2026-08-17 — Remains Open. Q2 property-sale revenue and construction gross profit confirm that the 2026 handover cycle began, but the filing does not attribute revenue, margin, cash collection, or remaining inventory to named projects and adds no decision-grade 2027 replacement schedule.

## Q-2026-004 — Guarantees and funding exposure

- **Status:** Open.
- **Opened:** 2026-08-04.
- **Question:** What cash, debt, guarantees, and contingent funding are committed to subsidiaries and property projects?
- **Why it matters:** Funding obligations can reduce per-share value and amplify a project delay even when accounting profit remains positive.
- **Current evidence:** A complete exposure map has not been prepared.
- **Resolution method:** Reconcile debt, guarantees, related parties, project ownership, and liquidity from primary filings.
- **Owner/next review:** Balance-sheet review in the next research cycle.
- **Status history:** 2026-08-04 — Opened with low confidence.
- **Status history:** 2026-08-17 — Remains Open. The Q2 filing quantifies consolidated borrowings, pledged assets, undrawn facilities, finance costs, unfinished construction contracts, and guarantee schedules. Project-level guarantee and funding attribution is still insufficient to estimate loss severity, but the balance-sheet channel is now demonstrably material.
