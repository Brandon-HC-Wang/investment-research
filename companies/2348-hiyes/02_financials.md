# Financials

## Initial earnings profile

**Fact —** TWSE summary data show highly variable annual EPS: TWD 15.58 in 2021, 7.88 in 2022, 13.86 in 2023, 14.43 in 2024, and 6.85 in 2025. First-quarter 2026 EPS was TWD 0.16. This volatility is consistent with a business mix that may include discrete project recognition, but the cause has not yet been reconciled by segment.

**Fact —** The same TWSE profile reports paid-in capital of TWD 1.819 billion as of its 2026-07-30 production date. This is not a substitute for diluted weighted-average shares in an EPS model.

## Earnings quality

Normalized earnings have not been established. Brokerage, development, investment income, asset transactions, and other one-off items must be reconciled separately. Until that work is complete, historical EPS should not be annualized and 2026 Q1 should not be extrapolated.

## 2026 interim evidence available before the Q2 filing

**Fact —** The reviewed 2026 Q1 consolidated filing reported revenue of TWD 1.308 billion, gross profit of TWD 368 million, operating profit of TWD 216 million, finance costs of TWD 137 million, and profit attributable to the parent of TWD 24 million. Basic EPS was TWD 0.16. Agency revenue was TWD 604 million with segment profit of TWD 362 million; construction revenue was TWD 704 million with segment profit of only TWD 6 million. Segment profit is measured before corporate costs, finance costs, tax, and selected non-operating items.

**Fact —** Inventory increased from TWD 25.611 billion at 2025 year-end to TWD 28.092 billion at 2026-03-31, while short-term borrowings plus the current portion of long-term debt increased to TWD 10.332 billion. Q1 bank and lease interest totaled TWD 228 million before TWD 91 million of capitalization. This creates material downside if completion or handover moves to a later period.

**Fact —** Consolidated revenue for 2026 H1 was TWD 3.116 billion, down 4.1% year over year. June revenue was TWD 1.498 billion; the TWSE disclosure attributes the large increase to recognition of construction revenue from projects including the Houbitian section. The Q2 income statement and attributable margin were not yet public as of 2026-08-04.

## Cash flow, balance sheet, and capital allocation

These areas remain under primary-filing review. Particular attention is required for inventory, contract assets and liabilities, debt, subsidiary guarantees, investment-project funding, dividends, stock dividends, and diluted share-count changes.
