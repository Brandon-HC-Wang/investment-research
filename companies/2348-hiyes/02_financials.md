# Financials

## Initial earnings profile

**Fact —** TWSE summary data show highly variable annual EPS: TWD 15.58 in 2021, 7.88 in 2022, 13.86 in 2023, 14.43 in 2024, and 6.85 in 2025. First-quarter 2026 EPS was TWD 0.16. This volatility is consistent with a business mix that may include discrete project recognition, but the cause has not yet been reconciled by segment.

**Fact —** The same TWSE profile reports paid-in capital of TWD 1.819 billion as of its 2026-07-30 production date. This is not a substitute for diluted weighted-average shares in an EPS model.

## Earnings quality

Normalized earnings have not been established. Brokerage, development, investment income, asset transactions, and other one-off items must be reconciled separately. Until that work is complete, historical EPS should not be annualized and 2026 Q1 should not be extrapolated.

## 2026 interim evidence

**Fact —** The reviewed 2026 Q1 consolidated filing reported revenue of TWD 1.308 billion, gross profit of TWD 368 million, operating profit of TWD 216 million, finance costs of TWD 137 million, and profit attributable to the parent of TWD 24 million. Basic EPS was TWD 0.16. Agency revenue was TWD 604 million with segment profit of TWD 362 million; construction revenue was TWD 704 million with segment profit of only TWD 6 million. Segment profit is measured before corporate costs, finance costs, tax, and selected non-operating items.

**Fact —** Inventory increased from TWD 25.611 billion at 2025 year-end to TWD 28.092 billion at 2026-03-31, while short-term borrowings plus the current portion of long-term debt increased to TWD 10.332 billion. Q1 bank and lease interest totaled TWD 228 million before TWD 91 million of capitalization. This creates material downside if completion or handover moves to a later period.

**Fact —** Consolidated revenue for 2026 H1 was TWD 3.116 billion, down 4.0% year over year. June revenue was TWD 1.498 billion; the TWSE disclosure attributes the large increase to recognition of construction revenue from projects including the Houbitian section.

## 2026 Q2 filing review

**Fact —** Q2 standalone revenue was TWD 1.808 billion, up 18.3% year over year. The mix changed sharply: property-sale revenue increased 115.4% to TWD 1.255 billion, while service revenue fell 41.5% to TWD 553 million. Gross profit was nearly flat at TWD 444 million, so gross margin fell to 24.5% from 28.8%. Operating profit fell 5.9% to TWD 269 million and operating margin fell to 14.9% from 18.7%.

**Fact —** Q2 profit attributable to the parent was TWD 193 million, up 4.3% year over year, and basic EPS was TWD 0.91 versus the retrospectively adjusted TWD 0.86. This was not solely an operating recovery: Q2 included TWD 113 million of fair-value gains on financial assets, while finance costs rose 38.8% to TWD 149 million. The quarter also benefited from TWD 15 million of losses assigned to non-controlling interests.

**Fact —** H1 revenue fell 4.0% to TWD 3.116 billion, gross margin fell to 26.0% from 38.7%, operating profit fell 46.1% to TWD 485 million, parent profit fell 59.9% to TWD 217 million, and basic EPS fell 65.8% to TWD 1.06. H1 agency external revenue fell 44.6% to TWD 1.157 billion; construction revenue rose 69.0% to TWD 1.960 billion. Agency segment profit was TWD 508 million and construction segment profit TWD 304 million. The filing defines these segment results before corporate operating costs, finance costs, tax, and selected non-operating items; together they reconcile to consolidated gross profit, not operating profit.

**Interpretation —** The filing confirms that the 2026 handover cycle began, because Q2 property-sale revenue and construction gross profit rose materially from Q1. It does not establish sustainable EPS. Agency activity contracted, the consolidated margin mix weakened, finance costs absorbed 55.1% of Q2 operating profit, and non-operating fair-value gains supported reported profit.

## Cash flow, balance sheet, and capital allocation

**Fact —** H1 operating cash outflow was TWD 2.120 billion, driven principally by a TWD 2.441 billion cash-flow increase in inventory. At 2026-06-30, cash was TWD 4.733 billion, inventory TWD 28.210 billion, and interest-bearing borrowings excluding leases approximately TWD 31.112 billion. Borrowings increased TWD 2.391 billion from 2025 year-end, while cash fell TWD 1.191 billion. Pledged assets were TWD 32.079 billion, or 63.8% of total assets. The group disclosed TWD 20.552 billion of undrawn facilities, which reduces immediate liquidity risk but does not remove leverage or refinancing risk.

**Fact —** H1 finance costs rose 30.8% to TWD 285 million after TWD 183 million of interest was capitalized. The filing's sensitivity indicates that a one-percentage-point rate increase would reduce six-month pretax profit by TWD 124 million, equal to 37.6% of reported H1 pretax profit. Unfinished construction contracts had a total price of TWD 18.984 billion, of which TWD 5.919 billion had been paid. The guarantee schedule confirms material parent and subsidiary support, but project-level attribution remains necessary before the contingent exposure can be converted into an EPS or liquidity estimate.

**Review scope —** The auditors issued a qualified review conclusion because certain non-significant subsidiaries and equity-method investees were not reviewed. The affected subsidiaries represented 7.82% of assets and 8.59% of liabilities at 2026-06-30. This is a scope qualification, not evidence of an identified accounting misstatement, but it reduces assurance over part of the group.
