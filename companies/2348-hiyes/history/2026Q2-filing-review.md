# Hiyes 2026 Q2 Filing-Review Snapshot

## Snapshot identity

- **Created:** 2026-08-17.
- **Information cutoff:** Reviewed 2026 Q2 consolidated financial statements issued 2026-08-12.
- **Research stage:** Discovery.
- **Confidence:** Low for sustainable EPS; high for reported consolidated figures.

## Business and earnings state

Q2 standalone revenue was TWD 1.808 billion, up 18.3% year over year. Property-sale revenue increased 115.4% to TWD 1.255 billion while service revenue fell 41.5% to TWD 553 million. Gross margin fell to 24.5% from 28.8%, operating profit fell 5.9% to TWD 269 million, and finance costs rose 38.8% to TWD 149 million. Parent profit was TWD 193 million and basic EPS TWD 0.91, supported partly by TWD 113 million of fair-value gains and TWD 15 million of losses assigned to non-controlling interests.

H1 basic EPS was TWD 1.06, down 65.8% year over year. H1 service revenue fell 44.6%, gross margin fell to 26.0% from 38.7%, and finance costs rose 30.8% to TWD 285 million. The filing confirms a Q2 construction handover recovery but does not establish normalized agency earnings or project-level profitability.

## Cash flow and balance-sheet state

H1 operating cash outflow was TWD 2.120 billion, principally reflecting a TWD 2.441 billion cash-flow increase in inventory. At 2026-06-30, inventory was TWD 28.210 billion, cash TWD 4.733 billion, and borrowings excluding leases approximately TWD 31.112 billion. Pledged assets were TWD 32.079 billion, or 63.8% of assets. Undrawn facilities of TWD 20.552 billion support near-term liquidity, but leverage, finance costs, guarantees, and inventory conversion remain material EPS risks.

## Scenario and thesis effect

The dated 2026 scenario points of TWD 5.58 and TWD 11.32 are preserved as historical boundaries, not current forecasts. Reaching them would require approximately TWD 4.52 and TWD 10.26 of H2 EPS. The prior 12%–18% parent-margin proxies are superseded for current forecasting because actual H1 parent-attributable margin was 7.0% and common earnings after the preferred dividend were 5.4% of revenue.

The current thesis is that 2026 recognition timing has improved, but sustainable per-share compounding remains unverified. Confirmation now requires named-project margin, inventory release, operating cash inflow, lower borrowings, agency stabilization, and a credible 2027 replacement schedule.

## Open questions and next action

`Q-2026-001`, `Q-2026-002`, and `Q-2026-004` remain Open. The filing improves segment and balance-sheet visibility but does not resolve normalized activity earnings, named-project attribution, or project-level guarantee and funding exposure.

Next action: reconcile the six scheduled 2026 projects to H2 handovers, project-level gross profit, inventory release, cash collection, debt reduction, and the 2027 replacement schedule.
