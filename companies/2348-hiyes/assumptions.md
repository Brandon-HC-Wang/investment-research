## A-2026-005 — Preliminary scenario recognition and margin ranges

- **Description:** For scenario bounding, 2026 recognizes 65% or 90% of the TWD 8.33 billion scheduled project-value upper bound and 2027 recognizes 40% or 70% of the TWD 9.84 billion upper bound. Parent-attributable net margin is 11.5%–12.0% in conservative cases and 18.0% in optimistic cases.
- **Confidence:** 0% for current forecasting.
- **Status:** Rejected.
- **Opened:** 2026-08-04.
- **Causal mechanism:** Project completion and handover determine construction revenue; agency activity and project mix determine gross profit; corporate expenses, finance costs, tax, minorities, preferred dividends, and common shares convert that result to EPS.
- **Supporting evidence:** Management disclosed project totals, participation, and expected completion years; three 2026 projects began handover in June; 2024 and 2025 provide observed high- and lower-margin reference years.
- **Contradicting evidence:** Q1 2026 construction segment profit was only TWD 6 million on TWD 704 million of revenue, and the Q2 filing was not public at the forecast date.
- **Confirmation test:** Q2 and Q3 segment disclosures, cumulative project recognition, gross margin, finance costs, and project-level completion evidence fall within the selected scenario path.
- **Rejection test:** Disclosed participation, recognition policy, project timing, or margins differ materially enough that the consolidated-revenue proxy no longer explains reported earnings.
- **Model effect:** Produces preliminary EPS point estimates of TWD 5.58 and TWD 11.32 for conservative and optimistic 2026 cases, and TWD 4.27 and TWD 10.86 for 2027.
- **Expected window:** 2026 Q2 filing through 2027 Q2.
- **History:** 2026-08-17 — Remains Open but reduced to 20% confidence for current forecasting. Actual H1 EPS was TWD 1.06, parent-attributable net margin was 7.0%, operating cash flow was negative TWD 2.120 billion, and finance costs were TWD 285 million. The dated scenario values are preserved, but the 12%–18% margin proxies are superseded for current use. Reaching the prior TWD 5.58 and TWD 11.32 points would require approximately TWD 4.52 and TWD 10.26 of H2 EPS, respectively.
- **History:** 2026-09-09 — Rejected for current forecasting. The TWD 5.58 and TWD 11.32 points remain historical boundaries, but their consolidated-margin method is replaced by `A-2026-006` and the segment-aware H2 bridge in `04_eps_model.md`.

### A-2026-008 — Preferred allocation and acquisition cash reconciliation

- **Description / claim:** The disclosed preferred reset changes common earnings allocation; the Baoyuan purchase must be funded within a reconciled parent cash budget before a 2027 ordinary payout forecast can be adopted.
- **Status:** Open. **Opened:** 2026-10-13. **Confidence:** 35% for EPS scenarios, 20% for cash capacity; high confidence only in retained announcement terms.
- **Causal mechanism:** Agency/development gross profit less corporate/finance costs, tax and NCI produces parent profit. Preferred claims reduce common profit. Purchase payments and funding calls can consume cash even when project revenue is recognized.
- **Supporting evidence:** Primary reset (4% to 5.666% from 2026-09-29), 30 million preferred shares at TWD 40, H1 EPS deduction 48 million, and company acquisition consideration 704.632 million / 20% interest.
- **Contradicting evidence / uncertainty:** Non-cumulative dividends are discretionary; day weighting and declaration timing remain assumptions. Purchase payments, CFO overlap, loan drawdown, parent remittances and replacement completion dates are undisclosed. Offsetting financing or later payments could invalidate the stresses.
- **Confirmation test:** Q3/future EPS notes and board dividend allocations reconcile to the separate reported-style and earnings-year bridges. Purchase instalments, existing CFO/project calls, financing and reserves reconcile without double counting.
- **Rejection test:** Different declared allocation, redemption/waiver, verified payment outside the window, funding already inside CFO, or material operating-margin/finance/NCI changes require rebuilding rather than preserving the numerical forecast.
- **Expected window:** Q3 2026 filings through 2027 H1.
- **Model effect:** Economic normalized base EPS 4.38 / 3.33 / 3.87 for FY2026–FY2028; reported-style 4.54 / 3.43 / 3.87. No current cash-DPS point/range. Explicit full-payment/no-offset stresses give 0.32 / 0.00 cash capacity, not dividends or floors.
- **History:** 2026-10-13 — Opened; prior model outputs remain dated history. [Evidence and reproducible calculations](model/2026-10-13-september-and-funding-update.md).
