# Research Assumptions

This is the append-only assumption register for Hiyes. Confidence is an analyst estimate of evidential support, not a probability of investment success. Valid statuses are `Open`, `Verified`, and `Rejected`. Never delete an assumption; append dated status or confidence changes.

## A-2026-001 — Recurring agency earnings exist beneath project volatility

- **Description:** Hiyes has a recurring, cash-backed agency earnings base that can be separated from development and investment-project recognition.
- **Confidence:** 40%.
- **Status:** Open.
- **Opened:** 2026-08-04.
- **Evidence needed:** Segment profit, represented sales value, effective fee rate, agency cost base, and cash conversion across multiple years.
- **Related question:** `Q-2026-001` in `07_open_questions.md`.

## A-2026-002 — The 2027 project pipeline may be insufficient

- **Description:** Attributable projects currently visible may not fully replace the project earnings recognized in 2023–2025, creating a potential 2027–2028 EPS gap.
- **Confidence:** 60%.
- **Status:** Open.
- **Opened:** 2026-08-04.
- **Evidence needed:** Attributable agency, development, and investment-project schedules with ownership, completion, margin, and recognition timing.
- **Related question:** `Q-2026-002` in `07_open_questions.md`.
