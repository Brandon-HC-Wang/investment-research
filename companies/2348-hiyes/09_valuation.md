# Valuation

> Current dated supplement: [2026-10-12](model/2026-10-12-dividend-enrichment.md). Older dated sections below remain historical.

## Current status

Valuation is intentionally deferred. Applying P/E to 2021–2025 reported EPS would capitalize an unknown mixture of recurring agency earnings, development recognition, investment income, and possible one-off items. A current multiple would therefore create false precision.

## Intended method

After the earnings reconciliation, the likely primary method is sum-of-the-parts:

- normalized P/E for recurring brokerage earnings;
- project-level net value or probability-weighted earnings for development and investment projects;
- explicit adjustments for net debt, investments, guarantees, minority interests, and dilution.

Consolidated P/E may be used only as a cross-check after normalized EPS is established. P/B may provide balance-sheet context but will not be treated as primary unless book value is shown to represent realizable project economics.

## Required evidence before valuation

The record needs normalized diluted EPS, segment margins, attributable project schedules, net debt and investments, guarantee exposure, diluted shares, and evidence supporting a two-to-five-year replacement path. The valuation will then use downside, base, and upside operating cases rather than a single multiple.
