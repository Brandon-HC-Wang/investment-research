## Reconcile revenue and hedges

H1 operating revenue = service-fee group **7,870.678** + operating interest **2,488.446** + operating dividends **890.370** + net trading bundle **8,083.486** − credit loss **135.166** = **19,197.814**. Service group includes brokerage **6,580.274**, loan fees 11.031, securities lending 504.740, underwriting 102.622, wealth management 112.790, transfer agency 97.590, Hong Kong futures commissions 326.340, manager fees 43.451, advisory 23.729 and other operating revenue 68.111. The two largest positive lines are brokerage and market-related trading; percentages of signed net revenue are composition indicators, not standalone segment margins.

Net trading bundle = securities-sale profits 37,498.217 + securities valuation 1,944.632 − short/borrowed-security cover 4,302.159 − short valuation 5,135.995 + FVOCI debt realization 16.573 + futures-margin securities valuation 736.760 − warrants 3,954.073 − futures derivatives 13,773.328 − OTC derivatives 4,947.141. This is an analytical grouping of published lines. It does not establish the economic hedge effectiveness or the sensitivity to a specific index fall. Ignoring offsetting legs materially overstates profit; removing all FVTPL ignores part of the broker's ordinary business. Cash-flow reconciliation reports combined FVTPL asset/liability net loss adjustment 2,454.603; it is not a complete normalized-EPS adjustment.

## Recurring versus episodic earnings

Operating interest 2,488.446 plus financial income 971.755 gives gross interest/financial income 3,460.201, against financing cost 1,591.341. These require funded balances and spreads; they are not risk-free fees. Operating/nonoperating dividends total 939.581. Associate profit 419.659 includes Capital Investment Trust 387.508 and insurance subsidiary 32.151, under disclosed accounting treatment.

Other P&L includes investment-disposal gain **1,178.667**, versus FY2025 **213.251**, and prior-liability release **43.295**. These are in EPS, unlike FVOCI equity transfers; transaction nature, repeatability and parent allocation remain incompletely disclosed. H1 compensation-estimate release 42.715 is disclosed separately, but is not added as another adjustment because its classification/overlap is unverified. Removing disposal plus liability release at assumed marginal tax 20% and unchanged NCI gives H1 diluted EPS sensitivity **2.83** versus reported **3.23**. This removes selected gains, not all trading cyclicality; it is not company adjusted EPS. No exceptional gain is treated as a recurring terminal source without evidence.
