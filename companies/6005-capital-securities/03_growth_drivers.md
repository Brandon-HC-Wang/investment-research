# Revenue stability, growth drivers and market downturns

2026-10-12. [Business](01_business.md), [financial evidence](02_financials.md), [IR26 / ETF919](sources/README.md).

## Does a stock-market fall reduce revenue?

**Opinion: a prolonged downturn can reduce both revenue and EPS materially; the first crash days need not reduce commissions.** Brokerage depends on traded value rather than index level alone. Panic, rebalancing and hedging can increase initial turnover; a later volume drought, declining loan balances, collateral losses and weaker trading returns can overwhelm that benefit.

| Mechanism | Short sharp drop | Persistent decline / lower activity | Observable test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brokerage | Heavy turnover may support fees | Value/volume and fee yield decline | Market traded value, share, commission yield |
| Financing/lending | Margin calls and borrowing demand shift | Outstanding loans/interest decrease; credit risk rises | Average balances, spreads, collateral and losses |
| Trading/market making | Hedge gains/losses offset; residual exposure can hurt | Lower opportunities or losses; basis/liquidity risk | Net trading after all hedge legs, VaR and capital |
| Associate asset management | Existing funds continue paying fees | NAV, flows and fees can decline | Average AUM, net flows, fund mix and fee rates |
| Underwriting/wealth management | Existing mandates may proceed | New issues and product sales slow | Completed mandates and recurring-fee penetration |

Do not assume a 30% index drop means a 30% company-revenue drop. For a stock ETF with unchanged units, NAV/AUM falling 30% reduces fee revenue about 30%, but flows and product mix alter the result. Bond/cash funds respond to other factors. With 00919's August AUM held constant initially, a full-year 30% AUM decline implies manager gross fee decrease about **523.451 million**; applying unchanged expenses, assumed manager tax 20% and 20% ownership gives an illustrative 6005 EPS effect about **-0.034**. This narrow one-fund sensitivity is not total broker downside or a prediction; it excludes other funds, costs reacting, flows and investor-level tax. The broker's direct trading/volume channels can be much larger.

## Historical stability test

The annual report/IR26 shows pre-2026-bonus basic EPS **2.42 in 2021**, **0.39 in 2022**, **1.90 in 2023**, **2.25 in 2024**, **2.64 in 2025**. The 2021–2022 decline was about **83.9%**; it demonstrates earnings cyclicality, not a controlled attribution solely to the index. In 2026, monthly group net income was 2,402.803 in April, 226.604 in July and 672.566 in September. Their composition and after-tax minority attribution differ, but the range rejects a fixed-fee annuity interpretation. No simple correlation coefficient is claimed from this limited series.

## Growth and replacement map

- 2026: stronger market volume, financing balances and trading profits; nine-month self-reported EPS is already 3.99. Confirmation requires Q3 parent earnings, trading/hedge reconciliation and monthly commissions, not merely headline revenue.
- 2027: retain a larger service/financing franchise, Capital Investment Trust flows and wealth management, while normalizing unusually strong 2026 market and disposal gains. Base service revenue 13,600 and net trading 7,200 are analyst assumptions; exact market-volume/fee-yield data are incomplete.
- 2028–2030: advisory, high-asset clients, recurring wealth fees, ETF/fund assets and digital distribution could diversify income. IR26's AI and wealth plans are management strategy, not signed backlog or independently measured incremental profit.

There is no disclosed binding multi-year revenue backlog. Track share and realized fees, funded balances, associate earnings, asset flows and capital adequacy. The [model](04_eps_model.md) changes operating mechanisms before valuation or payout.

## Hypothesis H-2026-001 — Fees soften, but do not remove, market cyclicality

- Claim/mechanism: service/interest and associate earnings provide a recurring base while direct trading determines much of variance.
- Support: brokerage franchise, funded balances, 20% manager stake and AUM-linked fees.
- Contrary evidence: 2022 earnings decline, 2026 monthly volatility, market-sensitive loan demand and AUM.
- Confirmation: through Q3/FY2026 and FY2027, service/financing income holds up as net trading weakens and capital remains adequate.
- Rejection: volume/loan contraction plus hedge/trading losses overwhelm fee contribution; assumed cash distributions become untenable.
- Status: Open; qualitative confidence 60%, not a scenario probability. Review 2026-11-16 and FY2026 distribution decisions in 2027.
