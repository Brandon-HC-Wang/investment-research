It requires sustainable risk-adjusted returns and adequate capital; the band is an analyst convention without a verified peer/historical dataset. Book is dated H1 and may change materially by Q3. Low P/B alone does not establish safety if trading losses impair book.

## Secondary: normalized scenario P/E

Apply **7–9×** to 2027 base diluted EPS **4.10**, giving an undiscounted earnings reference **28.7–36.9**. Those are assumed multiples, not verified consensus or a future-date price forecast. The P/B and earnings checks describe overlapping assets/earnings and must not be added or mechanically averaged. A 2027 downside EPS 0.45 makes a fixed P/E much less informative; stressed book/capital would need remeasurement. 2026 strong-cycle earnings cannot substitute for normalized duration.
