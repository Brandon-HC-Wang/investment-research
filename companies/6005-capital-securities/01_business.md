# Business and customer value — Capital Securities (6005)

As of 2026-10-12. Capital Securities Corporation / 群益金鼎證券股份有限公司, TWSE. [H126 / AR25 / IR26](sources/README.md).

The company enables securities/futures execution, financing and collateralized borrowing, securities lending, issuance/capital raising, wealth management and hedging/market making. Customers pay commissions, interest spreads and service fees; trading inventories also generate realized and fair-value returns. These are distinct profit mechanisms with different exposure to price, volume, rates and capital.

## Answer: is all revenue ETF management fees?

**Fact: no.** Capital owns **20.00%** of Capital Investment Trust (群益投信), an associate over which it has significant influence but not control. H126 Note 6(6), printed pp.19–20 / PDF pp.24–25, explains that it cannot obtain a majority of board seats or voting rights. The associate's gross fund-management revenue is not consolidated into Capital's operating revenue. Capital recognizes its share of the manager's net profit through equity-method investment income.

H1 2026 recognized share from Capital Investment Trust was **TWD 387.508 million**, about **4.06% of group pretax profit 9,541.633** and **0.158 per diluted share** before any additional investor-level tax effect. This is the entire associate's profit contribution, not a disclosed ETF-only share. Do not assert that 4.06% measures ETF-specific profit. The group also reports manager-fee operating income 43.451; that line does not represent all of the associate's ETF fees.

The company's consolidated brokerage commissions 6,580.274, operating interest 2,488.446 and net trading bundle 8,083.486 are much larger. The financial record reconciles those lines rather than treating securities profits and hedge losses separately. [Financials](02_financials.md).

## Ownership and customer channels

Capital Futures is a controlled listed subsidiary (55.46% shown in IR26), consolidated with noncontrolling interests deducted before parent EPS. Capital Investment Trust's 20.00% share follows the associate route; the two cannot be valued or added to earnings in the same way. The IR26 group diagram also shows wholly owned international holdings, investment management and private-equity/venture-capital entities. A group brand does not imply 100% economic ownership.

**Manager-presented position:** IR26 reports H1 brokerage share 3.48%, rank 10; margin-financing share 5.09%, rank 7; trust AUM TWD 16.889 billion and 396 transfer-agency clients. These are useful signs of franchise breadth, not independent proof of exceptional pricing power. Licenses, risk capital, systems and client relationships are barriers; digital commission discounts, bank-affiliated distribution and market concentration constrain the advantage. No verified broker-consensus forecast is claimed.

## ETF economics and limits

00919's official August 2026 factsheet reports AUM **TWD 581.612 billion** at 2026-08-31 and annual manager fee **0.30%**. Constant-AUM gross fee arithmetic is 581.612 × 0.30% = **1.745 billion/year** at the investment manager. It is not Capital Securities revenue, attributable net profit or actual annual fees: average daily AUM, costs, tax, other funds and ownership must be applied. The factsheet's bond/equity allocation concerns that fund, not the broker's balance sheet.

Fund fees persist while AUM remains, but price declines, redemptions and fee competition can reduce them. ETF distributions to fund holders and dividends to 6005 shareholders are different cash flows. The [brokerage industry framework](../../industries/securities-brokerage/README.md) guides the ownership, hedge and capital distinctions.
