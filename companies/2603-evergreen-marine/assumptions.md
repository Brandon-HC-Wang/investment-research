# Research Assumptions

Append-only register. Valid statuses are `Open`, `Verified`, and `Rejected`.

## A-2026-001

- **Description:** Elevated July-August revenue converts into a Q3 operating margin of about 25% in the base case.
- **Confidence:** 65%.
- **Status:** Open.
- **Opened:** 2026-09-10.
- **Evidence for and against:** Revenue already reached TWD97.06 billion in two months and WCI remained elevated; company-specific rate, liftings, and fuel data are missing.
- **Verification test:** Q3 revenue near TWD146 billion and operating margin 23-27% in the Q3 filing.
- **Model effect:** Every one-point margin difference changes quarterly EPS by about TWD0.5.
- **History:** 2026-09-10 opened at 65%.

## A-2026-002

- **Description:** Freight rates ease gradually rather than normalize abruptly in 2026Q4.
- **Confidence:** 50%.
- **Status:** Open.
- **Opened:** 2026-09-10.
- **Evidence for and against:** WCI was USD4,465/FEU on 2026-09-03 and broad Red Sea risk remained; isolated Suez transit testing and offered-capacity growth argue against permanence.
- **Verification test:** October-December WCI/SCFI and Evergreen monthly revenue remain above the Q1-Q2 run rate.
- **Model effect:** Main driver of the TWD28-45 FY2026 EPS spread.
- **History:** 2026-09-10 opened at 50%.

## A-2026-003

- **Description:** A provisional TWD22-28 EPS range represents mid-cycle earnings better than annualizing 2026H2.
- **Confidence:** 35%.
- **Status:** Open.
- **Opened:** 2026-09-10.
- **Evidence for and against:** It brackets 2023 EPS of TWD16.4 and 2025 EPS of TWD31.68, but lacks a full per-TEU cost and freight-rate cycle model.
- **Verification test:** Build a 2019-2027 liftings, effective-rate, cost-per-TEU, and capacity history.
- **Model effect:** Determines normalized P/E valuation and whether apparent current cheapness is real.
- **History:** 2026-09-10 opened at 35%.

## A-2026-004

- **Description:** Fleet renewal lowers unit cost enough to partially offset depreciation and financing expense.
- **Confidence:** 45%.
- **Status:** Open.
- **Opened:** 2026-09-10.
- **Evidence for and against:** New vessels are larger, younger, and dual-fuel capable; 53 deliveries and USD7.454 billion of unpaid commitments create utilization and capital-cost risk.
- **Verification test:** After delivery, cost per TEU falls while liftings/utilization and ROIC remain stable.
- **Model effect:** Drives sustainable 2027-2030 EPS and the justified P/B multiple.
- **History:** 2026-09-10 opened at 45%.

## 2026-10-12 — Eight-company dividend enrichment

### A-2026-005 — Conditional parent cash and sector EPS bridge

- **Description:** Operating drivers and parent cash conversion support the dated FY2026-FY2028 scenarios and estimated 2027 cash dividend.
- **Status:** Open.
- **Opened:** 2026-10-12.
- **Confidence:** 35% for EPS, 30% for cash capacity.
- **Causal mechanism:** Segment/cycle margins produce common profit; collections/remittances less funding, debt and reserves constrain ordinary cash allocation.
- **Supporting evidence:** Retained parent statements, reported H1 earnings and three-year dividend declarations; [dated EPS/dividend/cash supplement](model/2026-10-12-dividend-enrichment.md).
- **Contradicting evidence:** Subsidiary remittances, vessel funding schedule, freight volume/rate mix and payment-window cash.
- **Evidence test / confirmation:** Q3 2026 earnings and parent cash bridges, commitments and subsequent board allocation reconcile to the modeled path.
- **Rejection signals:** Material margin/collection shortfall, larger funding/reserve calls or materially different board cash/stock choice.
- **Expected window:** Q3 2026 disclosures through 2027 H1.
- **Model effect:** FY2026E normalized diluted EPS 36.65; FY2027E/FY2028E 26.48/26.93. Estimated 2027 ordinary cash DPS 11.27-18.33, point 17.97, downside 0.00.
- **History:** 2026-10-12 — Opened; projections are not verified distributable earnings.
