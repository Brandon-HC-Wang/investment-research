# EPS Model

## Model status

- **As of:** 2026-09-10
- **Currency:** TWD; fiscal year-end 31 December
- **Latest reported period:** 2026Q2; July-August revenue is reported
- **Forecast:** 2026Q3, Q4, and FY2026 basic EPS
- **Share count:** 2.165 billion weighted-average shares; no material dilution assumed

## Model logic

Monthly consolidated revenue is used as the observable bridge from freight rates and liftings. Operating margin is scenario-based because the company does not disclose monthly effective freight rate, volume, or contract/spot mix. Below-the-line income includes interest and equity-method profit net of finance cost and FX; a 20% effective tax rate and modest non-controlling interest are applied.

The two assumptions responsible for most of the spread are Q4 revenue, which captures the duration of elevated rates, and H2 operating margin, which captures contract/spot mix and operating leverage. The base case is close to the externally visible 2026 consensus of roughly TWD36 EPS, but is independently constructed from monthly revenue and reported margins.

## EPS bridge and normalization

2026H1 basic EPS is reported at TWD11.24. The model adds H2 parent earnings after estimated non-operating income, tax, and minorities. FVTPL is not normalized because its 2026H1 EPS contribution was below TWD0.001. Interest income and equity-method earnings are retained as recurring below-the-line items, while the freight-rate uplift remains classified as recurring cyclical rather than sustainable core earnings.

For valuation, sustainable mid-cycle EPS is provisionally set at TWD22-28. This range is below the 2026 base case and brackets the 2023 reported EPS of TWD16.4 and 2025 EPS of TWD31.68. It remains low confidence because a full per-TEU cost and rate history has not yet been assembled.

## Sensitivities

At around TWD135 billion quarterly revenue and 2.165 billion shares, each one percentage point of operating margin changes pre-tax operating profit by about TWD1.35 billion and after-tax EPS by about TWD0.50, before minorities. A TWD10 billion change in quarterly revenue changes EPS by roughly TWD0.8-1.0 at a 22-27% incremental margin and 20% tax rate.

## Model limitations

- Monthly revenue does not disclose volume, effective rate, route mix, bunker surcharge, or contract/spot mix.
- Q4 rates are especially sensitive to geopolitical events and effective capacity released by any Suez normalization.
- Newbuild delivery, depreciation, charter returns, and fuel costs are not modeled vessel by vessel.
- Consensus is a secondary cross-check, not a model input.
