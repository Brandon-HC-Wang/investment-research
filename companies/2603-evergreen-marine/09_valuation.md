# Valuation

Valuation follows the operating thesis and is not a recommendation.

## Context

At the 2026-09-09 TWSE close of TWD234, Evergreen traded at 0.90 times 2026Q2 book value per share of TWD259.09. The reported TWSE trailing P/E was 9.28 and dividend yield was 6.84%. The 2.165 billion share count, non-controlling interest, lease liabilities, and newbuilding commitments are included in the interpretation below.

## Normalized earnings basis

The [EPS model](04_eps_model.md) estimates FY2026 basic EPS at TWD28.1 / 36.8 / 44.7 in downside / base / upside scenarios. These are cyclical earnings and should not be capitalized as permanent. Provisional mid-cycle EPS is TWD22-28, with low confidence.

At TWD234, the stock is 6.4 times base-case 2026 EPS but 8.4-10.6 times provisional mid-cycle EPS. It is optically inexpensive on current earnings, but not clearly inexpensive on normalized earnings.

## Method selection

Price-to-book is the primary cross-cycle anchor because vessel assets, cash, debt, leases, and retained earnings are material, while annual EPS is highly rate-sensitive. A normalized P/E is used as a cross-check. Gross cash is not added separately because substantial cash is economically committed to newbuildings and working capital; adding it again would overstate value.

## Scenario valuation context

| Scenario | Operating condition | P/B range | Implied value (TWD/share) | Normalized P/E cross-check |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Downside | rates normalize quickly; new supply pressures returns | 0.65-0.75x | 168-194 | 7x × TWD22 = 154 |
| Base | gradual rate easing; balance sheet absorbs capex | 0.85-1.00x | 220-259 | 8-9x × TWD25-28 = 200-252 |
| Upside | disruption-supported rates persist and fleet earns adequate returns | 1.05-1.15x | 272-298 | 9x × TWD28 = 252, plus evidence of higher sustainable EPS required |

These are scenario implications, not price targets. Book value is held constant for simplicity; actual 2026 earnings, dividends, and OCI will change it.

## Dividend context

If FY2026 EPS is TWD36.8 and the payout ratio is 45-55%, an illustrative cash dividend is TWD16.6-20.2 per share, equivalent to 7.1-8.6% on TWD234. This is an estimate, not management guidance. The 53-vessel program and rate cycle can cause the board to retain more cash.

## Limitations and history

Vessel market values, maintenance capex, and route-level economics are incomplete. P/B can overstate value if vessel earnings fall below the cost of capital, while P/E can understate risk near a rate peak.
