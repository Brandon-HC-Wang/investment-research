# Novatek — Growth Drivers

## Evidence before extrapolation — 2026-11-05

**Fact:** Q2 SoC product sales rose 53.09% YoY to TWD14.706bn, while DDIC fell 14.83% to TWD13.990bn. SoC's increase TWD5.100bn exceeded DDIC's decline TWD2.436bn. Total product sales TWD28.696bn are before other revenue/returns; consolidated net sales were TWD28.660bn. Q3 product split and product-level gross margins have not been verified as of this cutoff. [S4 p. 3; S6 p. 2]

**Hypothesis H-2026-001:** Higher-value display and vision solutions can offset DDIC pricing erosion and sustain operating earnings through FY2028. Mechanism: qualification/IP → content and mix → gross profit above R&D needs. Support: Q2 SoC growth and Q3 operating-profit growth; contradiction: Q2 DDIC decline, Q3 margin decline and inventory accumulation. Confirm with two consecutive quarters of improving cash conversion, repeat platform shipments and GM around/above 40%; reject if inventory days remain above 100 and GM below 38% for two quarters without a documented temporary cause. Window: Q4 2026–FY2028. Status Open; confidence 55%. Thresholds are analyst monitoring rules, not management commitments. [A-2026-001; Q-2026-001/003]

**Hypothesis H-2026-002:** Q3 inventory primarily supports subsequent sell-through rather than excess stock. WIP dominance is consistent with pipeline build but does not prove firm demand. Rising finished goods and weak Q3 cash conversion contradict automatic optimism. Confirm by Q1 2027 inventory days falling below 80 with improving cash flow and no material charge; reject by persistent >100 days, customer cancellations or materially higher write-downs. Status Open; confidence 40%. [S2 pp. 9, 15–16; A-2026-002]

## Capacity, customers and order quality

Novatek is fabless; its own fab utilization is not the correct metric. Track foundry wafer allocations, cost/node mix, outsourced lead times, test capacity and customer inventory. Customer capacity-guarantee contracts and TWD11.306bn deposits provide some commitment evidence, but deposits are returned when contract conditions are met; minimum purchases and linkage to current inventory are unverified. Neither available wafer capacity nor a chip announcement proves backlog. The financial statements say remaining customer contract obligations are shorter than one year; they do not provide a multi-year executable backlog. Customer A is material; unnamed customers cannot be assigned to a brand. [S1 pp. 83, 99–100; S2 pp. 22, 26]
