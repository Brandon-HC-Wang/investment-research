# Novatek (3034) — Business

Information cutoff: 2026-11-05. Currency: TWD. Initial coverage, qualifying.

## Customer value and economic engine

**Fact:** Novatek designs display-driver ICs (DDIC) and system-on-chips (SoC), outsources wafer fabrication and packaging, and retains some testing capability. Customers are panel manufacturers, system brands and distributors. DDIC translates image signals into panel control; SoC integrates image processing, display control and related software. Customers pay for chips and associated solutions, predominantly when control transfers, rather than through a subscription. [S1 pp. 81–85, 98–100; S2 pp. 25–26]

| Product | Customer value | Revenue drivers | Economic constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| DDIC: LCD/OLED, touch integration, automotive | Display quality, power efficiency, panel compatibility and fewer external components | Device units × design share × chips/content per device × ASP × FX | Mature LCD price pressure; integration can reduce chip count even while increasing value per chip |
| SoC: TV/scaler, imaging and vision | Integrated image processing, firmware and customer engineering support | Customer platforms × shipments × share × price/mix × FX | Expensive development, software support, advanced-node wafer and IP costs |

**Fact:** FY2025 disclosed mix was DDIC 62.87%, SoC 36.47%, other 0.66%. Q2 2026 product sales were SoC TWD14.706bn and DDIC TWD13.990bn before other revenue/returns, approximately 51%/49%. This is a meaningful mix change, not proof that all SoC growth is AI. [S1 p. 81; S4 p. 3]

## Competitive advantage and durability

**Interpretation:** The strongest advantages are accumulated display/image IP, customer qualification and firmware integration, broad product coverage and purchasing scale. These can reduce customer development time and support repeat design wins; they require continuing R&D, not passive ownership of a franchise. FY2025 R&D was TWD16.725bn, 16.62% of sales. [S1 pp. 85–86, 94–95]

**Company-attributed evidence:** The annual report cites OMDIA's 1–3Q2025 DDIC revenue share of 21.2%, second globally. This is historical DDIC share, not independently verified current share or share of the entire SoC market. Relevant DDIC competitors include Samsung, LX Semi, Himax, Raydium, Fitipower, FocalTech, Sitronix, ChipOne and Will Semi. SoC competition includes platform and software economics; direct product-level comparisons still need work. [S1 pp. 90–91]

**Limits:** Mature DDIC is exposed to Chinese localization and pricing; qualification does not prevent replacement in the next platform. FY2025 largest disclosed customer A represented 20.71% of sales; its identity and ultimate brand exposure are undisclosed. UMC represented 29.90% of purchases, with unnamed supplier A another 22.07%. Do not infer their identities. [S1 pp. 85, 99–100]

## AI and technology exposure

**Management disclosures, not earnings forecasts:** OLED notebook/foldable-phone products, edge vision SoC, automotive display and a 4nm test-chip/ASIC roadmap provide potential replacement drivers. The annual report differentiates 6nm mass production from a 4nm test chip; a launch or test chip does not establish material revenue, margin or backlog. Exact AI revenue, named platforms, content per system and incremental profit remain unverified. [S1 pp. 6, 85–88; Q-2026-003]
