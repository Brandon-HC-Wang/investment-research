## Q3 bridge: stronger operations, lower reported EPS

**Calculated:** Revenue rose 8.06% QoQ and 26.03% YoY. Gross profit increased TWD0.509bn despite gross margin declining 1.53pp. Opex fell TWD0.110bn, so operating profit rose TWD0.619bn, or 9.57%. Non-operating income fell TWD1.036bn; tax expense fell TWD0.166bn. Net income therefore fell TWD0.251bn, or 3.97%. The exact reported Q3 operating margin rounds to 22.87%, not 22.88%. [S2/S3 p. 7]

Q2 had TWD0.860bn of fair-value financial-asset gains; Q3 had a TWD0.036bn loss. This explains most of the change in non-operating income. FX gains were only TWD0.028bn and TWD0.040bn, respectively. Do not describe the whole EPS decline as an FX loss or weakening operating profit. [S2 p. 31; S3 p. 28]

**Calculated:** Inventory rose 35.64% QoQ and 108.95% from year-end, versus Q3 revenue +8.06% QoQ. At September, WIP was TWD10.927bn and finished goods TWD4.922bn. Ending-inventory days using actual quarterly COGS and calendar days rose from 73.4 in Q2 to 90.7 in Q3; Q3 average-inventory days were 78.8. These are analyst calculations, not company guidance or a direct measurement of customer/channel inventory. [S2 pp. 5, 15–16; S3 pp. 5, 7]

9M operating cash flow was TWD12.929bn versus TWD16.172bn net income (79.9% conversion). Subtracting H1 operating cash flow of TWD11.826bn gives Q3 cash flow of only TWD1.103bn, 18.2% of Q3 net income. Q3 inventory absorbed TWD4.744bn; income tax paid increased to TWD2.059bn, versus H1 TWD0.461bn. Both working capital and tax timing matter. Cash declined after TWD13.996bn dividends paid in Q3, so the cash reduction alone does not establish operating distress. [S2/S3 p. 9]

9M FCF proxy = operating cash flow − cash purchases of PPE − intangibles − prepaid equipment = 12.929 − 0.930 − 0.338 − 0.064 = **TWD11.598bn**, before leases/acquisitions/investment flows. It does not cover TWD13.996bn cash dividends during the same nine months; this is a period comparison, not proof of full-year dividend unsustainability.

## Earnings quality, funding and dilution

9M financial-asset P&L gains were TWD0.842bn and FX gains TWD0.152bn. Ordinary chip profits are recurring but cyclical; investment remeasurement and FX should not be capitalized at the same confidence. An illustrative removal of these gains with an assumed 20% marginal tax effect gives diluted EPS about **25.24**, versus reported 26.55. Actual tax attribution is unavailable; this is a sensitivity, not an audited adjusted EPS. Separately, TWD5.180bn equity-investment gains were in OCI and **never entered EPS**. [S2 pp. 7, 31–34]

9M inventory write-downs TWD0.670bn remain in operating costs; no automatic add-back. Cash TWD40.556bn, lease liabilities TWD0.549bn and no bank/bond borrowings shown in the balance sheet support liquidity, but customer capacity deposits TWD11.306bn (included in TWD11.316bn other financial liabilities: current 2.342bn and non-current 8.974bn) and signed future capital commitments TWD4.668bn constrain treating all cash as distributable surplus. [S2 pp. 6, 16, 22, 35]

Issued shares increased from 608.512m to 611.512m after 3m restricted employee shares on 2026-09-24. Q3 diluted weighted-average shares were 609.056m; 9M were 609.186m. The model uses 612m prospectively, with ordinary stock compensation retained as expense. [S2 pp. 24–25, 34]

**Assurance limit:** EY's Q3 review has a qualified conclusion because specified subsidiaries were not reviewed (4.15% of consolidated assets and liabilities). This is a stated review-scope limitation, not a finding of misstatement. [S2 pp. 3–4]
