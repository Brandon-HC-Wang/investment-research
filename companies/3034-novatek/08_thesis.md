# Novatek — Thesis

As of 2026-11-05; **qualifying**, medium confidence in the operating baseline, low confidence in forecasts. Start with [business](01_business.md), [financials](02_financials.md) and [EPS model](04_eps_model.md). Full source trail: [register](sources/README.md).

## Current judgment

**Opinion:** Novatek is an established display/image semiconductor designer with qualification/IP advantages and substantial cash, but current evidence supports a cyclical operating recovery more strongly than a demonstrated 2–5 year earnings compounder. The key test is whether SoC/OLED content gains create repeat gross profit and cash after continued R&D, while mature DDIC pricing remains competitive.

**Facts:** Q3 sales TWD30.971bn, GM 40.89%, operating profit TWD7.085bn; basic/diluted EPS 9.99/9.98. Operating profit grew 9.57% QoQ; EPS fell because investment-related non-operating income declined. Inventory rose 35.64% QoQ and Q3 operating cash conversion was 18.2%. [S2/S3]

**Hypothesis, not fact:** Platform integration, OLED adoption and edge vision can replace mature LCD pressure and lift sustainable EPS into FY2028. Roadmaps and Q2 SoC growth support testing this claim; Q2 DDIC weakness and Q3 inventory build prevent verification. H-2026-001/002 and A-2026-001–005 remain Open.

## Causal cases

- **Downside:** Price/localization pressure plus inventory digestion lowers sales and GM while R&D stays sticky. Conditional diluted EPS FY2027/FY2028 about 19.5/14.0.
- **Base sensitivity:** Modest platform/content gains and 40% GM cover continued R&D; EPS about 33.5/36.1. This is a calculation reference, not a validated central forecast.
- **Upside:** Repeated multi-customer SoC/OLED ramps support 43% GM; EPS about 46.0/54.1. Requires revenue, margin and cash evidence absent today.

No scenario probabilities assigned. FY2026 reported-style conditional EPS 31.8–37.6 retains actual 9M investment gains; it is not comparable to normalized projections without the bridge in [04_eps_model.md](04_eps_model.md).

## The two largest EPS risks

1. **DDIC price/share erosion and disappointing high-value mix:** Lower ASP and GM across a large revenue base can exceed incremental SoC gross profit. At central modeled FY2027 sales, each 1pp GM costs roughly TWD1.60 EPS. Monitor product sales, customer design wins, ASP and wafer costs. [Q-2026-002/005]
2. **Inventory ahead of demand plus fixed R&D:** Slow sell-through locks cash and can force price concessions/write-downs; TWD1bn additional charge costs about TWD1.34 EPS under an 18% tax assumption. Monitor WIP/finished goods, inventory days and cash conversion, not monthly revenue alone. [Q-2026-001]

## Confirmation and falsification

Analyst monitoring rules: confirmation requires repeated platform shipments, GM around/above 40%, inventory days returning below 80 and improving cash conversion over the next two reported quarters. Reassess the thesis if GM is below 38% and inventory days above 100 for two quarters without an evidenced temporary cause, or if design wins fail to produce revenue by FY2028. These are internal tests, not disclosed company targets.

**Variant perception:** No verified consensus dataset was obtained. The potentially useful distinction is operating recovery versus temporary investment EPS and unquantified AI narrative; no claim is made that the market has mispriced it.

## Capital allocation and valuation implication

FY2025 dividend TWD23/share was approved and paid during 2026; future distributions are undecided. Cash is meaningful but is partly needed for operations, customer deposits and committed capex. Use normalized P/E, with cash/FCF as checks, after proving recurring earnings; avoid automatically adding full cash to an earnings value that includes interest. At the last verified TWD533 close (2026-11-04), reported trailing basic EPS implies about 16.3×; this does not demonstrate cheapness. [09_valuation.md](09_valuation.md)
