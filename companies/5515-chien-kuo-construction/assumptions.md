# Research Assumptions

This register is append-only. Valid statuses are `Open`, `Verified`, and `Rejected`.

## A-2026-001 — Open

- **Description:** 2026 full-year construction gross margin remains at least 17%.
- **Confidence:** 55%
- **Opened:** 2026-09-09
- **Evidence for:** 2025 margin was 16.6% and both 2026 H1 quarters were near 19.4%.
- **Evidence against or missing:** No project-type margin bridge or estimate-revision disclosure is available.
- **Verification test:** Q3 and Q4 results keep full-year margin at or above 17% without material provision reversals.
- **Model effect:** Each 1-point margin change affects EPS by about TWD 0.32 at TWD 8 billion revenue.
- **History:** 2026-09-09 — Opened at 55% confidence.

## A-2026-002 — Open

- **Description:** 2026 revenue reaches TWD 7.6–8.5 billion through backlog execution.
- **Confidence:** 75%
- **Opened:** 2026-09-09
- **Evidence for:** January–August revenue was TWD 5.355 billion and remaining obligations were TWD 46.52 billion. The downside and base cases require September–December monthly revenue of approximately TWD 561 million and TWD 686 million, respectively, versus the January–August average of TWD 669 million.
- **Evidence against or missing:** August revenue declined 4.4% year over year, monthly progress can be delayed, and monthly revenue does not reveal margin or project mix.
- **Verification test:** September–December cumulative revenue supports at least the lower bound without one-off non-construction revenue; the Q3 filing confirms that recognition did not require an unacceptable margin sacrifice.
- **Model effect:** Sets 2026 operating profit and fixed-cost absorption.
- **History:** 2026-09-09 — Opened at 70% confidence. 2026-09-10 — Raised to 75% after January–August revenue reached TWD 5.355 billion; kept `Open` because August slowed and gross margin remains unobserved.

## A-2026-003 — Open

- **Description:** New awards will move book-to-bill back toward at least 1.0 over a rolling twelve-month period.
- **Confidence:** 35%
- **Opened:** 2026-09-09
- **Evidence for:** Multiple public and industrial project starts were announced after April.
- **Evidence against or missing:** January–April awards of TWD 1.8 billion were below same-period revenue of about TWD 2.65 billion.
- **Verification test:** A complete quarterly backlog bridge shows rolling awards at least equal to revenue by 2027 H1.
- **Model effect:** Determines post-2027 revenue visibility and terminal multiple.
- **History:** 2026-09-09 — Opened at 35% confidence.

## A-2026-004 — Open

- **Description:** H2 2026 FVTPL gains or losses remain within plus or minus TWD 200 million.
- **Confidence:** 40%
- **Opened:** 2026-09-09
- **Evidence for:** The range is an explicit scenario device around a mixed portfolio, not a historical claim.
- **Evidence against or missing:** H1 gain was TWD 724 million and asset-level return attribution is unavailable.
- **Verification test:** Q3 and Q4 filing disclosures establish the actual change and portfolio composition.
- **Model effect:** Produces about plus or minus TWD 0.8 of reported EPS under a 20% tax assumption.
- **History:** 2026-09-09 — Opened at 30% confidence. 2026-09-09 — Raised to 40% after a Q3-to-date market check estimated a TWD 74 million base loss and a negative TWD 169 million to positive TWD 21 million range; kept Open because most holdings and post-June trades cannot be directly verified.
