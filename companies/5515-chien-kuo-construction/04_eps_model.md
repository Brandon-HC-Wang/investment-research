The reported-EPS ranges are not forecasts of markets. They illustrate that an apparently precise company EPS forecast is false precision while a large FVTPL portfolio is marked through income. A larger market move can push reported EPS outside these ranges.

## 2026-09-09 Q3-to-date FVTPL mark

Holding 2026-06-30 units constant, directly observable prices and NAVs imply a TWD 79.2 million pretax loss across AuthenX, `恆勁科技`, `泰宗`, `安聯四季雙收入息`, `瀚亞高科技`, `野村高科技`, and `富邦新台商`. A 0.30% proxy return on the TWD 1.662 billion named Taiwan money-market pool adds approximately TWD 5.0 million, producing a base Q3-to-date FVTPL loss of TWD 74 million. At a simplifying 20% tax rate and 201.6 million shares, the estimated EPS effect is negative TWD 0.29.

TWD 3.173 billion of the FVTPL pool cannot be repriced reliably from public information. Applying plus or minus 3% to that bucket produces a current FVTPL-value range of TWD 5.28–5.47 billion and an estimated Q3-to-date EPS effect of negative TWD 0.67 to positive TWD 0.08. This is a market-movement estimate, not a quarter-end forecast: post-June trades, distributions, foreign exchange, valuation changes, and the remaining three weeks of Q3 are unknown. The full-year scenario table is therefore not revised yet.

## Revenue and margin logic

January–August revenue was TWD 5.355 billion, including August revenue of TWD 638.0 million (-8.6% month over month and -4.4% year over year). To reach the unchanged TWD 7.6 billion downside, TWD 8.1 billion base, and TWD 8.5 billion upside revenue cases, September–December must average approximately TWD 561 million, TWD 686 million, and TWD 786 million per month, respectively. The base requirement is close to the January–August average of TWD 669 million and the July–August average of TWD 668 million, so the new data do not require a scenario revision. The downside case assumes progress slows and gross margin falls toward the 2025 level. The upside case still requires both stronger progress and sustained high-teens gross margin; backlog alone is insufficient.

## Normalization bridge

2026 H1 reported pretax profit of TWD 1.407 billion is reduced by TWD 724 million FVTPL gains, TWD 111 million land-disposal gain, and TWD 16.6 million related compensation income. The remaining indicative pretax profit is TWD 555 million. At a standard 20% tax assumption, normalized H1 EPS is about TWD 2.20 versus reported TWD 6.08.

For clarity, removing **only** the FVTPL gain from reported H1 net income gives an indicative EPS of about TWD 3.20: `TWD 6.08 - [TWD 724.1 million × (1 - 20%) ÷ 201.6 million shares]`. This is not the preferred normalized measure because it still includes the land-disposal and compensation gains. Removing those items as well and re-estimating tax produces the TWD 2.20 normalized H1 EPS used in the model.

FVTPL exposure is economically recurring because the assets remain on the balance sheet, but the quarterly gain is not a sustainable operating run rate. It is therefore excluded from normalized EPS and shown separately in reported-EPS sensitivity.

## Sensitivities

- Every 1 percentage-point gross-margin change on TWD 8.0 billion revenue changes estimated after-tax EPS by about TWD 0.32.
- Every TWD 100 million pretax portfolio gain or loss changes estimated after-tax EPS by about TWD 0.40.
- Every TWD 500 million of revenue at an 18% gross margin, with no incremental overhead, contributes at most about TWD 0.36 EPS after 20% tax; actual contribution is lower if overhead rises.

## Model limitations

The company does not disclose margin by project category, contract-level cost revisions, backlog cancellation terms, or a clean risk split within the FVTPL portfolio. The tax rate on normalized and investment items differs from the simplifying 20% sensitivity assumption. Monthly revenue confirms recognition volume but does not establish project mix, margin, cash conversion, or future award replacement.
