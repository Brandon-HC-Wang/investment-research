# Investment Thesis

This is a research thesis, not a buy, sell, or hold recommendation.

## Current thesis

Chien Kuo has unusually strong contracted activity visibility and materially improved construction margins, but sustainable per-share earnings are much lower than headline 2026 EPS. The constructive case requires conversion of TWD 46.52 billion remaining obligations at mid-to-high-teen gross margins and continued award replenishment. The candidate advantages are execution history, project-type qualifications, and customer diversification; no evidence yet proves structurally superior returns. Treasury investments provide substantial asset backing and earnings, but also make reported EPS volatile and reduce transparency.

## Variant perception

No reliable consensus model is recorded. A plausible analytical error is to capitalize 2026 H1 EPS of TWD 6.08 as if it were construction earnings. The evidence-based distinction is reported 2026 EPS of roughly TWD 7.6–8.1 in the base case versus normalized EPS of TWD 3.8–4.2.

## Evidence for and against

**Supporting evidence**

- Remaining performance obligations of TWD 46.52 billion were 6.6 times 2025 revenue.
- January–July revenue grew 28.1% year over year.
- Gross margin rose to 19.4% in 2026 H1 and operating cash inflow was TWD 700 million.
- Low reported borrowing and a large investment pool provide balance-sheet flexibility.

**Contradicting or missing evidence**

- TWD 852 million of fair-value, disposal, and compensation gains inflated H1 profit.
- Only TWD 1.8 billion of new awards were reported for January–April, below revenue recognized in that window.
- No gross-margin bridge by project type or contract cohort is disclosed.
- Financial assets, customer advances, guarantees, and working-capital needs make excess-cash value uncertain.

## Scenario thesis

- **Downside:** progress slows, gross margin returns near 16%, and H2 portfolio marks lose TWD 200 million; reported EPS is approximately TWD 6.2–6.7 and normalized EPS TWD 2.8–3.3.
- **Base:** revenue reaches about TWD 8.1 billion, gross margin normalizes to 18.5%, and H2 portfolio marks are flat; reported EPS is TWD 7.6–8.1 and normalized EPS TWD 3.8–4.2.
- **Upside:** revenue reaches about TWD 8.5 billion, high-teens margin persists, and H2 portfolio marks add TWD 200 million; reported EPS is TWD 8.8–9.4 and normalized EPS TWD 4.5–5.0.

## Principal risks and falsification

The two largest EPS risks are construction-margin normalization and investment-market losses. The thesis strengthens if Q3/Q4 gross margin remains at least 17%, awards restore book-to-bill toward or above 1.0, and operating cash flow remains positive without abnormal advances. It weakens if backlog falls without replacement or contract assets and receivables rise faster than revenue. It is falsified as currently framed if high margins reverse through repeated project losses or the portfolio generates losses large enough to overwhelm core profit (`Q-2026-001` to `Q-2026-003`).

## Capital allocation judgment

Mixed and unresolved. The company has low debt, paid TWD 3.30 per share from 2025 earnings, and retains substantial liquidity. FVTPL profit can enter distributable retained earnings after required reserves, but accounting capacity is not parent-company cash and does not prove the assets are excess to project and bonding needs. The investment portfolio generated a larger H1 profit contribution than construction and lacks disclosed return attribution or risk limits. It cannot yet be treated as either a durable advantage or fully distributable excess capital.
