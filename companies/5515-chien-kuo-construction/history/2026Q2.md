## Business and earnings state

Chien Kuo is primarily a contract constructor with residential, public, industrial, and office projects. At 2026-06-30, remaining performance obligations were TWD 46.52 billion, or 6.6 times 2025 revenue. 2026 H1 revenue was TWD 4.02 billion, gross margin 19.4%, operating profit TWD 505.0 million, net income TWD 1.23 billion, and basic EPS TWD 6.08.

Reported earnings are not sustainable at the H1 rate. TWD 724 million of FVTPL gains and TWD 128 million of land-disposal and compensation gains supplied approximately 61% of pretax profit. Indicative normalized H1 EPS is about TWD 2.20 after removing these items and applying a 20% tax assumption.

## Current scenarios

- **Downside:** normalized EPS TWD 2.8–3.3; reported EPS TWD 6.2–6.7.
- **Base:** normalized EPS TWD 3.8–4.2; reported EPS TWD 7.6–8.1.
- **Upside:** normalized EPS TWD 4.5–5.0; reported EPS TWD 8.8–9.4.

The reported ranges include illustrative H2 FVTPL changes of negative TWD 200 million, zero, and positive TWD 200 million. They are not market forecasts.

## Thesis and next action

Backlog supports activity visibility and construction margin has improved, but advantage durability, award replacement, high-teens margin, and portfolio risk remain unproven. Next action: reconcile Q3 contract mix, awards, margins, portfolio marks, and cash conversion.
