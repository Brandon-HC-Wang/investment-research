# 5515 Chien Kuo Construction — August 2026 Revenue Update

- **Snapshot date:** 2026-09-10
- **Information cutoff:** 2026-09-10; financial statements through 2026 Q2 and monthly revenue through 2026-08

## New evidence

August revenue was TWD 638.006 million, down 8.58% from July and 4.37% from TWD 667.219 million one year earlier as displayed by MOPS. January–August revenue was TWD 5,355.291 million, up 23.08% from TWD 4,350.935 million in the comparable period.

## Model implication

The 2026 revenue cases remain TWD 7.6 billion, TWD 8.1 billion, and TWD 8.5 billion. September–December must average approximately TWD 561 million, TWD 686 million, and TWD 786 million per month to reach those cases. The base requirement is close to the January–August average of TWD 669 million and the July–August average of TWD 668 million.

The normalized EPS cases remain TWD 2.8–3.3, TWD 3.8–4.2, and TWD 4.5–5.0. The reported EPS cases remain TWD 6.2–6.7, TWD 7.6–8.1, and TWD 8.8–9.4 because August revenue does not reveal gross margin and does not reduce the uncertainty around H2 FVTPL gains or losses.

## Thesis implication and next test

Backlog conversion remains supported, but the decline in August and the reduction in cumulative growth from 28.1% through July to 23.1% through August show that monthly recognition is uneven. The next revenue check compares September with the scenario run rates; the more important evidence gate is the Q3 filing for gross margin, award replacement, working capital, and FVTPL changes.
