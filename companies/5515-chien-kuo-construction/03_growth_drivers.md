# Growth Drivers

## Driver map

| Driver | Mechanism | Indicator | Margin effect | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Existing backlog conversion | Construction progress becomes revenue | Remaining obligations and monthly revenue | Depends on project mix and cost control | 80% |
| Replacement awards | New contracts replenish consumed backlog | Awards and book-to-bill | Tender discipline determines future margin | 35% |
| Residential/public/industrial mix | Project mix changes pricing and execution risk | Contract-value and revenue mix | Potentially material | 40% |
| Treasury portfolio | Market returns enter non-operating profit | FVTPL balance and quarterly mark | No construction-margin effect; high reported-EPS effect | 90% |

## Current drivers

At 2026-06-30, unsatisfied performance obligations were TWD 46.52 billion, scheduled for recognition from 2026 through 2032. This equals 6.6 times 2025 revenue and supports multi-year activity visibility. July 2026 revenue was TWD 697.9 million (+30.8% year over year), followed by August revenue of TWD 638.0 million (-4.4%). January–August revenue reached TWD 5.355 billion (+23.1%). The cumulative result confirms continued execution, while the August decline shows that monthly progress is uneven; neither result establishes the margin or cancellation quality of the backlog.

Gross margin rose from 9.3% in 2023 to 16.6% in 2025 and 19.4% in 2026 H1. The causal split among contract repricing, favorable mix, provision reversals, productivity, and project milestones is not disclosed. Sustainable EPS therefore depends more on margin normalization than on the existence of backlog alone.

## Future and replacement drivers

The May presentation reported only TWD 1.8 billion of new awards during January–April 2026. Compared with TWD 2.65 billion of revenue recognized in the same period, that partial-period book-to-bill was roughly 0.68 times. Later starts in social housing, industrial development, and Ministry of Economic Affairs projects may improve replacement, but their contract additions and attributable values have not been reconciled. The next evidence gate is the Q3 backlog and award schedule.

## Two-to-five-year visibility

- **2026–2027:** high activity visibility from current obligations; medium earnings visibility because margin by contract is unavailable.
- **2028–2030:** medium revenue visibility because the obligations extend over several years; low margin visibility and replacement-award visibility.
- **2031–2032:** contractual schedule exists, but timing, cancellation, modifications, and replacement economics are too uncertain for a point forecast.

## EPS-relevant risks

1. **Margin normalization or project loss:** a 1 percentage-point change in full-year gross margin on TWD 8 billion revenue changes pretax profit by about TWD 80 million, or approximately TWD 0.32 per share after an assumed 20% tax rate.
2. **Investment-market volatility:** each TWD 100 million pretax FVTPL change alters after-tax EPS by approximately TWD 0.40 under the same simplifying tax assumption. Actual tax can differ.

## Revision history

- **2026-09-09:** Initial driver record. Backlog conversion is supported; replacement awards and gross-margin durability remain open.
- **2026-09-10:** Added August revenue. Backlog conversion remains supported, but year-to-date growth slowed from 28.1% through July to 23.1% through August; no margin inference was made.
