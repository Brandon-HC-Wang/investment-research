# Source Library

- `earnings/`: audited and interim financial reports.
- `conference_calls/`: investor presentations and event material.
- `monthly_revenue/`: exchange monthly-revenue disclosures and reconciliations.
- `market_data/`: dated market-price, NAV, and mark-to-market estimates.
- `news/`: decision-relevant company and secondary reports.

Conclusions remain in the numbered canonical files. Retained PDFs preserve primary evidence that may later move or disappear online.
