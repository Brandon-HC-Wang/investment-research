# Valuation

## Valuation context

As of 2026-09-09, the Taiwan Stock Exchange reported a closing share price of TWD 52.4. With 201.6 million shares, market capitalization was about TWD 10.6 billion. Book equity at 2026-06-30 was TWD 5.85 billion, or about TWD 29.0 per share, implying approximately 1.8 times book value.

## Normalized earnings basis

The primary earnings basis is 2026 normalized EPS, not reported EPS. The base range is TWD 3.8–4.2 after removing fair-value and land-disposal gains. At TWD 52.4, this is about 12.5–13.8 times normalized EPS. Using base reported EPS of TWD 7.6–8.1 would show only 6.5–6.9 times, but that multiple capitalizes realized 2026 market and disposal gains and is not a sustainable operating comparison.

## Method selection

Normalized P/E is the primary method for contract construction, cross-checked with price-to-book and an asset/liquidity review. A mechanical enterprise value is misleading: gross financial assets are large, but customer advances, guarantees, working-capital needs, and portfolio income are integral to the current earnings and risk profile. Adding the investment portfolio to a P/E value would double count investment income unless the assets and related earnings are separated consistently.

## Scenario valuation framework

| Case | Normalized EPS | Illustrative normalized P/E | Earnings value range |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Downside | 2.8–3.3 | 8–10x | TWD 22–33 |
| Base | 3.8–4.2 | 10–13x | TWD 38–55 |
| Upside | 4.5–5.0 | 12–14x | TWD 54–70 |

These are scenario implications, not price targets or recommendations. The upper multiple requires sustained margin, book-to-bill near or above 1.0, and disciplined portfolio risk; the lower multiple reflects margin mean reversion and weak replenishment.

## Sensitivity and limitations

A 1-point gross-margin change alters normalized EPS by about TWD 0.32 at TWD 8 billion revenue. A TWD 100 million pretax portfolio mark alters reported EPS by about TWD 0.40 under a 20% tax assumption. The largest valuation limitation is that distributable excess investments are not separated from required construction liquidity. Peer multiples and through-cycle ROIC have not yet been normalized.

## Valuation history

- **2026-09-09:** Initial framework adopted normalized P/E with book-value/liquidity cross-check. Base normalized earnings multiple at the observed price is approximately 12.5–13.8 times.
