# Alltop — Growth Drivers

## 2026-11-03 driver map

**Hypothesis:** Server power density and interconnect complexity can raise qualified connector/cable content and gross profit per platform. Confirmation requires production revenue, content and margin evidence across customers; AI infrastructure spending alone does not confirm the claim. [Sources S2, S8, S9](sources/README.md).

Management statements relayed by Economic Daily News on 2026-08-05 describe BBU connector shipments, higher-power products, Vietnam production expansion and products under development for 2027–2028 (S9). These are attributed claims, not independently verified order backlog or guaranteed future sales. Server sales must not all be labeled AI sales.

| Horizon | Candidate driver / replacement | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| FY2026 | Server power/data content and automotive demand; replace mature consumer demand | Q3 margins and cash flow; October–December revenue; application mix |
| FY2027 | New qualified platforms and Vietnam output | Named production milestones, incremental content and utilization; cost of ramp |
| FY2028 | Next-generation platforms and broader customer adoption | Repeat wins across generations; evidence of stable share and pricing |

Automotive volume and energy-storage applications could diversify demand, but customer price concessions can erase unit growth. Separate shipment growth from ASP, FX and mix. No quantified cancelability, backlog or 2027–2028 contribution schedule was verified in this review; [Q-2026-003](07_open_questions.md) controls this gap.
