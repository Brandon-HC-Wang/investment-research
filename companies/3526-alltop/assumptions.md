# Alltop — Assumptions

## A-2026-001 — Qualified content can produce sustainable earnings growth

- **Opened:** 2026-11-03. **Status:** Open. **Confidence:** 55%.
- **Claim / mechanism:** Higher server power/data requirements increase qualified content and gross profit, overcoming mature-product replacement and dilution during FY2027–2028.
- **Supporting evidence:** Growing revenue and operating profit; product offerings and management development commentary (S2, S8, S9).
- **Contradicting evidence:** H1 gross-margin decline and only 3.66% diluted-EPS growth; no quantified multiyear backlog verified.
- **Confirmation:** Production wins across customers and generations, stabilizing margins, diluted-EPS and cash growth.
- **Rejection:** Delays/lost content or two reporting periods of declining comparable operating profit/diluted EPS despite revenue growth.
- **Model effect:** Controls forward revenue, margin, reinvestment and sustainable EPS; no numerical forecast adopted.
- **History:** 2026-11-03 Open, 55%; review Q3 evidence by 2026-11-16, then FY2027–2028 production.

## A-2026-002 — Proposed cash return can coexist with adequate funding

- **Opened:** 2026-11-03. **Status:** Open. **Confidence:** 55%.
- **Claim / mechanism:** Returning 168.835630 million does not materially displace required production investment or bond repayment capacity through FY2027.
- **Supporting evidence:** June cash 1,763.194 million and additional financial assets; cash return 9.58% of that cash.
- **Contradicting evidence:** Convertible liabilities 2,117.134 million, H1 distributions above operating cash flow; parent/restricted balances not reconciled.
- **Confirmation:** Q3 parent cash and a bond/working-capital/capex bridge demonstrate funding after distributions.
- **Rejection:** Required investment delays, distressed refinancing or inadequate debt-payment liquidity attributable to distributions.
- **Model effect:** Interest income, reinvestment, liquidity and dilution. No claim that gross cash is unrestricted surplus.
- **History:** 2026-11-03 Open, 55%; next review 2026-11-16 and 2026-12-17.
