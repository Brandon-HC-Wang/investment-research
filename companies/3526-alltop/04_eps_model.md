# Alltop — EPS Model

## 2026-11-03 initial model boundaries

Revenue = customer platform units × qualified content × supplier share × ASP/mix × FX. Gross profit less operating costs, plus nonoperating items, less tax gives attributable profit; divide by the matching weighted-average basic or diluted share count. [Reported baseline](02_financials.md); [sources S2–S4](sources/README.md).

H1 reported profit 598.414 / 65.940 million basic weighted-average shares gives EPS 9.08. The diluted numerator is 608.962 after convertible adjustments of 10.548; the diluted denominator is 76.718 million, including 10.449 million convertible equivalents and 0.329 million employee-compensation equivalents, giving EPS 7.94. Do not simply divide basic profit by the diluted denominator.

## Earnings quality and sensitivity

Start from operating profit; separately model deposit interest, FX, convertible finance costs/valuation, tax and dilution. The H1 financial-instrument gain of 12.301 is not core operating profit. FX losses must not be automatically removed as exceptional. A normalized diluted-EPS point estimate is intentionally withheld until these items are reconciled.

**Sensitivity, not forecast:** On the H1 revenue base, a 1 percentage point gross-margin change, with other costs fixed, changes pretax profit by 23.119 million. At an illustrative 20% tax rate and 65.940 million basic shares, the EPS effect is approximately 0.28. This isolates margin sensitivity and is not a prediction of tax or future EPS.

## Capital reduction mechanics

The proposal implies 67,534,252 old shares and 50,650,689 new shares, subject to subsequent capital changes (S1). In a static full-period example with unchanged profit and no other dilution, EPS scales by 1 / 0.75 = 1.3333. This is denominator arithmetic, not 33.33% operating growth or guidance on reported EPS accounting. Actual timing, weighted averages and convertible terms must be modeled.

Cash leaves the company; interest income can fall. Convertible conversion prices and equivalent shares must be adjusted under each bond's terms, rather than assuming the reduction eliminates dilution. [Q-2026-002](07_open_questions.md).
