## Reduction invariance example

A static pre-reduction EPS of 20 would become 26.6667 at unchanged profit with 25% fewer shares. At an illustrative 257 price, adjusted reference is (257 − 2.5) / 0.75 = 339.3333. Comparing old price with new EPS overstates cheapness. Cash return, lost interest, execution date and adjusted convertible dilution must be included; this is a teaching example, not an EPS forecast.

Downside/base/upside valuation work must first change the operational mechanisms in the thesis, then apply justified multiple or cash-flow assumptions. A reduction alone justifies no higher multiple.
