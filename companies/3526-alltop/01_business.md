# Alltop — Business

## 2026-11-03 initial assessment

**Fact:** Alltop sells connectors and cables for servers, automotive applications and consumer devices. Its product catalog includes high-power and high-speed interconnects. Revenue depends on customer platform shipments, qualified content per platform, supplier share, ASP and currency. It is a component supplier, so customer capital expenditure is not equivalent to its own revenue. [Sources S1–S10](sources/README.md).

**Interpretation:** Customer value comes from reliable power delivery and signal integrity within thermal, space and qualification constraints. Design qualification, tooling and manufacturing consistency may support retention; neither an AI label nor high historical margins independently proves durable pricing power.

Management describes customization, research investment and Vietnam capacity as competitive tools (S8). Evidence against assuming an unchanging moat is H1 gross-margin compression from 52.07% to 48.92% (S2). Customer-level share, socket continuity and competitor pricing remain unverified.

## Economic engine and durability tests

AI-server power and data complexity can increase connector content even without equal growth in server units. Automotive demand provides another application, but customer pricing pressure can offset shipment growth. Qualification must progress into production and cash collection to create value.

Monitor gross profit in TWD, operating margin, inventory, receivables, content per system and wins across successive platforms. See [growth drivers](03_growth_drivers.md), [financials](02_financials.md) and [open questions](07_open_questions.md).
