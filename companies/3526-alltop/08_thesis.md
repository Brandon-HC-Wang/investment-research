# Alltop — Thesis

## Current view — 2026-11-03

**Opinion; confidence medium on current economics, low on long-range earnings:** Alltop has plausible server and automotive content opportunities and substantial operating profitability. The evidence supports a growing business, but not an assumption that revenue growth produces equivalent diluted-EPS growth. The reduction is capital allocation, not a substitute for improving operating profit. [Financials](02_financials.md), [drivers](03_growth_drivers.md), [event review](06_notes.md).

No consensus variant perception was verified. A possible misunderstanding to test is that a smaller share count automatically creates shareholder wealth. Value requires durable profit/cash growth or a better allocation of capital, after matching share and price bases.

## Operational scenarios

| Case | Operating mechanism | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Downside | Automotive pricing or platform delays reduce mix/margin; inventories and receivables absorb cash; dilution offsets profit growth | Further margin compression and weak cash conversion alongside revenue growth |
| Base hypothesis | Existing production supports growth; margin stabilizes and new programs replace mature applications | Q3–Q4 profit conversion, orders and funded capacity; no numerical forecast adopted |
| Upside | Higher qualified power/data content and repeat platform wins improve gross-profit growth | Multi-customer production, stable/improving margin and cash after investment |

The two key EPS risks are (1) margin compression from mix, pricing or production costs and (2) weak profit-to-share conversion from convertible dilution, finance/FX effects and cash distribution demands. A 1 pp margin change on H1 revenue is 23.119 million pretax; see [model](04_eps_model.md).

## Falsification and monitoring

The claim that content growth creates sustainable per-share growth should be challenged if two consecutive reporting periods show growing revenue but declining operating profit or diluted EPS on a comparable share basis. Sustained inventory/receivable growth without cash recovery also challenges earnings quality. These are research thresholds, not company guidance.

Raise confidence only after verified FY2027 production wins, a margin explanation and a parent-level cash/bond funding bridge. Reassess Q3 evidence by 2026-11-16 and the proposed approval event on 2026-12-17. Historical state is retained in [2026Q4](history/2026Q4.md).
