# Research Notes

## 2026-05-21 — China format indicators

- **Date observed:** 2026-08-05
- **Type:** Management claim
- **Source:** *2026/05/21 Investor Conference Presentation*, Wowprime Corp./Taiwan Stock Exchange, 2026-05-21, pp. 34–40, <https://www.twse.com.tw/staticFiles/news/event/8a8216d69e2f5671019e491727280069.pdf>, accessed 2026-08-05.
- **Observation:** Management stated that Wang Steak's 2025 same-store customer count increased 5%, that the revised TASTy format's January–February 2026 revenue increased 43% year over year, and that TASTy's format was replicated to 36 stores.
- **Potential mechanism:** Durable traffic recovery and format replication could raise China sales and operating leverage.
- **Current implication:** Useful directional evidence, but the time windows and definitions differ and do not establish China-wide same-store growth.
- **Next verification:** Obtain full-period brand and China same-store sales, openings/closures, and segment margin.
- **Promotion target:** `03_growth_drivers.md` and `04_eps_model.md` if verified.

## 2026-05-21 — Digital member scale

- **Date observed:** 2026-08-05
- **Type:** Fact plus hypothesis
- **Source:** Same presentation, pp. 21–22.
- **Observation:** The company reported 5.83 million Wowprime app members at 2026-04-30.
- **Potential mechanism:** Cross-brand promotions and first-party data could increase visit frequency and lower acquisition cost.
- **Current implication:** Registered-member scale alone does not demonstrate retention or incremental profit.
- **Next verification:** Obtain active users, purchase frequency, member sales, redemptions, and cross-brand conversion.
- **Promotion target:** `01_business.md` and `08_thesis.md` if economics are demonstrated.
