## Method selection

If the operating thesis is validated, the primary method should be a normalized earnings multiple applied to mid-cycle diluted EPS, cross-checked with enterprise value to lease-adjusted operating cash flow. A simple price-to-earnings ratio alone can obscure lease obligations, new-store investment, and temporary ramp costs. A discounted cash-flow model is premature until maintenance capex, lease cash payments, and unit returns are understood.

## Scenario valuation

No downside, base, or upside valuation is assigned. Operating scenarios exist in `04_eps_model.md` and `08_thesis.md`, but applying multiples before numerical EPS and cash-flow cases would create unsupported precision.

## Relative context

No peer multiple comparison has been completed. Future comparison must normalize restaurant ownership/franchising mix, Taiwan/China exposure, lease accounting, growth capex, same-store growth, and margins.

## Sensitivity and limitations

The likely key valuation sensitivities are mature-store sales, operating margin, net openings, new-store payback, China segment profitability, diluted shares, and the treatment of lease liabilities. Current evidence is insufficient to quantify them.
