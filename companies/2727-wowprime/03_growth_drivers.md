# Growth Drivers

> Current state: 2026-11-09. All earlier sections retained below describe prior research and are superseded where this dated update differs.

## 2026-11-09 — Growth must be separated from profitable replication

**Fact / interpretation:** Net store increases of 9 in Taiwan and 7 in China during Q3 demonstrate added capacity. They do not establish same-store growth or gross openings/closures. Taiwan's 9M segment margin improved to 8.84% from 8.68%, but China's fell to 3.13% from 4.62%. Keep China revenue growth and China cash-return recovery as separate tests. [S1-S4](sources/2026-11-09-refresh.json).

Food-service sales grew 13.63% to TWD 18,706.846 million in 9M, while merchandise sales fell 4.90% to 818.127. Trade/retail category revenue fell to 750.426 from 778.601. These are different disclosure classifications and must not be equated. Retail is therefore an unverified replacement driver, not a demonstrated source of incremental consolidated growth. [S1, pp. 33-34](sources/2026-11-09-refresh.json).

| Horizon | Operating driver and replacement mechanism | Required evidence / confidence |
|---|---|---|
| 2026 Q4 | Existing Taiwan capacity plus seasonal meals/hot pot; China sales with disciplined costs | Monthly releases, reservations/traffic, segment profit; low |
| 2027 | Mature 2026 cohorts replace opening-driven revenue; China margin repair | Positive comparable traffic, closures, cohort margins and lease-adjusted payback; low |
| 2028-2030 | Repeatable brand renewal and member-led visits replace capacity-only growth | Brand survival, active-member economics and incremental returns; low |

Seasonality and openings are analyst mechanisms, not disclosed backlog or guaranteed bookings. A one-quarter holiday timing effect is recoverable only if subsequent traffic and margins recover; persistent weak cohorts and rising impairments represent economic loss rather than a simple delay.

## Current revenue drivers

Consolidated revenue grew 9.5% in 2026 Q1 and 12.1% in the first six months. China outgrew Taiwan in Q1, but Taiwan still supplied 81.2% of group revenue. The reported data establish geographic growth, not its causal mix. Same-store sales, net openings during the comparison period, average spending, and currency contribution must be reconciled before the growth quality can be scored higher.

## Future drivers

Management is pursuing continued multi-brand expansion in Taiwan and refreshed formats in China. The May 2026 presentation reported that the revised TASTy format had been replicated to 36 China restaurants and that the Yuncai Po model would expand in Jiangsu and Zhejiang in the second half if validated. These are development plans, not forecast revenue. Retail food and the 5.83-million-member app could extend customer frequency beyond restaurant visits, but no segment economics are disclosed.

## Leading indicators

Restaurant operators do not report conventional backlog. The relevant forward indicators are:

- same-store sales split between traffic and average spend;
- net openings, closures, and store age by brand and geography;
- sales and margin of new-store cohorts versus mature stores;
- reservations, active app users, repeat rate, and cross-brand conversion;
- food and labor inflation, rent burden, and restaurant-level margin;
- capex, lease additions, and cash payback per opening.

Only total store count, total app membership, geographic revenue, and group/segment profit are currently available.

## Replacement drivers

New stores cannot be a durable growth engine unless mature-store productivity and cash payback remain sound. The replacement mechanism should be either recurring same-store traffic, successful new brand cohorts, or measurable cross-brand member economics. If these do not emerge, additional store count may increase revenue while weakening lease-adjusted returns.

## EPS-relevant risks

1. **Traffic weakness under a fixed-cost base:** negative same-store traffic would reduce sales per store while labor and occupancy costs adjust slowly, compressing restaurant and operating margins. Early warnings are weak same-store sales, heavier promotions, closures, or a falling Taiwan segment margin.
2. **Expansion without adequate returns:** aggressive openings or China replication could add fit-out spending and lease liabilities before demand is proven. Early warnings are rising inventory and lease assets without proportional operating cash flow, delayed openings, impairments, closures, or lower China segment margin.
