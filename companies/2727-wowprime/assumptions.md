# Research Assumptions

This register is append-only. Valid statuses are `Open`, `Verified`, and `Rejected`.

## A-2026-001 — Open

- **Description:** 2026 revenue growth reflects positive underlying customer demand, not only new capacity or calendar timing.
- **Confidence:** 35%
- **Opened:** 2026-08-05
- **Evidence for:** Q1 revenue grew 9.5%, both geographic segments grew, and first-half monthly revenue grew 12.1%.
- **Evidence against or missing:** No consistent same-store traffic, spend, net-opening, calendar, or FX bridge is disclosed.
- **Verification test:** Positive traffic-led same-store sales across Taiwan and China over at least two comparable quarters in 2026.
- **Model effect:** Determines organic revenue, operating leverage, normalized EPS, and thesis confidence.
- **History:** 2026-08-05 — Opened at 35% confidence.

## A-2026-002 — Open

- **Description:** New restaurant cohorts earn an adequate lease-adjusted cash return after maturity.
- **Confidence:** 25%
- **Opened:** 2026-08-05
- **Evidence for:** The company has repeatedly expanded multiple brands and Q1 operating cash flow exceeded reported PP&E and intangible purchases.
- **Evidence against or missing:** Cohort capex, leases, payback, closures, mature margins, and maintenance capex are unavailable.
- **Verification test:** Cohort disclosures or a reconstructed model showing stable mature-store cash margins and acceptable payback after lease principal and growth capex.
- **Model effect:** Determines whether store growth compounds per-share value or only reported revenue.
- **History:** 2026-08-05 — Opened at 25% confidence.

## A-2026-003 — Open

- **Description:** The revised China formats can produce broad, profitable same-store recovery without excessive expansion losses.
- **Confidence:** 35%
- **Opened:** 2026-08-05
- **Evidence for:** China Q1 revenue grew 20.7%, segment profit improved, and management cited selected brand traffic and format gains.
- **Evidence against or missing:** Selected examples use inconsistent periods; China-wide same-store sales, closure rates, and new-format economics are absent.
- **Verification test:** China segment revenue growth accompanied by positive comparable sales and stable or improving segment margin through 2026 Q3.
- **Model effect:** Affects revenue mix, operating margin, EPS range, cash needs, and risk.
- **History:** 2026-08-05 — Opened at 35% confidence.

## A-2026-004 — Open

- **Description:** The Wowprime app creates incremental customer frequency or profit across brands.
- **Confidence:** 20%
- **Opened:** 2026-08-05
- **Evidence for:** The company reported 5.83 million registered members and operates a broad brand portfolio.
- **Evidence against or missing:** No active-user, retention, conversion, incentive-cost, or incremental-profit data are disclosed.
- **Verification test:** Disclosure linking active members to higher frequency, cross-brand spend, lower acquisition cost, or incremental margin.
- **Model effect:** Affects competitive-advantage durability, mature-store sales, and marketing efficiency.
- **History:** 2026-08-05 — Opened at 20% confidence.
