## Prior research — preserved through 2026-10-12

This is a research thesis, not a buy, sell, or hold recommendation.

## Current thesis

Wowprime may be able to compound sustainable per-share earnings through disciplined multi-brand restaurant expansion, mature-store productivity, and a recovery of its China formats. Its candidate advantage is a shared operating platform spanning procurement, brand incubation, staff training, site operations, and a 5.83-million-member app. Near-term reported growth is supportive: 2026 Q1 revenue and operating profit grew 9.5% and 11.1%, and both Taiwan and China segment profit improved. The thesis is not yet proven because the most important replacement driver—productive mature stores after expansion—cannot be observed without same-store sales, store cohorts, closures, and lease-adjusted returns.

## Variant perception

Unknown. No reliable, dated market-consensus assumptions for same-store sales, net openings, China margin, or 2026 EPS have been recorded. The repository therefore does not claim a variant view.

## Evidence for and against

**Supporting evidence**

- The company operated 365 Taiwan and 101 China restaurants at 2026-06-30, demonstrating multi-market operating scale.
- Q1 2026 revenue, operating profit, parent net income, and basic EPS all increased year over year; both geographic segments were profitable on the disclosed segment measure.
- A broad brand portfolio and shared app may allow customer segmentation and cross-brand distribution.

**Contradicting or missing evidence**

- Consolidated and geographic growth cannot be separated into same-store traffic, pricing, new stores, calendar effects, and FX.
- New-store capex, maturity, closure rates, and lease-adjusted cash returns are unavailable.
- Registered app members do not prove active use, retention, or incremental profit.
- China management examples cover selected brands and periods rather than a consistent segment-wide same-store series.

## Scenario thesis

- **Downside:** mature-store traffic weakens while labor and occupancy remain sticky; continued openings add pre-opening expense, lease liabilities, and impairments. Revenue may still grow, but operating margin and EPS decline.
- **Base:** mature-store sales remain modestly positive, new stores ramp without material margin dilution, and China stays profitable. Sustainable EPS grows approximately with operating profit, subject to lease finance cost and share count.
- **Upside:** Taiwan store cohorts achieve rapid cash payback, member data lifts cross-brand visits, and China's revised formats generate broad same-store growth. Sales productivity and operating margin improve together.

No numerical EPS ranges are assigned until the Q2 and cohort evidence gate is passed.

## Principal risks

1. **Same-store traffic and fixed-cost deleverage:** a small sales decline can have a larger EPS effect because labor and occupancy costs are sticky. Monitor same-store sales, promotions, closures, and Taiwan margin each quarter.
2. **Low-return expansion, particularly in China:** fit-out spending and multi-year leases can precede proven demand. Monitor lease additions, capex, inventory, impairments, closures, segment margin, and operating cash conversion.

## Confirmation and falsification

The thesis strengthens if, by 2026 Q3, the company demonstrates positive traffic-led same-store sales, stable or improving restaurant/segment margin, disciplined net openings, and operating cash flow that keeps pace with growth (`Q-2026-001` to `Q-2026-003`). It weakens if revenue growth is primarily capacity-led while mature-store sales fall. It is falsified as currently framed if repeated cohorts require persistent closures or impairments and fail to earn adequate lease-adjusted returns.

## Capital allocation judgment

Unresolved. Q1 operating cash flow covered reported PP&E and intangible purchases, but lease principal, maintenance versus growth capex, and cohort returns are not reconciled. Dividend distributions cannot establish good capital allocation if reinvestment returns are unknown.
