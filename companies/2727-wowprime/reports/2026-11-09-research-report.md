## 2026-11-09 — Current thesis: expansion with weaker profit conversion

**Opinion / confidence: low; discovery.** The multi-brand platform remains a plausible source of durable per-share growth, but Q3 evidence weakens the claim that China expansion is already profitable replication. Taiwan margins are resilient; China revenue growth accompanied lower segment profit, and group selling costs absorbed more revenue. The operating thesis is retained as a hypothesis, not verified compounding.

**Supporting facts:** 482 directly operated stores at September-end; Taiwan 9M segment profit +12.65%; group 9M parent profit +19.16% and CFO 3,160.251. **Contradicting facts:** China 9M segment profit -17.29%, China Q3 margin 3.62% versus 7.08%; group Q3 operating margin 7.13% versus 7.73%; inventory +37.67% year over year. CFO benefits from liabilities and excludes lease principal, so it cannot by itself prove dividend capacity. [S1-S4](../sources/2026-11-09-refresh.json).

**Conditional scenarios:** FY2026 adjusted diluted EPS downside/base/upside **17.26/18.43/19.47**; FY2027 **14.52/19.73/23.60**; FY2028 **14.84/21.54/26.49**. The base requires mature-store productivity and Taiwan margin recovery while China margins stabilize, with no claim that capacity alone raises returns. Details and normalization limits are in [EPS model](../04_eps_model.md).

**Two principal EPS risks:** (1) Traffic weakness and sticky selling/occupancy costs reduce profit conversion; monitor Taiwan comparable traffic, selling-cost ratio and margin. (2) Low-return China cohorts and working-capital/lease commitments absorb cash; monitor China margin, closures, inventory turnover, impairments, lease additions and post-lease cash. Full-year 2027 operating margin 1 percentage-point lower would reduce modeled EPS by about 2.60, other inputs unchanged.

**Confirmation:** During 2026 Q4-2027 H1, require two comparable quarters of traffic-led same-store improvement, stable/improving China margin, transparent closures/cohort returns and cash after leases and capex. **Rejection:** Two further comparable quarters of falling China margin/profit despite sales growth, together with weak cohort returns or rising closures/impairments, reject the profitable-replication path; rebuild the base case. Persistent mature-store declines and inadequate lease-adjusted payback would invalidate the current compounding thesis. These are analyst monitoring tests, not management targets.

Revenue depends on capacity, visits, table turns and average spend. Selected Taiwan networks are 12 Hotpot 100, TASTy 38, Tokiya 34 and Giguo 35. [S4](../sources/2026-11-09-refresh.json).

**Opinion:** Shared procurement, training, brand incubation and member distribution remain plausible advantages, but scale has not yet established superior unit returns. Store counts are period-end capacity, not a measure of mature-store productivity. China contributes 19.29% of 9M revenue but only 7.80% of the Taiwan-plus-China segment profit; its lower margins constrain the value of faster expansion. Registered members remain a marketing asset whose incremental economics are unverified.

**Economic test:** More locations create value only when post-ramp gross profit covers labor, occupancy, recurring closure costs, fit-out and lease cash commitments. Lease liabilities reached TWD 5,142.105 million; a durable moat requires repeatable lease-adjusted returns across cohorts. [S1, pp. 21-22, 50-51](../sources/2026-11-09-refresh.json).

### Formula and normalization

Segment profit = Taiwan revenue × Taiwan margin + China revenue × China margin - other-segment loss (Q4 0.140; future FY 0.600). Common profit = (segment profit + normal nonoperating) × (1 - 20% tax) - NCI. Diluted numerator adds after-tax if-converted interest (Q4 2.700; future FY 10.800). Lease interest remains an expense in normal nonoperating; do not add it back as if it were convertible interest.

The 9M reported diluted numerator is 1,193.583. Remove FX gain 12.278, lease-modification gain 6.547 and financial-asset gain 0.240 at assumed 20% tax: adjustment **15.252**, normalized diluted numerator **1,178.331**. The numerator already reverses issuer gain 0.350; do not subtract it again. Normalized common profit also removes issuer gain at the same assumed marginal tax, giving **1,170.305**. Selected-item tax/NCI attribution is not independently disclosed; this is an analyst convention. Ordinary disposal 16.187, impairment 15.781 and share compensation 5.766 remain expenses. FX is not economically non-recurring; the exclusion provides comparability to the previous supplement.

For FY2026, add Q4 parent profit and if-converted interest to the matching 9M numerator, then divide by **85.860** weighted diluted shares. The base reported-style numerator is **1,597.971**, selected-gain-adjusted **1,582.719**; reported-style EPS **18.61**, adjusted proxy **18.43**. Future years use **86.000** diluted shares. Full-year weighting is an estimate; conversion, buybacks, employee transfers and employee compensation can change it. Do not add rounded quarterly EPS.
