# Financials

## Current financial profile

Wowprime reports in TWD and has a December fiscal year-end. In 2026 Q1, revenue increased 9.5% year over year to TWD 6.519 billion, operating profit increased 11.1% to TWD 538 million, and parent-attributable net income increased 14.2% to TWD 418 million. Gross margin rose 31 basis points to 47.64% and operating margin rose 11 basis points to 8.25%. Reported basic EPS was TWD 5.04 versus TWD 4.41. The current record does not yet distinguish mature-store earnings from new-store ramp costs, so normalized earnings remain unverified.

## Revenue and margin explanation

Taiwan revenue increased 7.2% to TWD 5.294 billion in 2026 Q1; mainland China revenue increased 20.7% to TWD 1.225 billion. Taiwan represented 81.2% of consolidated revenue and China 18.8%. Geographic segment profit was TWD 491 million in Taiwan and TWD 48 million in China, versus TWD 443 million and TWD 42 million respectively one year earlier. The filing does not separate same-store sales, new-store contribution, pricing, or FX; attributing growth to any one factor would therefore be premature.

January–June 2026 monthly revenue totaled TWD 12.816 billion, 12.1% above TWD 11.435 billion in the same 2025 months, based on TWSE monthly figures. The mix between same-store traffic, calendar effects, new stores, and China recovery is unresolved.

## Earnings quality

Q1 pretax income of TWD 529 million included TWD 16.6 million of interest income, TWD 8.9 million of net foreign-exchange gain, TWD 4.0 million of disposal loss, TWD 8.6 million of impairment loss, and TWD 37.1 million of finance cost. These items were not large enough individually to explain the year-over-year profit growth. Lease accounting is economically material: depreciation includes right-of-use assets while cash rent is partly reflected in lease repayments and interest, so conventional operating margin and free cash flow require a lease-aware interpretation.

## Cash flow and reinvestment

In 2026 Q1, operating cash inflow was TWD 805 million, up from TWD 701 million. Purchases of property, plant, and equipment were TWD 311 million and intangible assets were TWD 6 million. A simple pre-lease-repayment free-cash-flow proxy was therefore about TWD 489 million. This is not normalized free cash flow because quarterly working capital, gift-certificate contract liabilities, growth capex, and lease principal have not been separated.

## Balance sheet

At 2026-03-31, cash was TWD 3.154 billion and current amortized-cost financial assets were TWD 2.472 billion. Inventory was TWD 1.716 billion, up 22.9% from 2025 Q1. Right-of-use assets were TWD 4.820 billion. Current and non-current lease liabilities totaled TWD 4.906 billion, and non-current bonds were TWD 672 million. Current contract liabilities were TWD 3.010 billion, which are operational obligations rather than conventional financing. Total assets were TWD 17.702 billion, total liabilities TWD 13.515 billion, and parent equity TWD 3.834 billion.

## Capital allocation

The company is simultaneously expanding restaurants, carrying material lease commitments, and distributing dividends. The return on new-store and brand investment cannot yet be evaluated because store-opening capex, closure costs, payback periods, and cohort sales are not disclosed. Dividend history alone is insufficient to judge per-share value creation.

## Financial assumptions and revisions

- **2026-08-05 — Initial record:** No normalization adjustment or forward EPS forecast is adopted. The binding gaps are brand-level same-store sales, store cohorts, lease-adjusted unit economics, and a five-year financial history.
