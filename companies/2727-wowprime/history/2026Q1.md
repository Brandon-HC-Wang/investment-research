# 2727 Wowprime — 2026 Q1 Research Snapshot

- **Snapshot date:** 2026-08-05
- **Information cutoff:** 2026-08-05; financial statements through 2026 Q1 and monthly revenue through 2026-06
- **Coverage stage:** Discovery
- **Confidence:** Low

## Business state

Wowprime is a directly operated, multi-brand restaurant group concentrated in Taiwan and mainland China. The company reported 365 Taiwan and 101 China restaurants at 2026-06-30 and 5.83 million app members at 2026-04-30. Shared procurement, operating systems, brand incubation, and customer data are candidate advantages, not yet verified economic moats.

## Earnings state

2026 Q1 revenue was TWD 6.519 billion, operating profit TWD 538 million, parent net income TWD 418 million, and basic EPS TWD 5.04. Revenue grew 9.5%, with Taiwan up 7.2% and China up 20.7%. Operating cash inflow was TWD 805 million and PP&E plus intangible purchases were TWD 316 million. Same-store growth, new-store contribution, and lease-adjusted unit returns remain unknown.

## Current thesis

Positive growth and geographic profitability justify continued research into a multi-brand compounding thesis. The thesis remains unproven because sustainable mature-store productivity and incremental store returns cannot be observed. No forward EPS or valuation is adopted.

## Active assumptions

- `A-2026-001` — underlying demand contributes materially to 2026 growth; Open, 35%.
- `A-2026-002` — new stores earn adequate lease-adjusted returns; Open, 25%.
- `A-2026-003` — China formats can recover broadly and profitably; Open, 35%.
- `A-2026-004` — app membership creates incremental economics; Open, 20%.

## Principal open questions

`Q-2026-001` through `Q-2026-003`: growth decomposition, new-store returns, and China recovery quality. Secondary questions cover member economics and peer comparison.

## Next action

Reconcile 2026 Q2 Taiwan and China growth, margins, store counts, capex, lease additions, and operating cash flow when the filing is available.
