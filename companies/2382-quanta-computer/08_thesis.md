# Investment Thesis

## Current thesis

Quanta has credible engineering and scale advantages in notebooks and cloud servers, and the 2025-2026 AI-server ramp is producing real absolute profit growth. The central constraint is economic dilution: 2026H1 revenue nearly doubled while gross profit grew 22.6%, and operating cash flow depends materially on a large increase in payables. Sustainable two-to-five-year EPS compounding is plausible only if Quanta retains qualifications across platforms, adds value beyond expensive component pass-through, and converts working capital without excessive borrowing. Current confidence is medium for 2026 and low beyond it.

## Variant perception

Reliable consensus estimates were not obtained, so a consensus comparison is unknown. The testable analytical variant is that headline AI-server revenue growth is a poor proxy for intrinsic earnings growth; gross-profit dollars, operating margin, and cash conversion carry more information.

## Evidence for and against

- **For:** 2026H1 operating profit rose 27.3% and EPS 37.1%; Q2 margin improved sequentially from the implied Q1 level; July revenue remained strong.
- **Against:** H1 gross margin fell to 4.92%; receivables and inventory each rose about 55% from year-end; product, customer, and backlog disclosure is limited.

## Scenario thesis and principal risks

The [EPS model](04_eps_model.md) estimates 2026 EPS at TWD21.6 downside, TWD27.0 base, and TWD30.5 upside. The downside is not merely multiple compression: deployment digestion and operating margin below 2.8% reduce gross profit while a payable reversal consumes cash. The upside requires both continued systems growth and evidence of higher-value integration that lifts margin.

The two principal risks are AI platform/customer deployment concentration and a working-capital funding reversal. Early warnings are monthly revenue deceleration without a normal seasonal explanation, gross-profit contraction, operating margin below 2.8%, inventory/receivable growth above revenue, falling payables, and rising net borrowings.

## Confirmation and falsification

- **Confirm:** 2026Q3-Q4 gross profit grows at least 20% year over year, operating margin stays at or above 3.1%, and operating cash flow remains positive without a disproportionate payable increase.
- **Strengthen:** evidence of production wins across successor platforms or broader customers, with rising value-added content.
- **Falsify:** two consecutive quarters of gross-profit decline while inventory remains elevated, or operating margin below 2.5% with net borrowing rising materially. See [Q-2026-001](07_open_questions.md) through [Q-2026-004](07_open_questions.md).

## Capital allocation judgment

The TWD15.60 dividend indicates shareholder distribution discipline, but negative operating cash flow in 2024-2025 and current working-capital expansion make the payout less informative than free cash flow. Capital allocation is provisionally adequate, pending proof that capex and geographic capacity earn durable returns.
