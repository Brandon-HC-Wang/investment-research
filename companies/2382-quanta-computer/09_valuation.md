# Valuation

## Valuation context

As of the 2026-09-09 TWSE close, Quanta was TWD342.00 per share. TWSE reported trailing P/E 14.90x, dividend yield 4.56%, and P/B 5.41x. The approximately 3.85 billion weighted share base and 2026-06-30 cash/borrowings are used; the group had about TWD90.05 billion cash deficit relative to borrowings before financial investments.

## Method and normalized earnings

Normalized forward P/E is the primary method because Quanta is an established manufacturer with positive earnings, but it must be applied to profit rather than revenue. P/B is a weak primary measure because value resides in customer qualifications and execution, while the balance sheet is dominated by working capital. DCF confidence is low until cash conversion normalizes.

## Scenario valuation

| Case | Earnings basis | Multiple range | Implied value (TWD/share) | Rationale |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Downside | 2027 EPS 17-20 | 10-12x | 170-240 | Deployment digestion, margin pressure, and funding risk |
| Base | 2027 EPS 27-31 | 12-15x | 324-465 | Continued platform demand but ODM/pass-through discount |
| Upside | 2027 EPS 35-40 | 15-18x | 525-720 | Repeat platform wins plus higher-value integration and cash conversion |

At TWD342, the base 2026 EPS estimate of TWD27.0 implies 12.7x and base 2027 EPS of TWD29.2 implies 11.7x. Equivalently, a 14x multiple requires normalized EPS of about TWD24.4. The price therefore does not require the upside case, but it does require the current ramp to leave earnings materially above 2025 and avoids a deep digestion case.

## Sensitivity and limitations

The widest uncertainty is normalized EPS, not the selected multiple. A 10-basis-point operating-margin change is worth about TWD0.8 EPS at the 2026 base revenue, while working-capital reversal can reduce equity value without immediately changing EPS. The valuation range is not a price forecast or recommendation; it is a map of operating assumptions. Reliable historical multiple bands and comparable-company normalization have not yet been obtained.
