# Research Assumptions

## A-2026-001 — AI-server mix remains the main 2026 growth driver

- **Description:** AI-server/product mix remains the principal reason 2026 revenue and gross profit exceed 2025.
- **Confidence:** 70%. **Status:** Open. **Opened:** 2026-09-10.
- **Evidence for and against:** Revenue and product-mix note support the claim; product units and AI revenue are not disclosed.
- **Verification test:** 2026Q3 management attribution and gross-profit bridge.
- **Model effect:** Determines 2026 revenue and margin.
- **History:** 2026-09-10 — Open at 70%.

## A-2026-002 — 2026 operating margin holds near 3.15%

- **Description:** Full-year 2026 operating margin is 3.0%-3.3% despite pass-through dilution.
- **Confidence:** 60%. **Status:** Open. **Opened:** 2026-09-10.
- **Evidence for and against:** H1 was 3.10% and Q2 3.30%; platform mix and ramp costs remain unknown.
- **Verification test:** Q3 and Q4 operating margins remain within range.
- **Model effect:** Each 10 basis points is about TWD0.8 EPS at base revenue.
- **History:** 2026-09-10 — Open at 60%.

## A-2026-003 — Successor platforms sustain growth after 2026

- **Description:** Successive platform wins support positive revenue and EPS growth in 2027-2028.
- **Confidence:** 40%. **Status:** Open. **Opened:** 2026-09-10.
- **Evidence for and against:** Scale growth suggests execution; no quantified successor wins or backlog are disclosed.
- **Verification test:** Production evidence across generations and customers by 2027H1.
- **Model effect:** Drives base 2027-2028 revenue growth of 10% and 8%.
- **History:** 2026-09-10 — Open at 40%.

## A-2026-004 — Working-capital funding remains available

- **Description:** Supplier credit and borrowing capacity prevent the AI-server ramp from causing a liquidity constraint.
- **Confidence:** 50%. **Status:** Open. **Opened:** 2026-09-10.
- **Evidence for and against:** H1 operating cash flow was positive, but it depended on TWD495.60 billion of payable growth and borrowings increased.
- **Verification test:** Positive rolling-12-month operating cash flow without payable days or net debt worsening materially by 2027H1.
- **Model effect:** Affects equity risk, dividend capacity, and valuation multiple more than near-term EPS.
- **History:** 2026-09-10 — Open at 50%.

## 2026-10-12 — Eight-company dividend enrichment

### A-2026-005 — Conditional parent cash and sector EPS bridge

- **Description:** Operating drivers and parent cash conversion support the dated FY2026-FY2028 scenarios and estimated 2027 cash dividend.
- **Status:** Open.
- **Opened:** 2026-10-12.
- **Confidence:** 35% for EPS, 30% for cash capacity.
- **Causal mechanism:** Segment/cycle margins produce common profit; collections/remittances less funding, debt and reserves constrain ordinary cash allocation.
- **Supporting evidence:** Retained parent statements, reported H1 earnings and three-year dividend declarations; [dated EPS/dividend/cash supplement](model/2026-10-12-dividend-enrichment.md).
- **Contradicting evidence:** GDR actual net receipts/use, asset payment schedule, working-capital conversion and convertible/employee-share reconciliation.
- **Evidence test / confirmation:** Q3 2026 earnings and parent cash bridges, commitments and subsequent board allocation reconcile to the modeled path.
- **Rejection signals:** Material margin/collection shortfall, larger funding/reserve calls or materially different board cash/stock choice.
- **Expected window:** Q3 2026 disclosures through 2027 H1.
- **Model effect:** FY2026E normalized diluted EPS 22.71; FY2027E/FY2028E 24.18/26.09. Estimated 2027 ordinary cash DPS 13.53-19.32, point 18.19, downside 4.04.
- **History:** 2026-10-12 — Opened; projections are not verified distributable earnings.
