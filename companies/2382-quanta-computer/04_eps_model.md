# EPS Model

## Model status

- **As of:** 2026-09-10
- **Currency:** TWD; revenue and profit in TWD billion
- **Latest reported period:** 2026H1
- **Forecast horizon:** 2026-2028
- **EPS:** provisional normalized/basic EPS; no one-off adjustment because evidence is incomplete

## Operating logic

The model uses consolidated revenue as a proxy for systems × bill-of-material content because product units and mix are undisclosed. This is a major limitation. Margin, rather than revenue, carries the estimate of value-added content. Approximately 3.85 billion weighted shares are used.

| Net below-the-line, tax, and NCI effect | (12.4) | (17.3) | (23.3) | (25.0) |

The 2026 revenue estimate starts with TWD2,212.09 billion reported through July and assumes an average TWD327.6 billion per month for August-December. Operating margin is kept near the 2026H1 level rather than restoring the 2025 margin, reflecting pass-through dilution. 2027-2028 growth assumes continued deployments but a sharp deceleration from the initial ramp.

## Scenarios

| Case | Operating path | 2026 EPS | 2027 EPS | 2028 EPS |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Downside | 2026 revenue TWD3.55tn and 2.8% margin; 2027 digestion with revenue -10% and 2.5% margin; slow 2028 recovery | 21.6 | 17.0 | 18.0 |
| Base | 2026 revenue TWD3.85tn and 3.15% margin; 2027-2028 growth 10%/8%, margin 3.2% | 27.0 | 29.2 | 31.6 |
| Upside | 2026 revenue TWD4.10tn and 3.3% margin; 2027-2028 growth 18%/12%, margin rises to 3.4%/3.5% | 30.5 | 37.0 | 43.0 |

The main spread is operating margin and post-ramp deployment volume. Each 10 basis-point change in operating margin at TWD3.85 trillion revenue changes after-tax EPS by roughly TWD0.8, before below-the-line interaction. Each 5% revenue change at constant 3.15% margin changes EPS by roughly TWD1.3.

## Normalization and limitations

The model does not strip out investment gains, FX, or fair-value effects because the H1 filing has not yet been decomposed into a reliable per-share bridge. Product revenue, AI-server units, ASP, customer share, accelerator pass-through, backlog, cancellation terms, and diluted share reconciliation are missing. Forecast confidence beyond 2026 is low; estimates are scenarios, not management guidance or facts.
