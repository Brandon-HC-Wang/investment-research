# Growth Drivers

## Driver map

| Driver | Revenue mechanism | Margin/EPS mechanism | Evidence and confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI-server deployment | Systems/racks × accelerator content × Quanta share | High component value lifts revenue; design, integration, rack, thermal, and service content determine gross profit | 2026H1 revenue +86.5% with gross profit +22.6%; medium |
| Successive platform qualifications | Retains or expands share across accelerator generations | Spreads engineering cost and supports utilization | Not separately disclosed; low |
| Notebook replacement | Units × ASP × model mix | Mature scale supports fixed-cost absorption, but pricing power is weak | Principal business confirmed; unit data absent; low |
| Geographic capacity | Capacity near customers × utilization | May reduce logistics/tariff risk but adds ramp cost and capex | PPE and H1 capex increased; low to medium |

## Current drivers and leading indicators

2026H1 revenue reached TWD1,845.81 billion and July lifted year-to-date revenue to TWD2,212.09 billion, up 92.7% year over year. The TWSE issuer note attributes July's comparison to a product-mix change. This supports an AI/server-rich mix but does not identify shipments, pricing, pass-through content, or backlog. The most useful leading indicators are therefore monthly revenue together with quarterly gross-profit dollars, operating margin, inventory, receivables, payables, and capex.

## Replacement drivers and visibility

The present ramp can persist only if Quanta carries qualifications into successive accelerator platforms, broadens customers, raises value-added rack/power/thermal content, or gains recurring service. A one-platform volume surge is not sufficient two-to-five-year visibility. Formal backlog and cancellation terms are not disclosed.

| Year | Primary driver | Replacement test | Confidence | Largest uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 | AI-server production ramp and high component content | H2 gross-profit growth and cash conversion | Medium | Pass-through share and customer deployment timing |
| 2027 | Broader and next-generation deployments | Repeat qualifications and stable operating margin | Low to medium | Customer concentration and platform allocation |
| 2028 | Replacement, inference, and richer rack integration | Revenue growth accompanied by gross profit and free cash flow | Low | Infrastructure digestion and architecture change |

## EPS-relevant risks

1. **AI infrastructure digestion or platform delay:** lower system shipments can leave inventory and capacity underutilized; a 10%-plus revenue shortfall combined with operating margin below 2.8% would pull EPS toward the low-TWD20s or below.
2. **Working-capital reversal:** if customers slow acceptance while suppliers shorten terms, receivables and inventory remain high but payables fall. Cash outflow and refinancing needs could rise even before the income statement fully weakens.
