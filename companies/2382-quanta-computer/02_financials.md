# Financials

## Current financial profile

Reporting currency is TWD and fiscal year-end is December. AI-server scale has accelerated revenue and absolute profit, but diluted economic quality is visible in lower percentage margins and large working-capital balances. No normalization adjustment is made to EPS because no material one-off has yet been isolated; this is conservative about evidence, not a conclusion that every below-the-line item is recurring.

## Capital allocation and earnings quality

The 2025 cash dividend was TWD15.60 per share, about 80% of reported basic EPS. The payout is attractive only if working-capital funding remains stable; simultaneous rapid inventory growth, higher borrowings, and rising capex reduce the amount of free cash truly available for distribution. Core manufacturing profit is recurring but cyclical and platform dependent; investment/FX income and fair-value movements require a future normalization bridge.
