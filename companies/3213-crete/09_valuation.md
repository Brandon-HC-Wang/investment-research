## Primary method: scenario forward P/E

Apply explicit multiples to 2027 diluted EPS and discount the hypothetical one-year-horizon value by a 10% required return. This is a conservative valuation convention, not a full dividend-discount model; interim shareholder distributions are not separately added. It must not be presented as an exact year-end target or guaranteed total return. Base 11–13 reflects niche qualification and growth, offset by concentrated customers, working-capital absorption and limited quantified order visibility. No independently verified peer or historical-multiple band is claimed. Upside 14–16 requires delivery and cash evidence; downside 9–11 reflects weaker growth and margins.

## Secondary check: operating earnings plus reserved financial assets

2027 base operating parent earnings after assumed 25% tax and minority profit 30 are TWD 784.875 million, or about 13.23 per diluted share, excluding net nonoperating income 50. Capitalizing at 10–11 and discounting one year at 10%, then adding an illustrative current surplus-asset allowance of 15–20 per share, gives roughly 135–152. The allowance is deliberately below gross financial assets: at 2026-06-30 these were 2,833.764 million; subtract leases 72.262, dividends payable 410.799, current tax 196.264, deferred tax 102.705, minority equity 39.384 and an analyst operating reserve of 1,000 million, leaving about 17.25 per basic share. This is a conservative group-level approximation, not an audited surplus-cash entitlement or a guarantee of liquidity. Investment market values and working-capital needs can change.

Do not add this allowance to the primary total-earnings P/E: that would overlap investment-income value. The secondary check supports the lower/middle portion of the primary band and cautions against valuing the whole balance sheet as free cash.

## Interpretation and review

The base implies conditional undervaluation relative to 124.00, but downside can put value materially below that price. A share price above the base band would require stronger earnings persistence, better cash conversion or a justified higher multiple. Price alone cannot determine whether those conditions hold. A 5 percentage-point tax increase cuts 2027 EPS by about 0.96 and current base value by approximately 10–11 at unchanged multiples.
