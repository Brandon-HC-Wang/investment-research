# Business — Crete (3213)

As of 2026-09-24. Sources: [FY25, AR25, IR25, H126](sources/README.md).

Crete designs and manufactures rugged notebooks, tablets and handheld/control computers for defense and demanding industrial environments, and separately distributes commercial computers through its Taiwan retail channel. Customers pay for reliable operation, customized interfaces, environmental qualification and long product support, rather than only commodity computing performance. The business is hardware/project delivery, not recurring subscription revenue.

Rugged engineering, qualification and maintenance commitments can create switching costs. Management presents military-standard testing, integrated hardware/firmware customization and long support periods. These support a niche-position hypothesis; independently measured market share and renewal retention are unavailable. Retail bargaining scale and service are weaker barriers than qualification in rugged products. No separate segment profit is disclosed, so segment margin claims are not modeled as audited facts.

2025 revenue was rugged TWD 1,390.864 million, commercial TWD 1,414.469 million and repair/other TWD 81.878 million. RODA accounted for 29.63% of total sales, creating customer/channel dependence. Its ownership connection changed after the 2025 disposal; a retained commercial relationship does not establish control. MilDef Group AB is a separate invested company and commercial ecosystem participant, not a wholly owned subsidiary whose earnings can be consolidated into Crete EPS.

The relevant [industry framework](../../industries/ai-hardware/README.md) is applied to product qualification and component costs. AI/drone functionality is a potential product extension, not evidence of an AI-server revenue stream or a quantified new profit pool.

## 2026-09-24 — Named customers and route to market

**Reported facts:** AR25 printed p. 77 reports RODA sales of TWD 855.596 million (29.63%) in 2025 and TWD 928.676 million (32.55%) in 2024. MilDef's acquisition announcement identifies RODA as Crete's exclusive laptop/tablet reseller in several Central European markets. RODA serves defense authorities, procurement agencies and defense-industry companies, mainly through framework agreements. This supports a Crete → RODA → defense customer channel; it does not identify each ultimate user's purchases from Crete.

MilDef's 2025 report, Note 28 p. 114, reports purchases from MilDef Crete Inc. of SEK 307.8 million; units follow Note 1 p. 89. This independently corroborates a procurement relationship at group level. Do not add this amount to Crete's RODA sales: consolidation scope, currency and timing differ and RODA entered MilDef's group on 2025-03-06. MilDef announced on 2026-09-23 that RODA became MilDef GmbH, retaining products, teams and customer relationships.

**Company-presented references:** IR25 slide 9, “Customer in Security & Defense,” displays NCSIST, AIDC, BAE Systems, HENSOLDT, Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, Elbit Systems, Raytheon, Rheinmetall, Airbus, Saab, L3Harris, Northrop Grumman, Kongsberg, Bundeswehr and FMV, among others. These are attributed presentation references, not individually verified current direct accounts. The slide does not disclose contract counterparties, dates, revenue amounts or whether supply is direct, through RODA, or through other partners. Do not treat every MilDef group customer or contract as Crete business.
