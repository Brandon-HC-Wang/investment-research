# Investment thesis

As of 2026-09-24. Evidence confidence medium; 2027 earnings confidence low-to-medium. No buy/sell/hold instruction.

## Current view

Crete can lift earnings as rugged-system demand and product mix improve, while its commercial channel supplies a steadier but less differentiated revenue base. Qualification/customization and long support can sustain a niche, but project timing and customer concentration constrain visibility. The case is a delivery-and-cash-conversion improvement, not an assumption that defense budgets or AI labels guarantee profits.

The base [model](04_eps_model.md) gives diluted EPS 12.15 in 2026 and 13.86 in 2027, with a low-confidence 2028 extension of 14.53. Upside requires capacity conversion and pricing; downside combines order delays, margin pressure and financial-item volatility. [Financials](02_financials.md) show improved operating profit alongside negative operating cash flow. No documented consensus is available, so variant perception is unknown.

## Confirmation and falsification

Through Q3/FY2026, seek sustained revenue near the required run rate, gross margin at least 34%, better collections and verified commissioning. Repeated margin below 32%, stalled rugged sales despite high inventory, or evidence of order cancellation would reject the base path. Unexplained recurring tax above the model requires an EPS reduction even if revenue holds up. These are analytical review triggers linked to [Q-2026-001 through Q-2026-005](07_open_questions.md), not company commitments.

Principal risk 1: RODA/project concentration plus delayed acceptance → inventory/receivables remain high → provisions or underutilization → lower EPS and cash. Principal risk 2: component costs, FX or persistent high tax → lower retained earnings despite revenue growth. Sensitivities are quantified in the model.

## Capital allocation and quality

Liquid assets cushion working-capital needs, but the declared dividend is close to prior-year basic earnings and securities appreciation is not operating cash generation. Do not count strategic investments both in a total-earnings P/E and as a full extra asset premium.
