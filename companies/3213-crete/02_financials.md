## Normalization boundary

The 182.342 after-tax gain transferred from FVOCI equity disposals to retained earnings in H1 is not income-statement profit. Do not add it to EPS or subtract it from reported EPS as if already included. H1 effective tax was 28.51%; Q2 alone was 34.91%. The interim note does not explain the full tax-rate increase; no automatic adjustment to 20% is made. Illustrative FX-neutral H1 diluted EPS is about 5.51 versus reported 5.56, removing 3.275 of FX gain at an assumed 25% tax rate, with unchanged minority attribution. This is an analytical sensitivity, not company adjusted EPS. Ordinary staff compensation, depreciation and inventory charges stay in expenses.
