# Research Scoring

Scoring standardizes attention and exposes disagreement. It does not produce a recommendation or replace written reasoning.

## Scale

Score each dimension from 1 to 5 using the full rubric:

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Structurally weak, adverse, or unsupported |
| 2 | Below average; material deficiencies or uncertainty |
| 3 | Adequate; mixed evidence and manageable limitations |
| 4 | Strong; evidence supports durable favorable economics |
| 5 | Exceptional; multiple independent sources support rare durability |

Scores must include a one-sentence rationale and the date assessed. Do not use half-points.

## Dimensions and weights

| Dimension | Weight | What the score measures |
|---|---:|---|
| Business quality | 20% | Customer value, returns, cash conversion, resilience, and capital intensity |
| Competitive advantage | 15% | Strength, evidence, and durability of barriers or differentiation |
| Revenue-driver quality | 10% | Structural demand, pricing, share, diversification, and driver measurability |
| EPS-driver quality | 15% | Margin durability, operating leverage, dilution, and forecast transparency |
| Sustainable earnings | 15% | Recurrence, cash backing, cyclicality, and dependence on exceptional items |
| Future visibility | 15% | Backlog or leading indicators, replacement drivers, and two-to-five-year evidence |
| Capital allocation | 10% | Reinvestment returns, balance-sheet discipline, acquisitions, and per-share outcomes |

The weighted quality score is:

`Σ(dimension score × weight)`

The result remains on a 1-to-5 scale. Valuation is deliberately excluded; report it separately so apparent cheapness cannot raise the quality score.

## Confidence

Confidence in `meta.yaml` describes evidence maturity:

| Confidence | Definition |
|---|---|
| `low` | Core claims rely on limited, indirect, stale, or conflicting evidence |
| `medium` | Major claims have credible support, but one or more EPS-sensitive questions remain |
| `high` | Major claims are triangulated, the causal model has held across updates, and residual uncertainty is bounded |

Never increase confidence only because reported earnings beat a forecast. Increase it when the causal explanation or future visibility becomes more reliable.

## Priority

Research priority controls analyst attention:

| Priority | Use |
|---|---|
| `1` | Time-sensitive evidence can materially change the thesis or close a major uncertainty |
| `2` | Important work should be completed in the current research cycle |
| `3` | Normal monitoring or model maintenance |
| `4` | Low urgency; wait for a defined trigger |
| `5` | Archived or intentionally deferred |

Priority is not conviction and must not be interpreted as expected return.

## Scoring discipline

- Cite the strongest supporting and contradicting evidence.
- Reduce scores when a dimension is not understood; do not assume average quality.
- Preserve dated prior scorecards when a material score changes.
- Explain a change of two or more points in the company timeline.
- Revisit scores after major acquisitions, governance changes, cycle turns, or evidence that alters normalized earnings.
