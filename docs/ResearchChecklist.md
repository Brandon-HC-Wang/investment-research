# Research Checklist

Use this checklist as a quality gate. “Unknown” is acceptable when recorded as an open question with a concrete resolution method.

## Context and scope

- [ ] Required repository context was read in the mandated order.
- [ ] The company identity, market, ticker, fiscal year, and reporting currency are unambiguous.
- [ ] The research question and time horizon are explicit.
- [ ] Relevant industry and model guidance was reviewed.

## Business quality and advantage

- [ ] Products, customer groups, revenue models, and segment economics are mapped.
- [ ] Customer value and purchase criteria are explained.
- [ ] Proposed competitive advantages have evidence, mechanisms, and erosion risks.
- [ ] Capital intensity, cash conversion, and reinvestment needs are understood.

## Revenue and EPS

- [ ] Revenue changes are decomposed into volume, price, mix, share, acquisition, cycle, and FX where relevant.
- [ ] Gross-margin and operating-margin changes have causal explanations.
- [ ] Inventory, utilization, PPA amortization, investment income, tax, and dilution are addressed where material.
- [ ] Reported EPS reconciles to normalized EPS.
- [ ] Recurring, cyclical, project-based, and non-recurring earnings are separated.

## Future visibility

- [ ] Backlog or forward indicators are assessed for quality and timing.
- [ ] Forecasts cover at least three explicit years and the broader two-to-five-year horizon.
- [ ] Each major current driver has a named replacement driver or an explicit gap.
- [ ] Base, upside, and downside cases change operational assumptions.
- [ ] The most EPS-sensitive uncertainty is identifiable.

## Thesis and risk

- [ ] The thesis states the sustainable earnings mechanism.
- [ ] One or two EPS-relevant risks are quantified or bounded.
- [ ] Confirmation and falsification signals are observable and time-bound.
- [ ] Supporting and contradicting evidence are both represented.
- [ ] Confidence is consistent with evidence maturity.

## Valuation

- [ ] Valuation follows the business and earnings assessment.
- [ ] The primary method fits the economics.
- [ ] One-off and peak-cycle earnings are not capitalized without justification.
- [ ] Operating assumptions are separated from multiple or discount-rate assumptions.
- [ ] Currency, period, share count, net debt, and sensitivity are disclosed.

## Evidence and history

- [ ] Material facts cite primary sources where available.
- [ ] Facts, management claims, estimates, hypotheses, and opinions are distinguishable.
- [ ] Historical assumptions and question states remain preserved.
- [ ] Every resolved or rejected question includes evidence and thesis impact.
- [ ] Dated changes use ISO format.

## Repository integrity

- [ ] All affected canonical files were updated together.
- [ ] `meta.yaml` reflects the current state, next action, and watch items.
- [ ] Reusable insights were promoted to an industry, model, or research-log record.
- [ ] Relative links and headings are valid.
- [ ] `python3 scripts/validate_repository.py` passes.
