# DRAM Cycle Thesis

## Industry

DRAM suppliers serve data-center, PC, mobile, consumer, automotive, and industrial demand through products with different performance, qualification, and pricing characteristics. Supply depends on wafer capacity, process migration, yield, and capital discipline.

## Current stage

- **Thesis status:** Open.
- **Research stage:** Cycle framework established; the current inventory, pricing, utilization, and capacity stage has not yet been verified from a common dated evidence set.
- **Confidence:** Low until supplier and customer data are reconciled.

## Working thesis

Sustainable DRAM earnings require bit demand and higher-value mix to absorb productivity and capacity growth without triggering destructive pricing. Price recovery can lift EPS rapidly, but durability depends on inventory normalization, capital discipline, product mix, and replacement demand after the initial cycle turn.

## Key companies

The primary observation set includes Samsung Electronics, SK hynix, Micron, Nanya Technology, and Winbond. Customers and equipment suppliers may provide independent evidence of inventory, capital intensity, and end-demand conditions. Inclusion is analytical, not a recommendation.

## Risks

- Supply growth or migration productivity exceeds bit demand.
- Customer restocking is mistaken for end-demand recovery.
- High-value product strength obscures weakness in conventional DRAM.
- Capital expenditure responds to pricing before inventories normalize.

## Future catalysts

- Verified customer inventory normalization.
- Higher memory content in servers, AI accelerators, PCs, and devices.
- Mix shift toward products with stronger qualification or performance barriers.
- Evidence of disciplined capacity and improving cost per bit.

## Verification

Track bit shipments, ASP, utilization, inventory, contract and spot pricing, capital expenditure, process migration, cost per bit, and product mix. Scenario conclusions must flow through gross margin and EPS rather than stop at price direction.
