# Property Market Thesis

## Industry

The property value chain includes landowners, developers, contractors, project-sales agencies, brokers, lenders, and buyers. Transaction volume, price, funding, policy, project supply, and recognition timing affect each role differently.

## Current stage

- **Thesis status:** Open.
- **Research stage:** Taiwan-focused driver framework established; the current transaction, credit, and project-completion stage requires a dated primary-source review.
- **Confidence:** Low because company-attributable pipelines and policy transmission lags are not yet reconciled.

## Working thesis

Property-agency earnings can weaken faster than headline prices when transaction volume falls against a fixed selling-cost base. Developers may still report discrete completion earnings, so current revenue can diverge from future visibility. The decisive question is which projects and mandates replace present recognition over the next two to five years.

## Key companies

The first linked company is [`2348 Hiyes`](../companies/2348-hiyes/08_thesis.md), which combines property agency, development, and investment exposure. Additional developers, agencies, contractors, and lenders should be added only with linked company or industry records.

## Risks

- Credit or transaction policy reduces volumes and delays sell-through.
- Completion timing masks a weakening replacement pipeline.
- Construction and financing costs reduce project margin.
- Guarantees and joint-project obligations amplify delays.

## Future catalysts

- Verified recovery in transactions and presales without price concessions.
- Attributable completion schedules that support replacement earnings.
- Urban-renewal milestones moving projects from optionality to executable backlog.
- Improved funding conditions and cash conversion.

## Verification

Track transaction value, presales, mortgage and policy conditions, represented project value, take rate, completion schedules, ownership, project margin, guarantees, and cash collection. Separate brokerage, development, and investment earnings.
