# Macro Investment Theses

This layer stores testable views that span multiple companies or an entire industry. A macro thesis explains the causal environment in which company earnings may develop; it never replaces company-specific business, EPS, risk, or valuation work.

## Thesis contract

Every thesis states the industry, current stage, key companies or value-chain roles, central mechanism, risks, future catalysts, observable confirmation and rejection signals, confidence, and dated history. Initial files remain `Open` until evidence establishes the market stage.
