## 2026-08-04 — Research system contract established

- **Scope:** Repository-wide methodology and future company research.
- **Type:** Methodology change.
- **Finding:** Long-term research requires stable company interfaces, explicit evidence labels, append-only hypothesis history, and a causal bridge from business activity to normalized EPS before valuation.
- **Evidence:** The repository’s initial principles and workflow contained the core analytical sequence but lacked a complete file contract, state schema, validation, and cross-agent update protocol.
- **Mechanism:** Stable interfaces make company evidence addressable across agents; append-only revisions preserve learning; causal EPS models prevent revenue or news summaries from being mistaken for earnings analysis.
- **Confidence:** High. The principal uncertainty is how the conventions will evolve after repeated use across real companies.
- **Repository impact:** Added the system context, agent contract, AI workflow, canonical framework, scoring and valuation standards, industry and model layers, company templates, metadata schema, and validation tooling.
- **Next verification:** Apply the contract to the first real company and record any friction that warrants a backward-compatible methodology revision.
