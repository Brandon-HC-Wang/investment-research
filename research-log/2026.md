- **Proposed separation:** A future `strategies/` layer should store testable economic-edge hypotheses and their validation state; a future `screens/` layer should store reproducible universe, filter, source, cadence, and append-only result snapshots. Screen output may nominate research candidates but must not become an automatic buy list.
- **Initial candidate:** “Quarter-end FVTPL earnings nowcast” — use the latest disclosed holdings as a starting inventory, reprice observable listed securities and fund NAVs at quarter-end, model the unobservable bucket separately, bridge the estimated fair-value change through tax and attribution to EPS, and compare the estimate with market expectations before the financial filing.

- **Recommended lifecycle:** `Idea` → `Testing` → `Validated` → `Monitoring`, with `Rejected` and `Archived` terminal alternatives. Each strategy should have `last_run`, `next_refresh`, `refresh_trigger`, `stale_after`, confidence, falsification criteria, and links to dated screen results.

## 2026-09-10 — Forward dividend-yield screen defined

- **Scope:** TWSE and TPEx common stocks that may distribute cash dividends in 2027 from 2026 earnings.
- **Type:** Cross-company discovery methodology; not a recommendation list or a validated strategy.
- **Definition:** Forward cash dividend yield is estimated 2027 cash dividend per share divided by the stock price on the screen date. Exchange-displayed yield is retained only as a discovery input because it reflects a historical distribution and can include non-recurring or non-earnings-funded components.
- **Current universe fact:** On 2026-09-09, the exchange APIs showed 65 TWSE and 76 TPEx records with displayed yield of at least 7%, or 141 in total. This count is not a forward-yield result.
- **Primary screen:** Estimate normalized attributable 2026 diluted EPS, then estimate cash DPS using the lower of a conservatively supported payout ratio and parent-company distributable cash capacity. A base-case candidate requires estimated forward cash yield of at least 7%, normalized-EPS coverage of required DPS of at least 1.25 times, and a downside yield of at least 5% to 5.5%.
- **Quality filters:** Test three- to five-year payout consistency, three-year cash dividend coverage by free cash flow, leverage and interest coverage, share-count dilution, liquidity, and the durability of 2027 revenue and EPS drivers. Separate financial companies, construction/property companies, and cyclical companies because their capital, project-recognition, and normalized-earnings tests differ.
- **Earnings-quality exclusions:** Flag rather than automatically capitalize asset disposals, FVTPL gains, FX gains, tax reversals, capital-surplus distributions, capital reductions, and other one-offs. A displayed yield above 7% with weak normalized EPS or cash coverage belongs in a yield-trap review, not the durable-income candidate set.
- **Evidence:** Taiwan Stock Exchange, “Daily PER, dividend yield and PBR for all listed stocks,” data date 2026-09-09, accessed 2026-09-10, [BWIBBU_ALL](https://openapi.twse.com.tw/v1/exchangeReport/BWIBBU_ALL); Taipei Exchange, “Mainboard stock PER, dividend yield and PBR,” data date 2026-09-09, accessed 2026-09-10, [tpex_mainboard_peratio_analysis](https://www.tpex.org.tw/openapi/v1/tpex_mainboard_peratio_analysis); Taiwan Stock Exchange OpenAPI, accessed 2026-09-10, [listed and dataset company financial, monthly-revenue, and dividend-distribution datasets](https://openapi.twse.com.tw/).
- **Confidence:** High for data availability and the displayed-yield count; medium for the proposed screening thresholds until point-in-time tests show how often the base and downside cases predict the subsequent declared cash dividend.
- **Repository impact:** Recorded a reproducible forward-yield definition and quality gates without creating the still-unapproved `screens/` or `strategies/` contracts.
- **Next verification:** Run the full market screen as an append-only point-in-time snapshot, disclose the EPS and payout assumptions for every retained candidate, and compare the estimates with 2027 board declarations and shareholder approvals.

## 2026-09-24 — Taiwan ETF distributions, underlying dividends, and NAV: 00919

- **Scope:** Reusable distribution-quality analysis for Taiwan equity ETFs, illustrated by 00919; no company forecast or recommendation.
- **Type:** Verified disclosure facts and accounting interpretation. The user's 2%–3% underlying dividend yield versus 11% ETF distribution rate is an unverified illustration, not a reconciled result.
- **Economic engine:** An equity ETF pools investors' assets into a portfolio. Its service is portfolio implementation and trading access; distributing cash does not create an additional earnings engine. Underlying businesses generate earnings and dividends; changes in their market valuations generate portfolio gains or losses. High distribution rates alone establish neither a durable advantage nor superior investment returns.
- **Verified facts:** TWSE's distribution list reports the following amounts and estimated source composition. Currency is TWD per ETF unit; source shares are percentages of each distribution, not yields on NAV.

| Ex-distribution date | Payment date | Distribution | Estimated dividend income | Estimated equalization | Estimated realized capital gains |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2026-03-17 | 2026-04-14 | 0.78 | 0.00% | 0.00% | 100.00% |
| 2026-06-16 | 2026-07-13 | 1.00 | 0.00% | 0.00% | 100.00% |
| 2026-09-16 | 2026-10-15 | 1.10 | 0.00% | 0.00% | 100.00% |

- **Disclosure boundary:** These are estimated allocation categories, not final recipient statements or a reconciliation of all fund income. The 2026-10-15 payment is still scheduled as of 2026-09-24. Zero dividend allocation does not mean the fund received no dividends. The disclosures do not establish when the distributed gains were earned or that every future distribution will have this composition.
- **Rate definitions:** Portfolio cash-dividend yield requires aligned dates and market-value weights, rather than a simple average of constituent yields. Actual fund dividend income also depends on holdings at dividend-entitlement dates, portfolio changes, and unit issuance/redemption. ETF trailing distribution yield equals the last four quarterly distributions divided by a specified price; annualizing the latest quarter is a different calculation. Capital's latest entry reports TWD 1.10 and a 3.37% quarterly distribution rate, using the prior business day's NAV. Multiplying the rounded rate by 4 gives an illustrative 13.48%, not a forecast or an observed annual return.
- **Mechanism:** Cash distributions can allocate stock dividends, other eligible income, realized gains, accumulated distributable balances, and equalization subject to the fund's applicable contract. Realized gains are investment profits, but their distribution is not proof that the whole portfolio earned the same amount in the current period: unrealized losses can coexist. Equalization allocates part of primary-market subscriptions/redemptions to accumulated income so unit-count changes do not dilute accrued distribution entitlements. It creates no investment profit and is not evidence that a 3% earning capacity becomes 11%.
- **NAV and pricing:** NAV per unit equals portfolio assets, including cash and receivables, less liabilities, divided by units outstanding. Exchange prices are formed by trading; creation/redemption arbitrage ordinarily keeps them near NAV, within costs and market constraints. Therefore a discretionary distribution divided by a desired yield is not an independent ETF valuation anchor. Other things equal, an ETF distribution reduces NAV by the distribution amount. A constituent's ex-dividend price adjustment is offset by its dividend receivable in the fund; receipt of that dividend must not be counted as a second windfall.
- **Illustration, not 00919 results:** With no flows, start with TWD 100, earn TWD 3 of net income, have zero net capital return, and distribute TWD 11, assuming the distribution is otherwise permitted and sufficient distributable balances exist. Ending assets are TWD 92; assets plus distributed cash are TWD 103, a 3% total return. With an additional TWD 8 net capital gain, ending assets are TWD 100 and total return is 11%. A distribution reallocates wealth between the fund and cash; it does not independently increase it.
- **Risk and monitoring:** A weak equity market can reduce NAV and replenish fewer distributable gains; an unchanged payout can then consume accumulated reserves, or the payout may fall. Constituent earnings/dividend cuts also weaken recurring income. Track net dividend income per unit, realized and unrealized gains, accumulated distributable balances, expenses, distributions, and time-weighted total return against an appropriate total-return benchmark. NAV declines alone do not prove inappropriate distributions; ex-distribution adjustments and market returns must be separated. Full recovery to the prior price is not required for a positive holding-period total return.
- **Confidence:** High for the observed disclosures and accounting mechanics; insufficient evidence for 00919's recurring dividend coverage or sustainable future payout. No stock-by-stock weighted dividend reconstruction, audited fund-account reconciliation, or realized investor return was completed.
- **Open evidence items:** ETF-00919-Q01 (Open, 2026-09-24): identify the user's comparison period, price denominator, and historical constituent holdings, then reconcile actual dividend income per unit. ETF-00919-Q02 (Open, 2026-09-24): compare final distribution statements with the estimates and reconcile realized gains, unrealized losses, retained balances, expenses, and unit changes to fund financial reports. These cross-product questions remain here because no company coverage directory is being created.
- **Repository impact:** Appended this canonical research-log entry and its Traditional Chinese companion; no methodology, schema, screen state, or company assumptions changed.
- **Next verification:** Before estimating payout sustainability, resolve Q01 and Q02 for matching periods; verify the 2026-09 distribution's final composition when its statement is available around the scheduled 2026-10-15 payment.

## 2026-09-24 — ETF flows, crowded holdings, and the evidence threshold for collusion

- **Scope / type:** Follow-up to the 00919 distribution analysis; causal interpretation and an untested market-flow hypothesis, not a finding of misconduct or quantified attribution of the current Taiwan equity rally.
- **Finding:** Domestic-equity ETF net subscriptions and portfolio implementation can create constituent demand. Similar mandates can concentrate that demand without coordination. Neither overlapping holdings nor realized-gain distributions demonstrate collusion. New fund counts, exchange turnover, and total ETF AUM are not measurements of incremental domestic-equity buying.
- **Mechanism:** Secondary-market ETF purchases transfer existing units between investors; primary-market creations/redemptions change units outstanding. Cash creations can lead to underlying purchases, while in-kind creations transfer securities that may already be held or hedged. New subscriptions funded by selling existing Taiwan stocks or other domestic ETFs can partly offset the apparent inflow. Overseas and bond ETFs must be separated. AUM growth also includes market appreciation; rising prices can attract subscriptions, creating reverse causality.
- **Hypothesis ETF-FLOW-H01:** Large net creations into funds with overlapping holdings can amplify short-term price pressure when required purchases are material relative to tradable supply and market depth. Status Open; confidence medium in the mechanism and low in its unmeasured contribution to the current rally. A possible feedback path is subscriptions → purchases → price gains → realized distributions/performance visibility → further subscriptions. Supporting evidence is the documented creation/redemption mechanism; counterweights include redemptions, substitution from existing equity holdings, in-kind transfers, and common responses to improving earnings. Confirmation requires flow changes preceding abnormal constituent returns, a stronger effect at higher purchase pressure, and controls for earnings news, global markets, and other investor flows. Reject the claim of a dominant contribution if measured domestic-equity demand is small or weakly overlaps index leaders and the relationship disappears with controls. Test daily event windows around launches/rebalances and the following 1–3 months; this is a proposed test, not a completed study.
- **Economic beneficiaries:** Fund trading gains remain in fund assets for unitholders; management fees are separate contractual compensation. Asset gathering can benefit fee revenue and create commercial incentives, but this does not establish personal appropriation of capital gains. Managers normally work for investment trust companies; a bank's distribution/custody role or shared financial-group ownership does not identify the portfolio decision-maker. Ordinary stock gains, ETF price/NAV arbitrage, and abusive trading must be distinguished.
- **Misconduct evidence boundary:** The securities regulator's 2023-09-12 sanctions concerning Fubon and Prudential investment trusts document specific compliance failures; the Fubon section describes certain former managers using information obtained through their roles to trade via specified persons' accounts and failures to report personal trades. This shows that concrete conflicts can occur; it is not evidence against 00919, an ETF-wide conspiracy, or a cause of the current rally. Allegations of coordination require linked accounts/orders, communications, beneficial ownership or money flows, and investigation findings. Matching trades alone may reflect common mandates. This review is not an exhaustive enforcement search.
- **Risk / next verification:** Flow reversal can pressure crowded holdings and reduce future distributable gains without any misconduct. Before attributing the rally, define the index and start/end dates; reconcile domestic-equity ETF daily unit changes and NAV with creation/redemption disclosures, estimate constituent demand relative to liquidity, and decompose index returns by constituents alongside earnings and valuation changes. Public data may identify crowding but cannot establish private coordination by itself.
- **Repository impact:** Appended this bilingual log; no company forecasts, screening state, or misconduct findings created.
