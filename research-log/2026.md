## 2026-08-04 — Research system contract established

- **Scope:** Repository-wide methodology and future company research.
- **Type:** Methodology change.
- **Finding:** Long-term research requires stable company interfaces, explicit evidence labels, append-only hypothesis history, and a causal bridge from business activity to normalized EPS before valuation.
- **Evidence:** The repository’s initial principles and workflow contained the core analytical sequence but lacked a complete file contract, state schema, validation, and cross-agent update protocol.
- **Mechanism:** Stable interfaces make company evidence addressable across agents; append-only revisions preserve learning; causal EPS models prevent revenue or news summaries from being mistaken for earnings analysis.
- **Confidence:** High. The principal uncertainty is how the conventions will evolve after repeated use across real companies.
- **Repository impact:** Added the system context, agent contract, AI workflow, canonical framework, scoring and valuation standards, industry and model layers, company templates, metadata schema, and validation tooling.
- **Next verification:** Apply the contract to the first real company and record any friction that warrants a backward-compatible methodology revision.

## 2026-09-09 — High-yield FVTPL screen established

- **Scope:** TWSE and TPEx common stocks.
- **Type:** Cross-company accounting and earnings-quality screen.
- **Finding:** Of 434 stocks with exchange-reported dividend yield above 5% on 2026-09-08, 264 had positive FVTPL financial assets at 2026-06-30; 35 had FVTPL equal to at least 10% of total assets.
- **Evidence:** TWSE and TPEx yield APIs, MOPS 2026Q2 Inline XBRL, TWSE financial-industry balance-sheet OpenAPI, and a clearly labeled FinMind gap-fill extract where MOPS throttled batch access.
- **Mechanism:** Combining a price-based income screen with balance-sheet investment exposure identifies companies where displayed yield may coexist with material mark-to-market earnings volatility; instrument composition and normalized dividend coverage still require company-level review.
- **Confidence:** Medium. All 434 yield candidates were classified, but 164 of 264 FVTPL matches rely on a secondary structured-data extract pending direct-filing verification.
- **Repository impact:** Added [`industries/FVTPLHighYieldScreen.md`](industries/FVTPLHighYieldScreen.md) and the complete 264-name CSV.
- **Next verification:** Prioritize direct filing review for non-financial companies with FVTPL above 10% of assets and separate low-volatility funds and bonds from equity and private-market exposure.

## 2026-09-09 — Strategy-research architecture gap identified

- **Scope:** Repository-wide workflow beyond single-company research.
- **Type:** Architecture assessment; no new directory contract established.
- **Finding:** The repository has company, macro-thesis, research-watchlist, model, and portfolio-risk layers, but no first-class lifecycle for reusable strategy hypotheses, screen definitions, point-in-time results, refresh schedules, or historical validation.
- **Proposed separation:** A future `strategies/` layer should store testable economic-edge hypotheses and their validation state; a future `screens/` layer should store reproducible universe, filter, source, cadence, and append-only result snapshots. Screen output may nominate research candidates but must not become an automatic buy list.
- **Initial candidate:** “Quarter-end FVTPL earnings nowcast” — use the latest disclosed holdings as a starting inventory, reprice observable listed securities and fund NAVs at quarter-end, model the unobservable bucket separately, bridge the estimated fair-value change through tax and attribution to EPS, and compare the estimate with market expectations before the financial filing.
- **Primary uncertainty:** Prior-quarter holdings may change during the quarter; unlisted assets are not independently markable; tax, non-controlling interests, valuation timing, and market anticipation can eliminate the apparent edge. Historical point-in-time testing is required before the idea can move beyond `Testing`.
- **Recommended lifecycle:** `Idea` → `Testing` → `Validated` → `Monitoring`, with `Rejected` and `Archived` terminal alternatives. Each strategy should have `last_run`, `next_refresh`, `refresh_trigger`, `stale_after`, confidence, falsification criteria, and links to dated screen results.
- **Repository impact:** Recorded the gap and proposed boundary without prematurely implementing a schema or automation contract.
- **Next verification:** Review and approve the naming, lifecycle, and separation between strategy hypotheses and screen definitions before adding directories, schemas, templates, validation, or automation.
