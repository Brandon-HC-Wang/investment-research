## 2026-09-09 — Strategy-research architecture gap identified

- **Scope:** Repository-wide workflow beyond single-company research.
- **Type:** Architecture assessment; no new directory contract established.
- **Finding:** The repository has company, macro-thesis, research-watchlist, model, and portfolio-risk layers, but no first-class lifecycle for reusable strategy hypotheses, screen definitions, point-in-time results, refresh schedules, or historical validation.
- **Proposed separation:** A future `strategies/` layer should store testable economic-edge hypotheses and their validation state; a future `screens/` layer should store reproducible universe, filter, source, cadence, and append-only result snapshots. Screen output may nominate research candidates but must not become an automatic buy list.
- **Initial candidate:** “Quarter-end FVTPL earnings nowcast” — use the latest disclosed holdings as a starting inventory, reprice observable listed securities and fund NAVs at quarter-end, model the unobservable bucket separately, bridge the estimated fair-value change through tax and attribution to EPS, and compare the estimate with market expectations before the financial filing.
- **Primary uncertainty:** Prior-quarter holdings may change during the quarter; unlisted assets are not independently markable; tax, non-controlling interests, valuation timing, and market anticipation can eliminate the apparent edge. Historical point-in-time testing is required before the idea can move beyond `Testing`.
- **Recommended lifecycle:** `Idea` → `Testing` → `Validated` → `Monitoring`, with `Rejected` and `Archived` terminal alternatives. Each strategy should have `last_run`, `next_refresh`, `refresh_trigger`, `stale_after`, confidence, falsification criteria, and links to dated screen results.
- **Repository impact:** Recorded the gap and proposed boundary without prematurely implementing a schema or automation contract.
- **Next verification:** Review and approve the naming, lifecycle, and separation between strategy hypotheses and screen definitions before adding directories, schemas, templates, validation, or automation.

## 2026-09-10 — Forward dividend-yield screen defined

- **Scope:** TWSE and TPEx common stocks that may distribute cash dividends in 2027 from 2026 earnings.
- **Type:** Cross-company discovery methodology; not a recommendation list or a validated strategy.
- **Definition:** Forward cash dividend yield is estimated 2027 cash dividend per share divided by the stock price on the screen date. Exchange-displayed yield is retained only as a discovery input because it reflects a historical distribution and can include non-recurring or non-earnings-funded components.
- **Current universe fact:** On 2026-09-09, the exchange APIs showed 65 TWSE and 76 TPEx records with displayed yield of at least 7%, or 141 in total. This count is not a forward-yield result.
- **Primary screen:** Estimate normalized attributable 2026 diluted EPS, then estimate cash DPS using the lower of a conservatively supported payout ratio and parent-company distributable cash capacity. A base-case candidate requires estimated forward cash yield of at least 7%, normalized-EPS coverage of required DPS of at least 1.25 times, and a downside yield of at least 5% to 5.5%.
- **Quality filters:** Test three- to five-year payout consistency, three-year cash dividend coverage by free cash flow, leverage and interest coverage, share-count dilution, liquidity, and the durability of 2027 revenue and EPS drivers. Separate financial companies, construction/property companies, and cyclical companies because their capital, project-recognition, and normalized-earnings tests differ.
- **Earnings-quality exclusions:** Flag rather than automatically capitalize asset disposals, FVTPL gains, FX gains, tax reversals, capital-surplus distributions, capital reductions, and other one-offs. A displayed yield above 7% with weak normalized EPS or cash coverage belongs in a yield-trap review, not the durable-income candidate set.
- **Evidence:** Taiwan Stock Exchange, “Daily PER, dividend yield and PBR for all listed stocks,” data date 2026-09-09, accessed 2026-09-10, [BWIBBU_ALL](https://openapi.twse.com.tw/v1/exchangeReport/BWIBBU_ALL); Taipei Exchange, “Mainboard stock PER, dividend yield and PBR,” data date 2026-09-09, accessed 2026-09-10, [tpex_mainboard_peratio_analysis](https://www.tpex.org.tw/openapi/v1/tpex_mainboard_peratio_analysis); Taiwan Stock Exchange OpenAPI, accessed 2026-09-10, [listed and dataset company financial, monthly-revenue, and dividend-distribution datasets](https://openapi.twse.com.tw/).
- **Confidence:** High for data availability and the displayed-yield count; medium for the proposed screening thresholds until point-in-time tests show how often the base and downside cases predict the subsequent declared cash dividend.
- **Repository impact:** Recorded a reproducible forward-yield definition and quality gates without creating the still-unapproved `screens/` or `strategies/` contracts.
- **Next verification:** Run the full market screen as an append-only point-in-time snapshot, disclose the EPS and payout assumptions for every retained candidate, and compare the estimates with 2027 board declarations and shareholder approvals.

## 2026-09-14 — Official market-discovery adapter implemented

- **Scope:** Official TWSE and TPEx price and displayed-yield collection for the `forward-dividend-yield` research workflow.
- **Type:** Source-adapter and provenance implementation; not an earnings model, formal source snapshot, screen run, or recommendation.
- **Finding:** The official exchange datasets can form a reproducible all-record market-discovery table when the yield datasets are joined to same-date closing-price datasets by market and ticker. The displayed-yield threshold is retained only as a review-priority label and does not remove securities from the table.
- **Source contracts:** TWSE `BWIBBU_ALL` and `STOCK_DAY_ALL`, and TPEx `tpex_mainboard_peratio_analysis` and `tpex_mainboard_daily_close_quotes`, accessed 2026-09-14. The official responses use ROC dates and different field names, so the adapter normalizes dates, identity, price, displayed yield, DPS where supplied, P/E, and P/B while preserving source URLs and raw JSON.
- **Diagnostic observation:** A non-persisting contract replay of the endpoints' 2026-09-11 responses contained 1,080 TWSE and 885 TPEx yield records. It joined 1,939 of 1,965 records to valid prices, leaving 26 incomplete and 441 labeled for review at a displayed-yield threshold of 5%. This `partial` dry run is a tooling diagnostic, not retained research evidence or a forward-yield candidate count.
- **Controls and limitation:** Live collection is explicit, requires TLS verification, reconciles all four source dates to `--as-of`, preserves raw and normalized SHA-256 checksums, rejects overwrites, and cannot populate normalized EPS or forward DPS. The local TPEx certificate chain was not trusted by the current command-line environment; inspection used a temporary non-persisted diagnostic download, while the production collector does not permit insecure TLS. A trusted CA bundle is required before retaining the first official snapshot.
- **Repository impact:** Added `scripts/collect_market_discovery.py`, synthetic source-contract fixtures and tests, `market-discovery-snapshot.schema.json`, append-only `source-snapshots/` guidance, stronger repository validation, and synchronized bilingual architecture documentation. Screen definition version advanced to 3 and remains due because no formal run exists.
- **Verification:** Thirty-two repository tests pass. Fixture dry runs for source collection and screen execution are non-mutating; repository validation and diff checks pass; `source-snapshots/` contains guidance only and `runs/` remains absent.
- **Next verification:** Resolve strict TPEx certificate validation, execute and validate the first retained official market snapshot, then design the separate filing and analyst-assumption enrichment contract before any formal screen run.
