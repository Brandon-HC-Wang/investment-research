## 2026-08-17 — Q2 handover recovery does not establish sustainable EPS

- **Type:** Primary-filing result and thesis update.
- **Finding:** Q2 property-sale revenue increased to TWD 1.255 billion and parent profit recovered to TWD 193 million, but H1 EPS was TWD 1.06, service revenue fell 44.6%, operating cash flow was negative TWD 2.120 billion, and borrowings excluding leases reached approximately TWD 31.112 billion.
- **Mechanism:** Handovers can release construction gross profit, but inventory-funded development, finance costs, fair-value gains, minorities, and the preferred dividend determine common EPS and cash backing.
- **Confidence:** High for consolidated figures; low for named-project attribution and sustainable earnings.
- **Repository impact:** The current analysis is integrated into [`02_financials.md`](../../companies/2348-hiyes/02_financials.md), [`04_eps_model.md`](../../companies/2348-hiyes/04_eps_model.md), and [`08_thesis.md`](../../companies/2348-hiyes/08_thesis.md); the point-in-time review is preserved in [`history/2026Q2-filing-review.md`](../../companies/2348-hiyes/history/2026Q2-filing-review.md).
