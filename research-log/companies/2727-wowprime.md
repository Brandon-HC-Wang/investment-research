# 2727 Wowprime Research Log

## 2026-08-05 — Multi-brand compounding remains unverified

- **Type:** Initial hypothesis and evidence-gap record.
- **Finding:** Q1 and first-half revenue growth are positive, but available disclosure cannot separate same-store demand from new capacity, calendar effects, pricing, or FX.
- **Mechanism:** If mature-store sales remain positive and new cohorts earn adequate lease-adjusted returns, shared brands, procurement, and customer distribution may support sustainable per-share growth. If not, added stores can raise revenue while weakening margins and cash returns.
- **Confidence:** Low; store cohorts, same-store sales, and active-member economics are missing.
- **Repository impact:** Current analysis is in [`../../companies/2727-wowprime/`](../../companies/2727-wowprime/08_thesis.md), assumptions in [`assumptions.md`](../../companies/2727-wowprime/assumptions.md), and the point-in-time state in [`history/2026Q1.md`](../../companies/2727-wowprime/history/2026Q1.md).
