# Construction Research Log

## 2026-08-04 — Backlog quality separated from backlog quantity

- **Type:** Methodology baseline.
- **Finding:** Construction backlog supports future EPS only after enforceability, ownership, pricing, execution capacity, cost-to-complete, recognition timing, and cash collection are assessed.
- **Mechanism:** Gross contract or project value can shrink through cancellation, partner shares, delays, cost inflation, financing, tax, and margin deterioration before reaching attributable EPS.
- **Confidence:** High as an analytical framework; company-specific application remains evidence dependent.
- **Repository impact:** Canonical framework stored in [`../../industries/construction/`](../../industries/construction/README.md) and [`../../knowledge/backlog.md`](../../knowledge/backlog.md).
- **Next verification:** Compare risk-adjusted backlog with subsequent revenue, margin, and cash collection across covered companies.
