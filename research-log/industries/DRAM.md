# DRAM Research Log

## 2026-08-04 — DRAM cycle requires a full supply-demand bridge

- **Type:** Methodology baseline.
- **Finding:** Price direction alone does not establish sustainable DRAM earnings; the cycle must reconcile bit demand, capacity and process productivity, inventory, ASP, utilization, mix, and cost per bit.
- **Mechanism:** Supply-demand balance affects price, while price, utilization, depreciation, mix, and cost determine gross margin and EPS.
- **Confidence:** High as an analytical framework; the current cycle stage remains open.
- **Repository impact:** Canonical concept stored in [`../../knowledge/dram_cycle.md`](../../knowledge/dram_cycle.md), with industry application in [`../../industries/semiconductor/`](../../industries/semiconductor/README.md) and macro framing in [`../../theses/dram_cycle.md`](../../theses/dram_cycle.md).
- **Next verification:** Establish a common dated evidence set for supplier inventory, customer inventory, bit shipments, ASP, utilization, and capital expenditure.
