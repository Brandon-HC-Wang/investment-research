# Research Watchlist

The watchlist prioritizes research actions and monitoring triggers. It is not a portfolio, recommendation list, or ranking of expected returns.

The canonical machine-readable queue is [`watchlist.yaml`](watchlist.yaml). Add a company only after creating its company directory. Each entry must link to that directory and contain a specific research reason, next action, trigger, priority, and review date.

## Priority policy

- `1`: new evidence could materially change the thesis or close a major uncertainty.
- `2`: important work for the current research cycle.
- `3`: normal monitoring or model maintenance.
- `4`: wait for a defined trigger.
- `5`: intentionally deferred or archived.

Priority indicates research urgency, not conviction or attractiveness. Remove an entry only by appending the reason to the company timeline or research log first.
